# 00 Core Intelligence Dataset – EPI Trimbakeshwar EMRS

This directory houses the primary official bid documentation and technical benchmark charters for the **Construction of Eklavya Model Residential School (EMRS) in Single-Phase at Trimbakeshwar, Nashik District, Maharashtra**, executed by **Engineering Projects (India) Limited (EPI)** on behalf of the **National Education Society for Tribal Students (NESTS)**, Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India.

## 📁 Catalog of Core Documents

### 1. Official Primary Bid Packages (Downloaded from Govt. Servers)
- **`1709981684_NIT334Rev.pdf`** (~26.8 MB, Official Master Bid Package):
  - Complete Volume I Tender Document issued by EPI Western Regional Office Mumbai.
  - Contains Notice Inviting Tender (NIT), Scope of Work, Technical Eligibility Criteria, Financial Turnover & Solvency requirements, Instructions to Bidders (ITB), General Conditions of Contract (GCC), Special Conditions of Contract (SCC), Integrity Pact, Bid Evaluation Criteria, and Proforma Forms.
- **`1705477492_TenderNotice.pdf`** (~644 KB, Official Reference Notice):
  - Official EMRS Scheme Benchmark Tender Notice outlining technical mandates, statutory guidelines, and state tribal development coordination terms.

### 2. Core Benchmark Engineering Charters (PDF 1.4 Vector Editions)
- **`01_Master_Project_Charter_and_Scope.pdf`**:
  - Executive charter summarizing statutory approvals, campus layout guidelines, administrative hierarchy, site coordinates at Trimbakeshwar, and 18-month execution milestones.
- **`02_Campus_Master_Plan_and_Building_Breakdown.pdf`**:
  - Spatial allocation charter detailing School Academic Block (G+2), Boys Hostel (240 capacity), Girls Hostel (240 capacity), Dining & Modern Commercial Kitchen (480 seats), Staff Quarters Type-III (32 units), Type-II (10 units), Principal Residence, Warden Quarters, and Sports ground.
- **`03_Contractor_Eligibility_and_Prequalification.pdf`**:
  - Clause 2.0 NIT eligibility criteria matrix (3 works of ₹14.47 Cr / 2 works of ₹18.09 Cr / 1 work of ₹28.94 Cr), ₹18.09 Cr annual turnover, ₹14.47 Cr bank solvency, CA certified net worth, and digital submission mandates.
- **`04_CPWD_DSR_Technical_Specifications_Standard.pdf`**:
  - CPWD Specifications 2019/2021 standards, DSR 2021 item specifications, M-25/M-30 RCC design mix, Fe-500D TMT reinforcement steel, anti-termite treatment, crystalline waterproofing, and mandatory testing frequencies.
- **`05_BOQ_Percentage_Rate_Cost_Analysis.pdf`**:
  - Price schedule breakdown of the ₹36.17 Crore tender sum, percentage excess/less bidding protocol, billing milestones, 5% retention money, and 3% Performance Bank Guarantee (PBG).
- **`06_MEP_STP_Infrastructure_Engineering.pdf`**:
  - Mechanical, electrical, and plumbing engineering specifications: 11kV substation, 315/500 kVA transformer, DG backup, 60 KLD MBBR/SBR Sewage Treatment Plant, 100% water recycling, rainwater harvesting, and solar PV.
- **`07_General_Special_Conditions_of_Contract_GCC.pdf`**:
  - Legal and contractual governance: EPI GCC (Works) Rev 2023, milestone-based penalties (LD at 0.5%/week up to 10%), labor welfare cess (1% BOCW), third-party quality auditing, and arbitration mechanisms.
