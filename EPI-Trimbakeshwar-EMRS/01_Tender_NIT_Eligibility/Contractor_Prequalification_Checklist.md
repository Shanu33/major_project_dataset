## ⚠️ Critical Failure Points Leading to Disqualification
1. Submission of EMD / Tender fee in wrong account or for an amount lower than specified.
2. Experience certificates from private individuals, private builders, or unverified subcontractors (only Central/State Govt. & PSUs accepted).
3. Failure to provide CA UDIN numbers on turnover or net worth certificates.
4. Any conditional price bid or commercial reservations entered in Envelope-I or Envelope-II.
