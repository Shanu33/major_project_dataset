## 2. Contractor Eligibility Criteria (Clause 2.0 NIT)

To qualify for award of this contract, bidders must meet all technical, financial, and statutory parameters:

### 2.1 Technical Experience (Past 7 Financial Years)
The bidder must have successfully completed works of similar nature during the last 7 (seven) years ending the last day of the month previous to the one in which applications are invited:
- **Option A**: **Three (3)** similar completed works each costing not less than **₹14.47 Crore** (40% of estimated cost); **OR**
- **Option B**: **Two (2)** similar completed works each costing not less than **₹18.09 Crore** (50% of estimated cost); **OR**
- **Option C**: **One (1)** similar completed work costing not less than **₹28.94 Crore** (80% of estimated cost).

> **Definition of Similar Work**: Execution and completion of institutional campus, residential educational complex, multi-storey residential quarters, university/school buildings, or composite commercial/hospital complexes comprising RCC framed structures including internal/external electrification, plumbing, sanitary, and allied development works executed for Central/State Govt. departments, Central/State PSUs, or autonomous bodies.

### 2.2 Financial Capabilities
1. **Average Annual Financial Turnover**:
   - The average annual financial turnover of the bidder during the immediate last three consecutive financial years (FY 2020-21, 2021-22, 2022-23) should be at least **₹18.09 Crore** (50% of estimated cost).
   - Must be supported by audited balance sheets and profit & loss accounts bearing valid Unique Document Identification Numbers (UDIN) issued by a practicing Chartered Accountant.
2. **Net Worth**:
   - The net worth of the bidder as on 31st March of the immediate preceding financial year must be **positive**.
3. **Bank Solvency Certificate**:
   - The bidder must submit a valid solvency certificate of minimum **₹14.47 Crore** (40% of estimated cost) issued by a Nationalized or Scheduled Commercial Bank not older than six months prior to the bid closing date.

## 3. Mandatory Compliances & Undertakings

1. **Integrity Pact (IP)**:
   - Mandatory submission of duly signed Integrity Pact on plain paper as per EPI format. Bids submitted without an executed Integrity Pact shall be summarily rejected.
   - Project oversight is conducted by EPI's Independent External Monitors (IEMs).
2. **Site Visit Undertaking**:
   - The bidder must inspect the site at Trimbakeshwar, Nashik, study the Western Ghats topographical terrain, access roads, monsoon rain patterns, water sources, and power availability before submitting the tender.
3. **No Deviation Certificate**:
   - Unconditional acceptance of all terms, conditions, specifications, and contractual clauses without any reservations or caveats.
4. **GST & Statutory Registrations**:
   - Valid GST registration certificate in Maharashtra (or undertaking to obtain GST registration in Maharashtra within 30 days of LOA), EPFO, ESIC, and PAN card.
