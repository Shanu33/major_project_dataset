## 3. Independent External Monitor (IEM) Oversight

In compliance with Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) directives, EPI has appointed Independent External Monitors (IEMs) to oversee the procurement integrity, transparent evaluation, and dispute handling for this tender:

- **Role of IEMs**:
  - Independent review of complaints from bidders regarding exclusion, procedural anomalies, or biased evaluation.
  - Review of the execution of the mandatory Integrity Pact signed by EPI and the contractor.
  - IEMs do not have administrative authority but submit binding recommendations directly to the Chairman & Managing Director (CMD), EPI.
- **Submission of Representation to IEM**:
  - Any bidder seeking redressal may submit representations to the appointed IEMs as designated in the master NIT package ([1709981684_NIT334Rev.pdf](../00_Core_Intelligence_Dataset/1709981684_NIT334Rev.pdf)) with a copy marked to the CVO, EPI Corporate Office, New Delhi.
