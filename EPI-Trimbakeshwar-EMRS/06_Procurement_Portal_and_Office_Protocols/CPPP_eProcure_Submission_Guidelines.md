# CPPP eProcure Submission Guidelines – Trimbakeshwar EMRS

**Central Procurement Portal**: Government of India Central Public Procurement Portal (CPPP)  
**Portal URL**: [https://etenders.gov.in/eprocure/app](https://etenders.gov.in/eprocure/app)  
**EPI Official Web Portal**: [https://epi.gov.in](https://epi.gov.in)  
**Tender Reference Number**: `WRO/CON/EMRS/872/334`

---

## 1. Digital Signature & Portal Enrollment Requirements

1. **Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)**:
   - Bidders must possess a valid **Class-III Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)** with both Signing and Encryption capabilities issued by any Certifying Authority (e.g., eMudhra, (n)Code Solutions, Safescrypt, Capricorn).
2. **CPPP Portal Registration**:
   - The bidder must enroll on `etenders.gov.in` using an active corporate email address and map their Class-III DSC to the user profile.
3. **Browser & Java Runtime Configuration**:
   - Chrome / Edge (Latest versions) with 64-bit Java Runtime Environment (JRE 8 Update 161 or higher) configured for PKI signing applets.

## 2. Two-Envelope Online Submission Protocol

The tender is invited under a **Single Stage Two Envelope Digital Bidding System**:

```
                                [ETENDERS.GOV.IN PORTAL]
                                           │
                  ┌────────────────────────┴────────────────────────┐
                  ▼                                                 ▼
        [ENVELOPE-I: TECHNO-COMMERCIAL]                   [ENVELOPE-II: FINANCIAL PRICE BID]
  - Tender Fee (₹29,500) Proof                      - Standard BoQ (.xls) downloaded from portal
  - EMD (₹46,17,451) BG / RTGS Receipt              - Single % rate entered (Excess / Less / Par)
  - Eligibility Certificates (Clause 2.0)           - Encrypted via portal public key
  - CA Audited Turnover (₹18.09 Cr) + UDIN          - Opened ONLY for technically qualified bidders
  - Bank Solvency (₹14.47 Cr)
  - Signed Integrity Pact (IEM monitored)
  - Technical Staff & Plant Deployments
```

### Critical Submission Steps:
1. **Fee & EMD Verification**: Upload scanned copy of RTGS / NEFT payment receipt or original Bank Guarantee scanned copy. Original physical Bank Guarantee must reach the EPI WRO Mumbai office before the scheduled bid submission deadline.
2. **Document Sizing & Formats**: Ensure individual PDF uploads do not exceed 10 MB per slot. Combine related certificates into compact, indexed multi-page PDFs.
3. **Price Bid Encryption**: Download the protected macro-enabled `BoQ_xxxxxx.xls` file. Enter company name and percentage quote. Do not modify or rename columns. Upload back to the portal and digitally sign.
4. **Bid Acknowledgment**: Save and archive the system-generated Bid Submission Acknowledgment Slip containing the unique Bid ID and submission timestamp.
