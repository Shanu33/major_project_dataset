# Civil & Structural Materials Specifications – EPI Trimbakeshwar EMRS

**Standard Specification Compliance**: CPWD Specifications 2019 / 2021 (Volumes 1 & 2)  
**Schedule of Rates**: CPWD Delhi Schedule of Rates (DSR) 2021 with up-to-date Correction Slips  
**Applicable Indian Standards**: IS 456:2000 (Plain and Reinforced Concrete), IS 1786 (TMT Bars), IS 1893 (Seismic Design), IS 875 (Wind Loads)

---

## 1. Structural & Concrete Specifications

### 1.1 Structural System
- **Framed Structure**: Special Moment Resisting Frames (SMRF) RCC framed structure designed to resist combined dead, live, wind, and seismic forces in accordance with IS 1893 (Part 1): 2016 (Seismic Zone III parameters).
- **Substructure**: Isolated column footings / combined strip footings founded on dense weathered basalt rock layer. Safe Bearing Capacity (SBC) confirmed at minimum 250 kN/sq.m at foundation depth.
- **Plinth & Damp-Proofing**: RCC plinth beams with heavy-duty chemical damp-proof course (DPC) 50mm thick M-20 concrete with integral waterproofing compound (Impermo / Fosroc / Pidilite).

### 1.2 Concrete Grades & Mix Design
- **Lean Concrete (PCC)**: Nominal mix 1:4:8 (1 cement : 4 coarse sand : 8 graded stone aggregate 40mm nominal size) under footings and ground flooring.
- **Reinforced Concrete (RCC)**:
  - Footings, plinth beams, columns: **M-25 / M-30 Grade** Design Mix Concrete as per IS 10262 and IS 456.
  - Slabs, beams, lintels, chajjas, stairs: **M-25 Grade** Design Mix Concrete.
  - Water retaining structures (UG Sump, OHT, STP tanks): **M-30 Grade** with water-reducing and crystalline waterproofing admixtures.
- **Cement**: 43 Grade / 53 Grade Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC) conforming to IS 269 / IS 12269 or Portland Pozzolana Cement (PPC) conforming to IS 1489 (Part 1). Approved brands: UltraTech, ACC, Ambuja, Shree.

### 1.3 Reinforcement Steel (TMT)
- **Grade**: High yield strength deformed Thermo-Mechanically Treated (TMT) bars conforming to **IS 1786: Grade Fe-500D** (elongation ≥ 16% for enhanced ductility in seismic design).
- **Approved Primary Producers**: Steel Authority of India Limited (SAIL), Tata Steel (Tiscon), Jindal Steel & Power Limited (JSPL), JSW Steel. Secondary re-rolled steel is strictly prohibited.

## 2. Masonry & Waterproofing Specifications

### 2.1 Masonry Works
- **External Walls**: 230mm thick fly ash / autoclaved aerated concrete (AAC) blocks (density 550-650 kg/cu.m) or first-class burnt clay bricks conforming to IS 1077 in cement mortar 1:6 (1 cement : 6 coarse sand).
- **Internal Partition Walls**: 115mm thick half-brick masonry in cement mortar 1:4 with 2 Nos. 6mm MS bars embedded every third course for structural bonding.

### 2.2 Waterproofing Systems
- **Basement & Substructure**: Two coats of polymer-modified bituminous membrane or crystalline waterproofing slurry applied on concrete face.
- **Sunken Toilets & Bathrooms**: Brickbat coba / crystalline slurry coating with integral acrylic waterproof polymer, finished with cement plaster 1:3 mixed with water-proofing compound, turned up 300mm on walls.
- **Roof Terraces**: Brickbat coba grading laid to 1:100 slope with China mosaic / reflective ceramic tile finish to ensure 100% water tightness and thermal insulation (reducing indoor temperature by 3-5°C).
