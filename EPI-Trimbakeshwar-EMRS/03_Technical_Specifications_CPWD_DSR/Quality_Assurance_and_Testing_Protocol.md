# Quality Assurance & Field Testing Protocol – EPI Trimbakeshwar EMRS

**Executing Agency**: Engineering Projects (India) Limited (EPI)  
**Tender Reference**: `WRO/CON/EMRS/872/334`  
**Quality Manual**: CPWD Quality Assurance Manual & Mandatory Field Testing Frequency

---

## 1. On-Site Quality Control Laboratory Setup

Prior to commencement of any RCC or masonry work, the contractor must establish a fully equipped, climate-controlled testing laboratory at the Trimbakeshwar site staffed by a qualified Senior Materials / QA/QC Engineer:

### Required Laboratory Equipment
1. **Compression Testing Machine (CTM)**: 2000 kN capacity, electrically operated with digital load readout and valid calibration certificate.
2. **Standard Sieve Sets**: IS sieves for coarse aggregates (40mm, 20mm, 10mm, 4.75mm) and fine aggregate sieves (2.36mm down to 75 microns) with mechanical shaker.
3. **Slump Test Apparatus**: 4 sets of slump cones, tamping rods, and base plates conforming to IS 7320.
4. **Concrete Cube Moulds**: Minimum 72 sets of 150mm x 150mm x 150mm cast iron moulds with base plates.
5. **Curing Tanks**: Thermostatically controlled concrete cube curing tanks (capacity minimum 200 cubes).
6. **Core Cutting Machine**: Diamond bit core drill for non-destructive / semi-destructive RCC inspection.
7. **Rebound Hammer & Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV)**: Calibrated digital testing instruments for in-situ non-destructive concrete strength assessment.
8. **Moisture Meter & Electronic Balances**: Accuracy 0.1g and 1.0g for moisture determination and silt content tests.

## 2. Mandatory Field Testing Frequency Schedule

| Material | Test Description | Testing Standard | Mandatory Frequency | Acceptance Threshold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cement** | Fineness, Initial/Final Setting Time, Soundness, Compressive Strength | IS 4031 | 1 test per consignment / 50 MT | Setting time > 30 min / < 600 min; 28-day strength ≥ 43/53 MPa |
| **Coarse Aggregate** | Sieve analysis, Flakiness & Elongation index, Impact value | IS 2386 | 1 test per 45 cu.m or 1 test per source consignment | Combined flakiness < 30%; Impact value < 30% |
| **Sand / Fine Aggregate**| Silt content, Bulking, Sieve analysis (Zone II/III) | IS 2386 | Silt content daily / Sieve analysis 1 per 20 cu.m | Silt content ≤ 5% by volume; Fineness modulus 2.2 to 3.2 |
| **Reinforcement Steel**| Tensile strength, Proof stress, Elongation, Bend/Re-bend | IS 1786 | 1 sample per 10 MT of each bar diameter from each heat | Fe-500D: Yield ≥ 500 MPa, Tensile ≥ 565 MPa, Elongation ≥ 16% |
| **Fresh Concrete** | Workability (Slump Test) | IS 1199 | Every transit mixer / batch at point of discharge | 75mm to 125mm depending on placement location |
| **Hardened Concrete** | Compressive Strength (7-day & 28-day) | IS 516 | 1 set of 6 cubes for 1-5 cu.m; 2 sets for 6-15 cu.m; 3 sets for 16-30 cu.m; 4 sets for 31-50 cu.m + 1 set per addl 50 cu.m | ≥ 70% of characteristic strength at 7 days; ≥ 100% at 28 days |
| **Bricks / AAC Blocks**| Compressive strength, Water absorption, Efflorescence | IS 3495 | 1 test per 50,000 bricks / 20 cu.m blocks | Burnt clay: Strength ≥ 3.5-7.5 N/mm²; Absorption ≤ 20% |
| **Waterproofing** | Ponding Test (Toilets & Terraces) | CPWD Specs | 100% of wet areas ponded with 50mm water for 72 hours | Zero dampness or seepage on underside of slab |

## 3. Third-Party Laboratory Testing Protocol
- In addition to on-site testing, 10% of all critical samples (reinforcement bars, concrete cubes, cement, admixtures) must be dispatched to NABL-accredited independent government laboratories (e.g., VJTI Mumbai, IIT Bombay, Government Engineering College Pune, or NCCBM) for independent verification.
- Testing expenses shall be borne entirely by the contractor as per GCC Clause 31.
