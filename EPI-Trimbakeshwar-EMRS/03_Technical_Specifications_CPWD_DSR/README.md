# 03 Technical Specifications & CPWD DSR Standards – EPI Trimbakeshwar EMRS

This directory provides the technical specifications, Indian Standard (IS) code compliances, material selection schedules, and Quality Assurance / Quality Control (QA/QC) field testing protocols governing the construction of Trimbakeshwar EMRS campus under Tender No. `WRO/CON/EMRS/872/334`.

---

## 📑 Included Documents
1. [Civil_Structural_Materials_Specifications.md](Civil_Structural_Materials_Specifications.md):
   - Comprehensive engineering specifications covering M-25/M-30 RCC, IS 1786 Fe-500D TMT reinforcement, AAC blocks/burnt clay bricks, integral crystalline waterproofing, and architectural joinery/finishing palettes.
2. [Quality_Assurance_and_Testing_Protocol.md](Quality_Assurance_and_Testing_Protocol.md):
   - Mandatory on-site quality control laboratory requirements, apparatus inventory, 8-tier field testing frequency schedules, and NABL third-party laboratory verification protocols.

---

## 🏛️ Specification Benchmarks
- **Governing Standard**: CPWD Specifications 2019 / 2021 (Volumes 1 & 2)
- **Schedule Base**: CPWD Delhi Schedule of Rates (DSR) 2021
- **Structural Integrity**: IS 456:2000 & IS 1893:2016 (Seismic Zone III)
- **Primary Rebar Brands**: SAIL, TATA Steel, JSW Steel, JSPL
- **Core Benchmark Charter**: [04_CPWD_DSR_Technical_Specifications_Standard.pdf](../00_Core_Intelligence_Dataset/04_CPWD_DSR_Technical_Specifications_Standard.pdf)
