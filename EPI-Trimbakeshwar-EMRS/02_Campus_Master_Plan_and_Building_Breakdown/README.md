# 02 Campus Master Plan & Building Breakdown – EPI Trimbakeshwar EMRS

This directory provides the comprehensive architectural layout, spatial program, building schedules, and zoning guidelines for the Eklavya Model Residential School campus at Trimbakeshwar, Nashik.

---

## 📑 Included Documents
1. [EMRS_Campus_Master_Spatial_Plan.md](EMRS_Campus_Master_Spatial_Plan.md):
   - Spatial zoning diagram, 4-zone master layout, topographical adaptations, and building footprint schedule (~13,210 sq.m BUA total).
2. [Residential_Hostel_and_Quarters_Schedule.md](Residential_Hostel_and_Quarters_Schedule.md):
   - Detailed breakdown of student accommodation (Boys Hostel 240, Girls Hostel 240) and residential quarters (32 Type-III, 10 Type-II, Principal bungalow, Warden suites).
