# Residential Hostel & Staff Quarters Schedule – Trimbakeshwar EMRS

**Client**: National Education Society for Tribal Students (NESTS)  
**Executing Agency**: Engineering Projects (India) Limited (EPI)  
**Campus Occupancy**: 480 Resident Students (240 Boys + 240 Girls) + 45 Resident Staff & Faculty

---

## 1. Student Hostels Architectural Program

### 1.1 Boys Hostel Block (240 Capacity)
- **Configuration**: Ground + 2 Floors (G+2) RCC Framed Structure.
- **Dormitories**:
  - 24 large dormitories designed for 8 to 10 students each (providing minimum 4.5 sq.m floor area per student).
  - Built-in study tables, cupboards, ceiling fans, and LED study fixtures.
- **Sanitary & Ablution Blocks**:
  - Ratio of 1 WC / Urinal for every 6 students; 1 Shower / Bath for every 8 students.
  - Solar hot water piped distribution to all bathroom modules.
- **Ancillary Spaces**:
  - Warden Office & Consultation Room on Ground Floor.
  - Study / Library Reading Hall on First Floor.
  - Indoor Games & TV Recreation Hall on Second Floor.
  - Linen store, luggage room, laundry drying yard, and janitor rooms on each floor.

### 1.2 Girls Hostel Block (240 Capacity)
- **Configuration**: Ground + 2 Floors (G+2) RCC Framed Structure.
- **Security & Enclosure**:
  - High perimeter wall with biometric access control and 24x7 female security guard cabin.
  - CCTV surveillance covering entrance lobby, corridors, and perimeter grounds.
- **Dormitories & Facilities**:
  - Identical 24 large dormitories (8-10 students each) with built-in storage.
  - Attached Matron Quarters and Visiting Parents Lounge.
  - First-aid clinic / infirmary with 4 recovery beds and attached nursing station.
  - Dedicated sanitary incinerators and hygiene disposal facilities in each ablution block.

## 3. Finishes and Internal Fixtures Norms
- **Flooring**: 600x600 mm double-charged vitrified tiles in living/dining/bedrooms; anti-skid ceramic tiles (300x300 mm) in toilets and balconies.
- **Doors & Windows**: Flush doors with hard-wood frames and laminate finish; aluminum sliding windows with mosquito mesh and MS safety grills.
- **Kitchen Counters**: Polished black granite counter slabs with stainless steel single-bowl sink and ceramic tile dado up to 600 mm height.
- **Sanitary Fittings**: CP fittings (Jaquar / Hindware / Parryware equivalent), dual-flush cisterns, and water-saving aerator faucets.
