# EMRS Campus Master Spatial Plan – Trimbakeshwar

**Client**: National Education Society for Tribal Students (NESTS)  
**Executing Agency**: Engineering Projects (India) Limited (EPI)  
**Project**: Construction of Eklavya Model Residential School (EMRS) at Trimbakeshwar, Nashik District, Maharashtra  
**Normative Guideline**: Ministry of Tribal Affairs (MoTA) Normative Space Standards for 480-Student Residential Campuses

---

## 1. Campus Spatial Zoning & Topography

The Trimbakeshwar campus is situated in the scenic Western Ghats foothills of Nashik District. The architectural layout divides the campus into four distinct, functional zones designed for security, smooth pedestrian circulation, and maximum daylight/ventilation:

## 3. Site Development & Landscaping Elements
- **Compound Wall**: 2.1m high brick/RCC boundary wall with 0.6m concertina coil fencing along the perimeter.
- **Internal Roads**: Heavy-duty 6.0m and 7.5m wide concrete/bituminous campus arterial roads with storm drainage channels.
- **Pedestrian Network**: 2.0m wide non-skid precast concrete paver pathways connecting all educational and residential blocks.
- **Sports Complex**: 200-meter 4-lane athletic track, multi-purpose outdoor sports ground (Football/Cricket), basketball court with synthetic/acrylic surfacing, and volleyball courts.
