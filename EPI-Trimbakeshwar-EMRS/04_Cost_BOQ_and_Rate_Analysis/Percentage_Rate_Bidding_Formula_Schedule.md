# Percentage Rate Bidding Formula & Financial Terms – Trimbakeshwar EMRS

**Executing Agency**: Engineering Projects (India) Limited (EPI WRO Mumbai)  
**Tender Reference**: `WRO/CON/EMRS/872/334`  
**Contract Pricing Mechanism**: Percentage Rate Tender over Estimated Schedule of Quantities

---

## 1. Percentage Rate Bidding Mechanics

In accordance with the Central Public Works Department (CPWD) percentage rate contracting procedure adopted by EPI:
- The tender estimated cost of **₹36,17,45,100/-** is published in the online Financial BoQ template (`BoQ_xxxxxx.xls`) on `etenders.gov.in`.
- Bidders quote a **single percentage** that applies uniformly across all items:
  1. **Percentage Excess (Above)**: e.g., `+ 4.25%` over estimated rates
  2. **Percentage Less (Below)**: e.g., `- 3.50%` below estimated rates
  3. **At Par**: `0.00%` equal to estimated rates
- The quoted percentage is automatically factored into every running account (RA) bill throughout the project execution.

## 3. Financial Guarantees & Security Deposits

1. **Performance Bank Guarantee (PBG)**:
   - Within 15 days of issue of Letter of Award (LOA), the successful bidder must furnish an unconditional PBG of **3% (or 5%)** of the accepted contract value from a Scheduled Commercial Bank valid up to the completion period plus 60 days.
2. **Security Deposit / Retention Money (SD)**:
   - A deduction of **5%** shall be made from each gross Running Account (RA) bill until the full security deposit is accumulated.
   - 50% of the Security Deposit is released upon issue of the formal Completion Certificate.
   - The remaining 50% is retained until the successful completion of the **36-month Defect Liability Period (DLP)**.
3. **Mobilization Advance**:
   - Contractor may request Mobilization Advance up to **10%** of the contract value in two equal installments against an irrevocable Bank Guarantee for 110% of the advance amount, bearing interest as per EPI GCC rules, recoverable pro-rata from RA bills.
4. **Labor Cess**:
   - **1.0%** Building and Other Construction Workers (BOCW) welfare cess is mandatorily deducted at source from each bill and deposited with the Maharashtra BOCW Board.
