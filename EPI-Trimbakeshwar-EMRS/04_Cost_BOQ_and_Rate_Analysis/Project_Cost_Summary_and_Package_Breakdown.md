## 2. Detailed Work Head Sub-Breakdowns

### 2.1 Civil & Structural Superstructure (₹24.80 Crore)
- **Earthwork & Foundation**: Site clearance, excavation in hard basalt rock, backfilling, anti-termite treatment, PCC 1:4:8 base: **₹ 2.65 Cr**
- **RCC Superstructure**: Design mix M-25 / M-30 concrete in columns, beams, lintels, slabs, and stairs: **₹ 11.20 Cr**
- **Reinforcement Steel**: High yield Fe-500D TMT reinforcement (approx. 1,450 Metric Tonnes) including bending and tying: **₹ 6.10 Cr**
- **Masonry & Plastering**: AAC block masonry in CM 1:6, internal 12mm plaster, external 20mm waterproof plaster: **₹ 2.45 Cr**
- **Finishing & Joinery**: Vitrified tiles, granite counters, aluminum windows, flush doors, and internal/external painting: **₹ 2.40 Cr**

### 2.2 Internal & External Electrical Works (₹4.52 Crore)
- **11kV Substation & Transformers**: 315/500 kVA step-down transformer, HT metering panel, VCB, earthing pits: **₹ 1.35 Cr**
- **DG Set Backup System**: 125/160 kVA acoustic enclosed diesel generator with AMF auto-switch panel: **₹ 0.45 Cr**
- **Internal Point Wiring & Lighting**: FRLS copper wiring, modular switches, LED energy-saving fixtures: **₹ 1.85 Cr**
- **External Street & Campus Lighting**: Underground 4-core XLPE cable distribution, 35W/50W LED octagonal street lighting poles: **₹ 0.52 Cr**
- **Rooftop Solar PV (Grid-tied)**: 50 kWp solar PV system with grid inverters: **₹ 0.35 Cr**

### 2.3 Public Health Engineering & Sanitary Installations (₹2.81 Crore)
- **Sanitary Fixtures & CP Brass Fittings**: Ceramic water closets, urinals, wash basins, faucets, shower heads: **₹ 0.75 Cr**
- **Internal Water Lines**: CPVC / composite piping networks for hot and cold water distribution: **₹ 0.65 Cr**
- **Soil, Waste & Vent Stacks**: UPVC SWR pipe systems (110mm and 75mm) conforming to IS 13592: **₹ 0.55 Cr**
- **Underground & Overhead Water Reservoirs**: 150,000 L RCC sump, 50,000 L rooftop distribution tanks, hydro-pneumatic booster pumps: **₹ 0.86 Cr**

### 2.4 Campus Infrastructure & Allied Works (₹2.48 Crore)
- **Perimeter Boundary Wall**: 2.1m high brick/RCC wall (approx. 1,800 running meters) with concertina razor fencing: **₹ 1.15 Cr**
- **Campus Arterial Roads**: 6.0m & 7.5m bituminous / concrete roads with storm water box drains: **₹ 0.88 Cr**
- **Main Entrance Portal & Security Cabin**: Structural architectural gate, boom barriers, security guard post: **₹ 0.20 Cr**
- **Sports Fields & Landscaping**: 200m running track, basketball court, turfing, ornamental plantations: **₹ 0.25 Cr**

### 2.5 STP, Rainwater Harvesting & Environmental Protection (₹0.99 Crore)
- **60 KLD Sewage Treatment Plant**: Moving Bed Biofilm Reactor (MBBR) plant with tertiary filtration (Dual media filter, Activated Carbon filter, UV/Chlorination): **₹ 0.65 Cr**
- **Rainwater Harvesting Recharge Wells**: 6 deep percolation borewells with gravel filters, desilting chambers, and storm collection trenches: **₹ 0.34 Cr**

### 2.6 Fire Fighting & Renewable Systems (₹0.57 Crore)
- **Fire Protection Wet Riser System**: 150mm MS heavy-duty risers, internal landing valves, fire hose cabinets, yard hydrants, main fire pump (2280 LPM), and jockey pump: **₹ 0.37 Cr**
- **Solar Water Heating System**: 5,000 Litres Per Day (LPD) centralized solar evacuated tube collector system for student hostels: **₹ 0.20 Cr**
