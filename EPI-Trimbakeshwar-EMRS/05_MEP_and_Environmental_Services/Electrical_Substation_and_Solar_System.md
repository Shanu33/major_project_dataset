# Electrical Substation, Power Distribution & Solar PV System – Trimbakeshwar EMRS

**Executing Agency**: Engineering Projects (India) Limited (EPI WRO Mumbai)  
**Standard Compliance**: Indian Electricity Rules 1956 / Central Electricity Authority (CEA) Regulations, National Electrical Code (NEC), and MSEDCL norms  
**Campus Power Authority**: Maharashtra State Electricity Distribution Company Limited (MSEDCL)

---

## 1. High Tension (HT) Substation & Power Infrastructure

### 1.1 Incomer & Substation Parameters
- **Primary Grid Supply**: 11 kV, 3-phase, 50 Hz overhead supply tapped from the nearest MSEDCL 33/11 kV rural distribution substation.
- **Transformer**:
  - 1 No. **315 kVA / 500 kVA**, 11kV / 433V, Dyn11 vector group, oil-immersed, naturally cooled (ONAN), outdoor step-down transformer conforming to IS 2026 / IS 1180 (Level 2 energy efficiency).
  - Equipped with off-circuit tap changer (+5% to -10% in steps of 2.5%), silica gel breather, oil level gauge, and Buchholz relay.
- **HT Switchgear**:
  - 11 kV Indoor Vacuum Circuit Breaker (VCB) panel with 25 kA fault breaking capacity for 3 seconds, microprocessor-based numerical overcurrent and earth-fault protection relays.
- **Main Low Tension (LT) Panel**:
  - Free-standing, floor-mounted, compartmentalized dust and vermin-proof switchboard (IP-54) with 800A 4-pole Air Circuit Breaker (ACB) incomer and outgoing MCCBs feeding various campus load centers.
  - Automatic Power Factor Correction (APFC) panel of 100 kVAR capacity with heavy-duty capacitor banks to maintain power factor ≥ 0.98 lag.

### 1.2 Standby Emergency Power (DG Set)
- **Diesel Generator**: 1 No. **125 kVA / 160 kVA** silent type diesel generator set with acoustic enclosure (noise level ≤ 75 dB(A) at 1 meter distance) compliant with CPCB-IV+ emission norms.
- **AMF Panel**: Auto Mains Failure (AMF) control panel that automatically starts the DG set and transfers essential loads (water pumps, hostel corridors, cold storage, security lighting) within 15 seconds of utility grid failure.

---

## 2. Internal Electrification & Renewable Energy

### 2.1 Wiring & Distribution
- **Conduiting**: Concealed heavy-duty rigid PVC conduits (minimum 2.0mm wall thickness) conforming to IS 9537 Part 3.
- **Wiring**: Multi-strand flame retardant low smoke (FRLS) copper conductors conforming to IS 694. Approved brands: Polycab, Havells, Finolex, RR Kabel.
- **Fixtures**: 100% LED energy-efficient lighting fixtures:
  - Classrooms: 36W LED batten / surface panel fixtures delivering 300 lux average illumination.
  - Labs & Library: 36W recessed / suspended LED panels delivering 400 lux illumination.
  - Hostels: 18W LED fixtures with 28W BLDC energy-saving ceiling fans (5-star rated).

### 2.2 Campus Solar PV & Hot Water Generation
- **Grid-Interactive Solar PV**:
  - **50 kWp** rooftop crystalline solar photovoltaic plant installed over the Academic Block roof.
  - Grid-tied string inverters with net-metering synchronization to MSEDCL grid, expected to offset ~35% of campus daytime electrical consumption.
- **Solar Water Heating System**:
  - **5,000 Litres Per Day (LPD)** total capacity evacuated tube collector (ETC) solar hot water heating systems installed on Boys and Girls hostel terraces with insulated PUF piping to bathroom showers.
