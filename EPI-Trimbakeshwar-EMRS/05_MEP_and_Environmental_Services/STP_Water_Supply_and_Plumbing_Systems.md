# Sewage Treatment Plant (STP), Water Supply & Environmental Systems – Trimbakeshwar EMRS

**Executing Agency**: Engineering Projects (India) Limited (EPI WRO Mumbai)  
**Client**: National Education Society for Tribal Students (NESTS)  
**Environmental Standard**: Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) & Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB) Guidelines for Educational Campuses

---

## 1. Campus Water Supply & Distribution Network

### 1.1 Water Demand Calculations
- **Per Capita Water Norms**:
  - Resident Students & Hostel Staff: 135 Litres per Capita per Day (LPCD) as per IS 1172.
  - Day Scholars & Administrative Visitors: 45 LPCD.
- **Total Gross Daily Fresh Water Demand**: Approx. **72,000 Litres/Day (72 KLD)**.
- **Flushing & Horticulture Demand**: Approx. **28,000 Litres/Day** (met entirely via treated recycled water from the STP).

### 1.2 Storage Reservoirs & Pumping
- **Underground Raw / Domestic Water Sump (UG Sump)**:
  - RCC M-30 water tank of **150,000 Litres (150 KL)** capacity, bifurcated into domestic water compartment and dedicated fire fighting reserve (100,000 L).
- **Overhead Tanks (OHT)**:
  - Rooftop sectional tanks (totaling 50,000 Litres) installed on Academic Block, Boys Hostel, Girls Hostel, and Staff Quarters.
- **Pumping Machinery**:
  - Dual hydro-pneumatic booster pump sets (1 Working + 1 Standby) with Variable Frequency Drives (VFD) for continuous pressure distribution.

## 2. Decentralized Sewage Treatment Plant (60 KLD STP)

### 2.1 Process Technology: Moving Bed Biofilm Reactor (MBBR)
The Trimbakeshwar campus features an eco-friendly, compact 60 KLD MBBR-based sewage treatment plant designed for zero liquid discharge (ZLD) into local water bodies:

```
[Raw Sewage Inflow] 
       │
       ▼
 [Bar Screen Chamber] ──▶ [Oil & Grease Trap (Kitchen Waste)]
       │
       ▼
 [Equalization Tank] (Air Diffusers for Homogenization)
       │
       ▼
 [MBBR Bio-Reactor 1 & 2] (High Surface Area Virgin Polypropylene Carriers)
       │
       ▼
 [Tube Settler / Clarifier Tank] ──▶ [Sludge Holding Tank & Drying Beds]
       │
       ▼
 [Chlorine Contact / Ozone Disinfection Tank]
       │
       ▼
 [Dual Media Sand Filter (DMF)] ──▶ [Activated Carbon Filter (ACF)]
       │
       ▼
 [Treated Water Sump (50 KL)]
       ├─────────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────┐
       ▼                                 ▼                                 ▼
[Toilet Flushing Distribution]   [Campus Horticulture Irrigation]   [Sports Field Dust Suppression]
```

| **Chemical Oxygen Demand (COD)**| 450 – 600 mg/L | **< 50 mg/L** | < 50 mg/L |

## 3. Rainwater Harvesting & Stormwater Drainage
- **Catchment Area**: Over 12,000 sq.m of building rooftops and paved campus networks.
- **Recharge Pits**: **6 Nos.** specialized deep rainwater harvesting borewells (150mm diameter drilled up to 45m depth into fissured basalt strata) preceded by 3-stage filtration chambers (graded gravel, sand, charcoal).
- **Stormwater Network**: RCC rectangular covered drains alongside campus perimeter and roads to prevent erosion during heavy Western Ghats monsoon downpours.
