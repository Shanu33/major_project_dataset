# 05 MEP & Environmental Services – EPI Trimbakeshwar EMRS

This directory contains the engineering specifications, design parameters, schematic process flows, and discharge standards for the mechanical, electrical, plumbing (MEP), renewable energy, and environmental treatment infrastructure of the Trimbakeshwar EMRS project.

---

## 📑 Included Documents
1. [Electrical_Substation_and_Solar_System.md](Electrical_Substation_and_Solar_System.md):
   - 11kV substation, 315/500 kVA transformer, 125/160 kVA DG set standby, FRLS internal copper wiring, 50 kWp rooftop grid-tied solar PV, and 5,000 LPD solar water heating systems.
2. [STP_Water_Supply_and_Plumbing_Systems.md](STP_Water_Supply_and_Plumbing_Systems.md):
   - 60 KLD MBBR-based Sewage Treatment Plant flow schematic, dual-plumbing recycled water distribution, CPCB/MPCB treated water standards, 150 KL UG sump, and 6 deep rainwater harvesting recharge borewells.

---

## ⚙️ Key Technical Baselines
- **Power Sourcing**: MSEDCL 11kV Feeder + 315/500 kVA Substation
- **Standby Power**: 125/160 kVA CPCB-IV+ Acoustic DG Set
- **Solar Energy**: 50 kWp Solar PV + 5,000 LPD Solar Hot Water
- **STP Technology**: 60 KLD Moving Bed Biofilm Reactor (MBBR) with ZLD
- **Rainwater Harvesting**: 6 Deep Basalt Aquifer Recharge Borewells
