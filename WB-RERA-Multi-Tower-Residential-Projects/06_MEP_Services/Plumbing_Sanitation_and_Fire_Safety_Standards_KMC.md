# Plumbing, Sanitation & Fire Safety Standards (KMC & West Bengal Fire Services)

## 1. Water Supply & Plumbing Infrastructure

- **Source:** Municipal bulk connection (KMC / KMDA / AMC) supplemented by deep tubewells with water treatment plant (iron removal filters).
- **Per Capita Water Requirement:** 135 LPCD designed per NBC 2016 guidelines.
- **Storage Tank Capacity:**
  - Underground Reservoir (UGR): 1.5 days domestic requirement + dedicated 100,000 to 200,000 liter fire reserve compartment.
  - Overhead Tank (OHT): 0.5 days domestic requirement + dedicated 20,000 to 50,000 liter fire header tank.
- **Pumping Scheme:** Hydro-pneumatic variable frequency drive (VFD) pressure booster pumps for upper floors; gravity down-feed for lower floors.

---

## 2. West Bengal Fire & Emergency Services (WBFES) Compliance

Governed by the **West Bengal Fire Services Act, 1950** and **NBC 2016 (Part 4)**:
1. **Fire Tower & Refuge Provision:**
   - Mandatory for buildings exceeding 24 m height.
   - Dedicated fire refuge floor located at the 8th level (~24 m from ground) with minimum 15 m² clear area and direct access to fire escape stairs.
2. **Wet Riser & Sprinkler Installation:**
   - Wet riser of minimum 150 mm internal diameter with twin landing valves at every floor level and hose reel drum with 30 m hose.
   - Automatic sprinkler coverage throughout basements, parking areas, and residential corridors.
3. **Fire Pumps:**
   - Main Electric Fire Pump (,850	ext{ LPM} @ 7.0	ext{ kg/cm}^2$).
   - Standby Diesel Fire Pump (,850	ext{ LPM} @ 7.0	ext{ kg/cm}^2$).
   - Electric Jockey Pump (	ext{ LPM} @ 7.5	ext{ kg/cm}^2$) for line pressurization.
