# WBRERA Statutory Framework, Municipal Sanctions & Registration Protocols

## 1. Statutory Registration Mandate

Under Section 3 and Section 4 of Central RERA (read with West Bengal Real Estate Rules, 2021):
- Promoters must register every real estate development where land area exceeds 500 square meters or where the number of apartments exceeds 8 units.
- **Section 4(2)(l)(D) 70% Escrow Rule:** 70% of collections from homebuyers must be deposited in a scheduled bank account to be utilized exclusively for land purchase and construction.
- **Withdrawal Certification:** Strict three-tier certification required for withdrawals:
  1. Certificate from Project Architect (percentage of physical work executed)
  2. Certificate from Structural Engineer (structural integrity and code compliance)
  3. Certificate from Practicing Chartered Accountant (audited cost incurred vs funds withdrawn)

---

## 2. Municipal Sanctioning Authorities in West Bengal

Before WBRERA registration can be granted, developers must obtain formal Sanction Letters and approved Building Plans from local urban authorities:
1. **KMC (Kolkata Municipal Corporation):** Administering building permits under the Kolkata Municipal Corporation Act, 1980 and KMC Building Rules, 2009.
2. **NKDA (New Town Kolkata Development Authority):** Governing Action Areas 1, 2, and 3 in Rajarhat-New Town under specialized planned smart-city building bylaws.
3. **KMDA (Kolkata Metropolitan Development Authority):** Regional planning authority overseeing master land-use zoning and infrastructural integration across the greater metropolitan area.
4. **AMC (Asansol Municipal Corporation) / DMC (Durgapur Municipal Corporation):** Governing urban industrial growth centers in Paschim Bardhaman.

---

## 3. Mandatory Statutory Uploads on WBRERA Portal

The WBRERA portal requires promoters to upload verified legal, financial, and technical documents:
- **Sanction Letter / Sanctioned Plan:** Formal clearance showing sanctioned FAR, number of floors, height, and setbacks.
- **Title Search Report & Non-Encumbrance Certificate:** Comprehensive 30-year search by legal counsel confirming clean land ownership.
- **Joint Development Agreement (JDA) & Power of Attorney (PoA):** Establishing legal right of promoter to develop, construct, and market units on landowner property.
- **Architectural Plans & Structural Drawings:** Site master plan, floor plans, elevations, and structural drawings certified by empanelled LBS (Licensed Building Surveyor) / ESE (Empanelled Structural Engineer).
