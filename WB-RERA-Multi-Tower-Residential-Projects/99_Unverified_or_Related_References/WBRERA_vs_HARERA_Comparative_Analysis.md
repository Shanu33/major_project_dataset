# WBRERA vs HARERA — Comparative Regulatory & Dataset Analysis

## 1. The Complementary Nature of State RERA Disclosures

Within the national construction intelligence benchmarking hub, **Haryana RERA** and **West Bengal RERA** present contrasting yet complementary data disclosure profiles:

| Feature / Data Dimension | Haryana RERA (HARERA) | West Bengal RERA (WBRERA) | Synergy in AI Construction Modeling |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Financial Disclosures (Form A-H)** | ✅ **Exhaustive:** Line-item breakdown of Land, Apartment Construction, Infrastructure, and Statutory costs. | ⚠️ **Embedded:** Declared in project profile and promoter audited balance sheets; no standalone Form A-H table. | HARERA provides exact macro-cost ratios (cost/m² of carpet, infra allocation %). |
| **Sanctioned Architectural Plans** | ❌ **Withheld:** Building plans, site layouts, and floor plans not publicly accessible. | ✅ **Publicly Downloadable:** Sanctioned ground floor, typical floor, elevation, and refuge plans publicly available. | WBRERA supplies precise geometric floor plates, core ratios, and elevation profiles. |
| **Structural Drawings** | ❌ **Withheld:** Structural drawings not in public domain. | ⚠️ **Available for Some Projects:** Foundation layouts, pile details, and structural sheets downloadable for key schemes. | WBRERA validates structural framing, pile depths, and seismic detailing for private high-rises. |
| **Quarterly Progress Reports (QPR)** | ✅ **Structured:** Web-based quarterly percentage progress submissions. | ⚠️ **Status Tracking:** Periodic updates and municipal CC certificates. | Cross-validates North vs East India high-rise construction cycle times. |

---

## 2. Final Dataset Classification: **Reference only (Borderline SILVER)**

- **Classification:** **Reference only**
- **Borderline SILVER Justification:** While lacking public contractor BOQs (standard for private real estate), the presence of **downloadable sanctioned architectural plans, typical floor plates, and structural drawings** places WBRERA multi-tower projects ahead of standard reference datasets, providing direct geometric inputs for architectural and structural takeoff models.
