# Project Lifecycle Status, Monitoring & Handover Charter

## 1. Lifecycle Tracking on WBRERA Portal

Projects registered on the WBRERA portal transition across standardized lifecycle states:
1. **Under Construction:** Active execution phase where promoters submit quarterly progress updates and updated financial disclosures.
2. **Completed:** Physical construction concluded, municipal Completion Certificate (CC) obtained from KMC/NKDA/AMC, and uploaded to the portal.
3. **Occupancy Certificate (OC) / Possession:** Statutory clearance permitting physical handover to allottees.

---

## 2. Completion Documents & Handover Protocol

- **Architect Completion Certificate:** Form certified by empanelled Architect confirming construction conformity with approved municipal building plans.
- **Structural Stability Certificate:** Issued by Structural Engineer confirming structural performance under dead, live, wind, and earthquake loads.
- **Fire Safety Certificate:** Final Fire NOC issued by West Bengal Fire & Emergency Services following live pressure test of hydrants and sprinkler pumps.
- **Conveyance & Association Formation:** Execution of registered sale deed and formation of Apartment Owners Association under the **West Bengal Apartment Ownership Act, 1972**.
