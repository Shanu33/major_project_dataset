# Structural & Civil Finishes Specifications Standards (West Bengal Context)

## 1. Structural Materials & Specifications

- **Cement Grade:** 43-Grade / 53-Grade Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC) conforming to IS 269 or Portland Pozzolana Cement (PPC) conforming to IS 1489 (Part 1) for foundations and masonry.
- **Concrete Mix Design:**
  - Bored cast-in-situ piles: Minimum M30 / M35 grade with controlled slump (150 mm to 180 mm for tremie placement).
  - Raft & Pile caps: Minimum M30 grade with water-cement ratio $\le 0.45$.
  - Columns and shear walls: M35 grade (lower floors) reducing to M30 / M25 (upper floors).
  - Beams and slabs: M25 grade concrete.
- **Steel Reinforcement:** High-yield strength deformed TMT bars of grade **Fe-500D** conforming to IS 1786 (minimum elongation .5\%$ for enhanced seismic ductility in Zone III).

---

## 2. Geotechnical & Foundation Specifics in Bengal Delta

- **Soil Stratigraphy:** The lower Gangetic delta (Kolkata, Howrah, North 24 Parganas) consists of soft to medium alluvial silty clay underlain by sand layers at depths of 15 m to 40 m.
- **Foundation Selection:**
  - High-Rise Towers (G+10 to G+15): Bored cast-in-situ RCC piles (diameter 500 mm to 750 mm, depth 20 m to 32 m) terminated in dense silty sand layers ( > 30$).
  - Low-Rise / Commercial (G+4): Driven precast piles or shallow under-reamed / strip raft foundations where competent strata exist at shallow depths.
- **Waterproofing:** High ground water table necessitates crystalline waterproofing admixtures in substructure concrete and APP-modified bitumen membranes on basement/retaining walls.

---

## 3. Architectural Joinery & Interior Finishes

- **Masonry:** High-quality traditional red clay bricks (1st class kiln-burnt, crushing strength $\ge 105	ext{ kg/cm}^2$) or Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) blocks (density  - 650	ext{ kg/m}^3$).
- **Flooring:**
  - Living / Dining: 800×800 mm or 600×600 mm vitrified tiles (Somany, Kajaria, Nitco).
  - Bedrooms: Vitrified tiles or laminated wooden flooring in master bedrooms.
  - Kitchen & Toilets: Anti-skid ceramic tiles; glazed ceramic dado tiles up to 2.1 m height.
- **Doors & Windows:** Seasoned Salwood / Teakwood frame with flush door shutters; anodized or powder-coated aluminum sliding windows with 5 mm float glass.
- **Sanitary & Plumbing:** CPVC / UPVC concealed piping (Astral / Supreme); white vitreous sanitaryware (Hindware / Parryware / Jaquar) with chrome-plated brass fittings.
