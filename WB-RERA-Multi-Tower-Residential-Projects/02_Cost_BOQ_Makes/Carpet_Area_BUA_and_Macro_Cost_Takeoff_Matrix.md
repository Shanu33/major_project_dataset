## 3. Takeoff Modeling Rationale

While line-item contractor BOQs are not published under RERA, the availability of **Sanctioned Typical Floor Plans, Elevation Profiles, and Structural Pile Schedules** allows direct takeoff of:
1. **Concrete Volume (^3$):** Calculated directly from slab area, floor height (typically 3.0 m floor-to-floor), beam sections, and pile cap schedules.
2. **Steel Reinforcement ($):** Calculated using standard structural density ratios:
   - Residential high-rise superstructure (Zone III):  - 90	ext{ kg/m}^3$ of concrete.
   - Deep bored pile foundation:  - 110	ext{ kg/m}^3$ of concrete.
3. **Masonry & Plaster (^2$):** Calculated from exterior perimeter and interior partition layouts.
