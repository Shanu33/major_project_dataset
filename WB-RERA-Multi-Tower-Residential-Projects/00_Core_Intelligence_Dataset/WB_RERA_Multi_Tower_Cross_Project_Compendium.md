# WB RERA Multi-Tower Residential Projects — Cross-Project Compendium & Intelligence Synthesis

## 1. Executive Summary & Regulatory Authority

The **West Bengal Real Estate Regulatory Authority (WBRERA)** operates under the **Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (Central Act 16 of 2016)** and the **West Bengal Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Rules, 2021**. 

Historically, West Bengal enacted the *West Bengal Housing Industry Regulation Act, 2017 (WB-HIRA)*, which was subsequently struck down by the Supreme Court of India in May 2021 (*Forum for People's Collective Efforts v. State of West Bengal*), restoring the primacy of Central RERA. Projects originally registered under HIRA transitioned into WBRERA, maintaining dual registration tracking (e.g., HIRA/P/... and WBRERA/P/...).

### Unique Dataset Characteristics in National Benchmark Repository
Across state RERA portals, West Bengal RERA possesses a distinctive feature:
- **Public Disclosure of Sanctioned Drawings & Structural Plans:** Unlike Haryana RERA (which discloses detailed financial Form A-H statements but keeps architectural/structural CAD drawings confidential), the WBRERA portal makes **sanctioned building plans, floor plates, elevation drawings, and structural drawings publicly downloadable** for multiple private residential projects.
- **Classification:** **Reference only (Borderline SILVER)**. While private sector projects do not publish public competitive contractor BOQs, the presence of sanctioned municipal architectural layouts, typical floor plans, and structural foundation sheets provides an authentic spatial and structural foundation for quantity takeoff modeling.

## 3. Spatial & Spatial-Density Parameters (West Bengal Urban Centers)

Empirical data extracted across WBRERA project filings establishes key spatial ratios:

1. **Floor Area Ratio (FAR) & Ground Coverage:**
   - Governing Codes: **Kolkata Municipal Corporation (KMC) Building Rules, 2009**, **West Bengal Municipal (Building) Rules, 2007**, and **NKDA (New Town Kolkata Development Authority) Rules**.
   - Permissible FAR: Base FAR ranges from **1.75 to 2.50**, expandable up to **2.75 to 3.25** through purchasable FAR or green building incentives.
   - Ground Coverage: Typically capped at **40% to 50%** for mid-rise blocks (G+4) and **35% to 45%** for high-rise towers (G+12 to G+15), ensuring mandatory open spaces for fire access and landscaping.
2. **Carpet Area Efficiency:**
   - RERA Carpet Area to Built-Up Area (BUA) efficiency in West Bengal towers ranges between **78% and 84%**.
   - Carpet Area to Super Built-Up Area (SBUA) efficiency averages **70% to 75%**, reflecting common circulation, lift lobbies, and municipal refuge corridors.
3. **Fire Refuge Floors:**
   - Under NBC 2016 and West Bengal Fire Services Act, towers exceeding 24 m in height mandate dedicated refuge terraces or refuge balconies every 7 floors above 24 m (e.g. Tarang Towers 6 & 7 have dedicated 8th-floor refuge provisions separating the 1st-7th and 9th-12th typical floor plates).
