# TKD Structural Drawing Series & Foundation Engineering Details

## 1. The TKD Drawing Designation Standard

In West Bengal engineering and municipal sanction filings, the **TKD (Technical Knowledge Drawing / Tender-to-Construction Drawing)** series represents the canonical structural and civil drawing breakdown:

| Drawing Sheet Code | Drawing Title | Engineering Focus & Content |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TKD-SD-00** | General Structural Notes | Seismic Zone III parameters, concrete grades, rebar lap lengths, clear cover requirements, soil bearing capacities. |
| **TKD-SD-10** | Foundation & Pile Layout | Bored cast-in-situ pile locations, pile diameters (500/600 mm), pile cut-off levels, pile cap reinforcement. |
| **TKD-SD-15** | Plinth & Tie Beam Layout | Plinth tie beams, grade slab reinforcement (100 mm slab with 8mm @ 200 c/c mesh), damp-proof course (DPC). |
| **TKD-SD-20** | Column Schedule & Shear Core | Column cross-sections, rebar schedules, stirrup confinement detailing per IS 13920, lift core shear wall layout. |
| **TKD-SD-25** | Floor Beam & Slab Framing | 1st to Typical Floor framing plans, continuous beam spans, one-way/two-way slab reinforcement, sunken slab details. |
| **TKD-SD-30** | Refuge Floor Structural Detailing | Reinforced cantilever refuge slab, fire-rated parapet structural anchors, heavy loading provisions. |
| **TKD-SD-35** | Staircase Structural Details | Waist slab thickness, main reinforcement, landing beam support detailing, shear key connections. |
| **TKD-SD-40** | Roof Tank & Machine Room | Overhead water tank (domestic + fire reserve compartments), lift hoist beam structural calculations, roof waterproofing slope. |

## 2. Seismic Zone III Design Parameters (West Bengal Urban Agglomerations)

Kolkata, Howrah, North 24 Parganas, and Paschim Bardhaman are situated in **Seismic Zone III**:
- **Zone Factor ($):** .16$ (per IS 1893 Part 1: 2016).
- **Importance Factor ($):** .20$ for residential multi-tower projects.
- **Response Reduction Factor ($):** .0$ for ductile RC moment resisting frames (SMRF) detailed per IS 13920:2016.
- **Design Wind Speed ($):** 	ext{ m/s}$ (180 km/h) per IS 875 (Part 3) due to cyclone-prone Bay of Bengal maritime weather influence.
