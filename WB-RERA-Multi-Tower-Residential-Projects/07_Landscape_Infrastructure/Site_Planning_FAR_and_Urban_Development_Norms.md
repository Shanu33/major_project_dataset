# Site Planning, FAR & Urban Development Norms (West Bengal Municipal Rules)

## 1. Urban Planning & Setback Rules

Under the **KMC Building Rules 2009** and **West Bengal Municipal (Building) Rules 2007**:
- **Front Open Space:** Minimum 6.0 m to 10.0 m depending on road width and building height.
- **Rear Open Space:** Minimum /3$ or 6.0 m (whichever is greater) for adequate ventilation and fire tender circulation.
- **Side Open Space:** Minimum 4.0 m to 6.0 m on each side; peripheral continuous motorable paved driveway of minimum 6.0 m clear width for fire appliances.

---

## 2. Infrastructure & External Services

- **Internal Roads & Pavements:** 7.0 m to 9.0 m wide internal carriage ways finished with interlocking heavy-duty concrete paver blocks (80 mm thickness) or bituminous macadam.
- **Surface Drainage:** Precast concrete covered drains along both sides of internal roads connected to municipal storm water outfalls.
- **Tree Plantation Norms:** Statutory requirement to plant minimum 1 tree per 80 m² of plot area; retention of existing mature trees where feasible.
- **Solid Waste Management:** Segregated waste collection bins (biodegradable vs recyclable) with compactor space.
