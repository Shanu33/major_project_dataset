## 2. Floor Plate Efficiency Analysis (Tarang Towers 6 & 7 Example)

- **Tower Footprint:** Approximately 450 m² to 550 m² per tower floor plate.
- **Core-to-Carpet Area Distribution:**
  - Vertical Core (2 High-Speed Passenger Lifts + 1 Fire Escape Staircase + 1 Smoke-Stop Lobby + Utility Shafts): ~75 m² per floor (14% - 16% of gross floor plate).
  - Residential Units (4 to 6 flats per floor): ~380 m² to 450 m² per floor (82% - 84% of gross floor plate).
  - Efficient central core design minimizes corridor lengths, maximizing carpet area delivered to allottees.

## 3. Elevation & Facade Treatment

- **External Envelope:** Weather-resistant exterior acrylic emulsion paint with textured finish coats (Asian Paints Apex Ultima / Berger WeatherCoat).
- **Chajjas & Projections:** Reinforced concrete horizontal weather shades (chajjas) projected 450 mm to 600 mm over window openings to protect against torrential monsoon downpours typical of coastal/deltaic West Bengal.
