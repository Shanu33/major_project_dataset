# Structural Design Basis & Engineering Notes

## 1. Structural Scheme
- **Superstructure:** Cast-in-situ Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC) Moment Resisting Frames conforming to IS 456:2000.
- **Storey Heights:**
  - Type-II Multi-Unit Blocks: Ground + 2 Upper Floors (G+2), floor-to-floor height 3.30 m.
  - Single-Storied Quarters: Ground Floor (G+0), clear ceiling height 3.20 m.
- **Walling:** 250 mm external brick masonry in cement mortar 1:6; 125 mm internal partition walls in cement mortar 1:4 with 2 nos 6 mm dia MS bars every third layer.

---

## 2. Design Loads & Seismic Criteria
- **Dead Loads (IS 875 Part 1):** RCC self-weight (25 kN/m3), brick masonry (19 kN/m3), floor finish (1.5 kN/m2).
- **Imposed Loads (IS 875 Part 2):** Rooms (2.0 kN/m2), corridors & stairs (3.0 kN/m2), roof (1.5 kN/m2).
- **Seismic Criteria (IS 1893:2016):**
  - Seismic Zone: Zone III (Dhanbad / Koderma region)
  - Zone Factor (Z): 0.16
  - Importance Factor (I): 1.2
  - Response Reduction Factor (R): 5.0 (SMRF ductile detailing per IS 13920:2016).
- **Wind Criteria (IS 875 Part 3):** Basic wind speed Vb = 47 m/s; Terrain Category 2.

---

## 3. Contractor Responsibilities for GFC Drawings
Under Special Conditions of Contract (SCC):
- The contractor is responsible for structural analysis, detailed RCC scheduling, and preparing Good-For-Construction (GFC) drawings.
- Structural proof-checking must be conducted through an approved Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) or National Institute of Technology (NIT) prior to construction.
