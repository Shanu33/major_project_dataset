# Project Executive Summary & Core Intelligence Extraction

- **Master Tender Reference:** `KKK-EN-QTR-DHN-I-PH-I`
- **Balance Work Tender Reference:** `KKK-EN-QTR-DHN-I-PH-I-R`
- **Tender Inviting Authority:** Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Limited (DFCCIL) — Kolkata Unit (under Ministry of Railways, Government of India)
- **Principal End User:** East Central Railway (ECR), Dhanbad Division
- **Project Section:** Sonnagar – Gomoh Section (Phase-I), Grand Chord Route, State of Jharkhand

## 4. Housing Typologies & Spatial Parameters
- **Type-II Quarters (2BHK):** Plinth area ~50-55 m2 (540-590 sq.ft.). Two bedrooms, living room, kitchen, independent bath and WC, front verandah.
- **Type-III Quarters (3BHK):** Plinth area ~65-70 m2 (700-750 sq.ft.). Three bedrooms, living/dining hall, kitchen, pantry, attached toilet and common toilet, rear balcony.
- **Type-IV Quarters (4BHK):** Plinth area ~85-95 m2 (915-1,020 sq.ft.). Senior railway officers housing with larger drawing room, master bedroom with en-suite bath, visitor toilet, spacious kitchen, and porch.
