# MEP Engineering Specifications & Deep Boring Charter

## 1. Electrical System (Schedule H Scope)
Extracted from Pages 3 to 6 of Advertised NIT:
- **Point Wiring:** Concealed PVC conduit wiring using FRLS 1.5 mm2 single-core copper conductors (Havells/Anchor/Finolex/Polycab).
- **Power Circuits:** 4.0 mm2 copper conductor circuit wiring for geysers, ACs, and power plugs.
- **Main Busbar Chambers:** 4-way 100A busbar chamber (Havells/C&S/L&T) with brass glands and cable terminations.
- **Service Overhead Connection:** 14-foot long 2-inch dia GI pipe (Tata/Jindal) service brackets with shackle insulators and clamp bolts.
- **Exhaust & Air Movement:** Heavy-duty metal body exhaust fans (216 units across all quarters).

## 2. Deep Boring & Water Supply (Schedules F & G Scope)
Because station staff quarters in rural Jharkhand lack municipal piped water supply:
- **Deep Tube Well Boring:** Rotary rig drilling through soil, boulder, and hard granitic rock strata down to potable aquifers (100-150 m depth).
- **Casing Pipes:** Heavy-duty ERW GI casing pipes through overburden strata to prevent collapse.
- **Submersible Pumps:** Multi-stage stainless steel submersible pumpsets with automatic water level controllers.
- **Manual Hand Pumps:** Installation of deep-well Indian Mark II hand pumps as failsafe manual backup during grid power failure.
