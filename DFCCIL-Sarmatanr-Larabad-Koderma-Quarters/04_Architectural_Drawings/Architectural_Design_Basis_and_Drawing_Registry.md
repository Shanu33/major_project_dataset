## 2. Railway Board Standard Typology Specifications
The quarters follow standardized Indian Railways / Railway Board architectural designs:

### A. Type-II Staff Quarters (2BHK)
- **Target Cadre:** Group C operational staff (Track maintainers, pointsmen, station masters, technicians)
- **Plinth Area:** 50.00 - 55.00 m2 (538 - 592 sq.ft.)
- **Building Formats:**
  - Format 1: Three-storied block (G+2 Floors) accommodating 6 dwelling units per block with central common staircase.
  - Format 2: Single-storied semi-detached block (2 units per block).
  - Format 3: Single-storied independent unit.
- **Spatial Arrangement:** Living room (3.5 m * 3.0 m), Master bedroom (3.5 m * 3.2 m), Kitchen with cooking platform, bathroom, separate water closet, front verandah.

### B. Type-III Staff Quarters (3BHK)
- **Target Cadre:** Senior supervisors, Section Engineers, Station Superintendents
- **Plinth Area:** 65.00 - 70.00 m2 (700 - 753 sq.ft.)
- **Format:** Single-storied independent or twin bungalow.
- **Spatial Arrangement:** Living room, dining space, two main bedrooms, kitchen, store, independent bath and WC, front and rear verandahs.

### C. Type-IV Staff Quarters (4BHK)
- **Target Cadre:** Assistant Divisional Engineers, Gazetted Officers, Branch In-charges
- **Plinth Area:** 85.00 - 95.00 m2 (915 - 1,022 sq.ft.)
- **Format:** Single-storied spacious bungalow with portico.
- **Spatial Arrangement:** Drawing room, dining hall, master bedroom with en-suite bath, guest bedroom, kitchen, pantry, store room, front portico/porch, lawn setback.
