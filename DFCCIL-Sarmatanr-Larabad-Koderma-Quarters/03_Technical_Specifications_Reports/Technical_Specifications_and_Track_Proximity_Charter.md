# Technical Specifications & Track Proximity Safety Charter

## 1. Governing Code Hierarchy
- **Indian Railways Unified Standard Specifications (IRUSS 2010 / 2021)**
- **Indian Railway Concrete Bridge Code & IS 456:2000**
- **Indian Railways Permanent Way Manual (IRPWM)**
- **Indian Railways Schedule of Dimensions (IRSOD) 2004 (Revised)**
- **CPWD Specifications 2019 (Volumes I & II)** for non-railway specific finishes

---

## 2. Concrete & Foundation Engineering
- **Concrete Mixes:**
  - RCC Members: Design Mix M25 / M20 concrete with minimum cement content per IRC/IS codes.
  - Lean Concrete: M10 / PCC 1:4:8 for leveling beds and foundation trench bottoms.
- **Foundation Schemes:**
  - Sarmatanr & Larabad: Isolated and combined RCC spread footings on compacted bearing strata.
  - Koderma Junction & Embankment Margins: Bored cast in-situ RCC piles (Part II Chapter III) where weak strata or railway embankment surcharge exists.
- **Reinforcement:** Fe-500/Fe-500D TMT bars with minimum 0.20% proof stress test certificates from primary producers.

---

## 3. Precautions for Working Near Running Railway Tracks (Part III Chapter V)
Because construction occurs in active railway station yards along the high-density Grand Chord route:
1. **Safety Clearance:** No material, machinery, or scaffolding permitted within 3.5 meters of running track centerline without sanctioned railway traffic block.
2. **Barricading:** Continuous barricading using GI sheets or wire mesh along the railway boundary to prevent personnel or construction vehicles from infringing moving train envelopes.
3. **Safety Watchmen:** Deployment of trained railway-certified lookouts/watchmen equipped with red hand-flags and sirens during working hours.
4. **Excavation Shoring:** Sheet piling or robust timber shoring mandatory for all foundation trenches adjacent to railway tracks to prevent subgrade slope failure.
