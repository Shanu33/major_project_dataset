## 3. Minimum Eligibility & Technical Criteria
1. **Technical Experience:** The tenderer must have successfully completed or substantially completed any of the following during the last 7 years ending the last day of the month previous to the bid opening:
   - Three similar works each costing not less than 30% of advertised value (Rs. 5.24 Cr each), OR
   - Two similar works each costing not less than 40% of advertised value (Rs. 6.99 Cr each), OR
   - One similar work costing not less than 60% of advertised value (Rs. 10.48 Cr).
   *Definition of Similar Work:* Construction of residential/institutional multi-storied or residential quarters with RCC framed structure.
2. **Financial Turnover:** Average annual contractual turnover over the previous 3 financial years must be at least 150% of the advertised value of the tender.
3. **Bid Capacity:** Available Bid Capacity = (A * N * 2) - B must exceed the advertised tender value.
