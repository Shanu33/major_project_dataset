# Site Development & External Infrastructure Schedule

## 1. Site Clearing & Ground Preparation (Schedule F Scope)
- **Demolition & Debris Disposal:** Mechanical demolition of obsolete structures using JCB excavators (200 hours provisioned).
- **ROW Demarcation Pillars:** Pre-cast M20 RCC boundary demarcation pillars (150 mm * 150 mm * 1225 mm) installed at 50-meter intervals along DFC right-of-way.

---

## 2. Approach Roads & Pathways
- **Station Approach Roads:** Water Bound Macadam (WBM) base with Premix Carpet (PMC) or Rigid Concrete Pavement connecting station platforms to staff colony.
- **Pathways:** Paved concrete / paver block pedestrian pathways between quarter blocks.

---

## 3. Perimeter Security & Drainage
- **Boundary Fencing:** Brick boundary wall or precast RCC post and wire mesh fencing around colony periphery.
- **Surface Drainage:** Pucca brick masonry drains with cement plaster connecting roof down-take pipes to station outfall drains.
