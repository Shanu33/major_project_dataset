### 2. Quality Assurance & Precast Lifecycle Supervision
* **Quality Assurance Framework**: Stringent precast quality checks covering aggregate grading, cement tests, steel tensile testing, concrete cube compressive strength tests, and non-destructive ultrasonic pulse velocity (UPV) tests.
* **Factory & Yard Audits**: Periodic inspection of casting moulds, steam curing temperature cycles, lifting anchor embedded parts, and joint grout flowability tests.
* **Third-Party Inspection**: Independent technical review and site auditing by IITH Civil Engineering faculty and external quality assurance agencies.
