# Electrical, Mechanical, Plumbing & Fire Life Safety Schedule
## IIT Hyderabad Precast Housing Package (Volume 04 Extract)

---

### 1. Electrical & Power Distribution Systems
* **Campus Power Supply**: 11 kV underground ring main distribution fed from the IIT Hyderabad 33/11 kV main receiving substation.
* **Step-Down Transformers**: Compact packaged outdoor/indoor dry-type transformers (11 kV / 415 V) with OLTC/OCTC.
* **Standby Power Backup**: Centralized Diesel Generator (DG) synchronization system providing 100% emergency backup power for all essential loads (elevators, fire pumps, water supply, corridor lighting, and partial residential circuits).
* **Internal Electrification**: Concealed FRLS copper multi-strand wiring in rigid heavy-duty conduits cast directly into precast panels during factory manufacture.

---

### 2. Public Health Engineering (PHE) & Water Infrastructure
* **Dual Plumbing Network**:
  * Potable Water Line: Sourced from the campus central water treatment plant delivering chlorinated potable water to kitchens and bathrooms.
  * Recycled Water Line: Sourced from the campus Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) for toilet cistern flushing and landscaped green irrigation.
* **Underground & Overhead Storage**:
  * Combined Under Ground Reservoir (UGR) with dedicated compartments for domestic, flushing, and static fire reserves.
  * Reinforced concrete / GRP sectional overhead tanks on each tower roof with automated hydro-pneumatic booster pumping.
* **Solar Water Heating**: Rooftop centralized solar thermal collector arrays with electric backup integration supplying 24x7 hot water to hostel and apartment bathrooms.

---

### 3. Fire Life Safety & Disaster Preparedness
* **Compliance**: Conforming to **NBC 2016 Part 4** (Life Safety) and Telangana State Disaster Response & Fire Services Department guidelines.
* **Active Fire Fighting**:
  * External yard hydrant network with 63mm landing valves and delivery hoses around all 8 blocks.
  * Internal wet riser systems installed in pressurized escape staircase landings with first-aid hose reels.
  * Automatic fire sprinkler network covering basements, stilt parking, electrical rooms, and common corridors.
* **Fire Detection & Alarm**:
  * Addressable analogue fire alarm system with optical smoke detectors and thermal heat sensors.
  * Manual Call Points (MCP) and electronic sounder-strobes on every floor landing connected to the central campus security control room.
