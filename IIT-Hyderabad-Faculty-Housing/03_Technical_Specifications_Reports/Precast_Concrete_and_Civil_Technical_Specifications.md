# Precast Concrete & Civil Technical Specifications
## IIT Hyderabad Precast Housing Package (Volume 03 Extract)

---

### 1. Precast Concrete Manufacturing & Erection Standards
* **Design Standards**: IS 15916:2010 (Code of practice for building design and erection using prefabricated concrete), IS 456:2000, IS 13920:2016.
* **Concrete Grades**:
  * Foundations & Substructure: M35 / M40 grade design mix.
  * Precast Vertical Elements (Walls, Columns): M40 / M50 high-performance concrete with low water-cement ratio and superplasticizers.
  * Precast Horizontal Elements (Beams, Slabs): M35 / M40 grade prestressed / reinforced concrete.
* **Curing & Moulding**:
  * Steel-faced precision moulds with tolerance within $\pm 2$ mm.
  * Controlled steam curing / thermal curing to achieve 70% design compressive strength within 16-24 hours for rapid mould turnaround.
* **Jointing & Grouting Systems**:
  * Vertical rebar continuity achieved using factory-embedded **grouted splice sleeves**.
  * Non-shrink, high-strength thixotropic cementitious grout (minimum 60 MPa compressive strength at 28 days) injected under pressure.
  * Continuous horizontal shear keys and perimeter bond beams with cast-in-situ concrete screed toppings reinforced with welded wire mesh (WWM).
  * Two-stage weatherproofing of external joints using backing rods, polysulfide / polyurethane joint sealants, and internal decompression chambers.

### 2. Architectural Finishes Schedule
* **Flooring**:
  * Living, Dining, Bedrooms: Premium 600x600mm or 800x800mm Double Charged Vitrified Tiles.
  * Bathrooms, Kitchens, Balconies: Anti-skid ceramic tiles (Group IV/V durability).
  * Corridors & Common Staircases: Polished Granite / Kota Stone with anti-slip grooves.
* **Walls & Ceiling**:
  * Internal: Smooth plaster/POP punning finished with low-VOC Acrylic Oil Bound Distemper (OBD) / Plastic Emulsion Paint.
  * External: High-durability weather-proof elastomeric acrylic paint resistant to fungal growth and UV degradation.
* **Fenestration & Joinery**:
  * Main Doors: Teakwood / Hardwood composite frames with flush door shutters faced with natural veneer.
  * Internal Doors: Pre-hung skin-moulded factory-engineered doors with stainless steel hardware.
  * Windows: Heavy-duty multi-chambered UPVC sliding / casement windows with 5mm to 6mm toughened float glass.
* **Sanitary & Plumbing**:
  * CP Fittings: Jaquar / Kohler / Hindware or equivalent approved makes.
  * Vitreous Chinaware: White wall-hung / floor-mounted EWC with dual-flush concealed cisterns.
