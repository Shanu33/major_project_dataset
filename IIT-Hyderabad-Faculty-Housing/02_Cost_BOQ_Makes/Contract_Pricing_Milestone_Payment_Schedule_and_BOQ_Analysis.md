### 2. Volume 02b Payment Schedule & Milestone Framework

Payments in the EPC precast model are structured against verifiable engineering and construction milestones:

1. **Milestone 1 - Preliminary Engineering & Submittals (3-5%)**:
   * Detailed architectural GFC drawings, structural 3D BIM models, geotechnical verification, and precast shop drawing approvals.
2. **Milestone 2 - Substructure & Foundation (15-20%)**:
   * Site grading, bulk excavation, foundation casting (isolated/raft/piles), plinth beams, and basement/ground slab readiness.
3. **Milestone 3 - Offsite Precast Element Manufacturing (25-30%)**:
   * Progressive casting of precast columns, load-bearing shear walls, precast beams, and hollow-core / solid precast slabs in certified casting yard.
4. **Milestone 4 - Tower Erection & Structural Top-Out (25-30%)**:
   * Erection, alignment, joint grouting, post-tensioning/stitching, and structural topping slab casting floor-by-floor up to 12th floor roof level.
5. **Milestone 5 - Internal MEP & Architectural Finishes (10-15%)**:
   * Conduiting, wiring, plumbing risers, sanitaryware, doors, UPVC windows, vitrified tiling, and internal/external painting.
6. **Milestone 6 - Testing, Commissioning & Handover (5-7%)**:
   * Fire NOC, elevator commissioning, sub-station energization, STP connections, as-built documentation, and final completion certificate.
