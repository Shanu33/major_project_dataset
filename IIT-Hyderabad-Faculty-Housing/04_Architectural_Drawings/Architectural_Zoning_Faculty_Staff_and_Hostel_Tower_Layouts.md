# Architectural Zoning, Faculty, Staff & Hostel Tower Layouts
## IIT Hyderabad Precast Housing Package (Volume 05 Extract)

---

### 1. Master Campus Zoning & Orientation
* **Site Location**: Residential and Hostel Zone of the IIT Hyderabad 576-acre permanent campus at Kandi, Sangareddy.
* **Micro-Climatic Orientation**:
  * Towers oriented along North-South solar azimuth to minimize direct solar insolation on extensive facades while capturing dominant Southwest monsoon breezes.
  * Optimal building separation preventing overshadowing and ensuring adequate daylight penetration conforming to National Building Code (NBC) 2016 standards.

---

### 2. Typology Planning & Space Program

| Typology Identifier | Building Footprint & Height | Spatial Configuration | Target Occupant Demographics |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Faculty Housing Towers** (2 Towers) | G + 12 Storeys | 3BHK and 4BHK spacious executive suites with dedicated study rooms, servant quarters, and double balconies | Institute Professors, Associate Professors, and Visiting Scholars |
| **Staff Housing Towers** (3 Towers) | G + 12 Storeys | 2BHK and 3BHK functional family apartments with living-dining, kitchen, utility wash area, and 2 toilets | Administrative Officers, Technical Staff, and Support Personnel |
| **Hostel Blocks** (3 Blocks) | G + 6 Storeys | Single and twin-occupancy student dormitory rooms with attached/centralized washroom cores, common study halls, reading rooms, dining mess, and recreation rooms | Undergraduate, Postgraduate, and PhD Research Scholars |

---

### 3. Circulation & Universal Accessibility
* **Vertical Circulation**: High-speed automatic gearless passenger lifts (13-passenger capacity) equipped with Automatic Rescue Devices (ARD) and dedicated stretcher-cum-service elevators in each tower.
* **Universal Accessibility**: 100% barrier-free access with exterior ramps (1:12 slope), tactile pavers, braille-embossed elevator push buttons, and specially designed accessible ground-floor living units.
