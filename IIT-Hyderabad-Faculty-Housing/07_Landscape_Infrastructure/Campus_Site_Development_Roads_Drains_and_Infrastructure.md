# Campus Site Development, Roads, Drains & Infrastructure
## IIT Hyderabad Precast Housing Package (Volume 05 Extract)

---

### 1. Campus Infrastructure Integration
* **Road Network**: Concrete paved dual-lane access roads connecting the housing and hostel cluster to the central academic spine and outer campus ring road.
* **Pedestrian Infrastructure**: Continuous shaded pedestrian sidewalks, bicycle tracks, and crosswalks paved with heavy-duty interlocking concrete paver blocks.
* **Stormwater Drainage**: RCC box drains and roadside swales engineered to handle peak monsoon precipitation, channeling surface runoff into campus recharge ponds and harvesting pits.

---

### 2. Environmental & Landscape Planning
* **Landscape Architecture**: Native drought-tolerant tree species, manicured lawn mounds, reflexology walkways, and shaded outdoor seating gazebos around the residential towers.
* **Recreational Amenities**: Outdoor sports courts (badminton, basketball, volleyball) and open-air recreational plazas integrated between the 3 hostel blocks.
* **Outdoor Lighting**: High-efficiency LED street lights and pathway bollards powered by dedicated solar-PV microgrid circuits.
