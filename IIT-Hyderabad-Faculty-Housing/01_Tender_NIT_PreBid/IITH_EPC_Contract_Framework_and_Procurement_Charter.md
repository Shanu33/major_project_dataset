# IITH EPC Contract Framework & Procurement Charter
## IIT Hyderabad Faculty Housing, Staff Housing & Student Hostels (NIT No. IITH/CMD/CIVIL/NIT/2022-23/09)

---

### 1. EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) Contract Architecture
* **Turnkey Delivery Mechanism**:
  * The contractor is single-point responsible for architectural adaptation, structural engineering, precast factory production, logistics, craneage/erection, MEP integration, interior finishes, and commissioning.
  * Governed by **Volume 06 (General Conditions of Contract for EPC Projects)** and **Volume 01 (Special Conditions of Contract)**.
* **Tender Procedure**:
  * Two-cover open online bidding system published via the Central Public Procurement Portal (CPPP - `eprocure.gov.in`) and IIT Hyderabad Tender Portal (`iith.ac.in/tenders/`).
  * Rigorous technical pre-qualification assessing contractor experience in precast high-rise structures (minimum G+8 to G+12 execution credentials).

---

### 2. Financing & Governance Structure
* **Financing Channel**: Funded under the **Higher Education Financing Agency (HEFA)** term loan window sanctioned by the Ministry of Education (MoE), Government of India.
* **Institutional Governance**:
  * Executive Engineer (Civil) and Superintending Engineer, Construction & Maintenance Division (CMD), IIT Hyderabad serve as the Engineer-in-Charge.
  * All major contractual awards, scope expansions, and budget allocations are ratified directly by the **IIT Hyderabad Board of Governors (BoG)**.
* **Defect Liability Period (DLP)**: 24 to 36 months comprehensive post-completion defect rectification warranty backed by Performance Bank Guarantees (PBG).
