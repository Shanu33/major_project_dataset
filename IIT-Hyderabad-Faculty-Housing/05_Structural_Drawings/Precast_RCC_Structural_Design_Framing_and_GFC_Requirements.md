# Precast RCC Structural Design Framing & GFC Requirements
## IIT Hyderabad Precast Housing Package (Volume 05 Extract)

---

### 1. Structural Codes & Loading Criteria
* **Design Standards**:
  * **IS 456:2000**: Plain and Reinforced Concrete - Code of Practice.
  * **IS 1893 (Part 1): 2016**: Criteria for Earthquake Resistant Design of Structures.
  * **IS 13920:2016**: Ductile Design and Detailing of Reinforced Concrete Structures.
  * **IS 875 (Parts 1-5): 2015**: Design Loads (Dead, Imposed, Wind) for Buildings and Structures.
  * **IS 15916:2010**: Code of Practice for Building Design and Erection Using Prefabricated Concrete.
* **Seismic Parameters**:
  * Seismic Zone: **Zone II** (Low Damage Risk Zone, Zone Factor $Z = 0.10$).
  * Importance Factor ($I$): $1.2$ for high-occupancy residential campus buildings.
  * Response Reduction Factor ($R$): $5.0$ (Special RC Shear Wall Precast System with ductile joint connections).
* **Wind Parameters**: Basic wind speed $V_b = 44$ m/s (Sangareddy / Hyderabad region).

---

### 2. Foundation & Geotechnical Baseline
* **Sub-soil Investigation (Volume 05)**:
  * Borehole profiling reveals red gravelly soil/clayey silts underlain by dense disintegrated rock (murrum) and sound granite bedrock at shallow-to-moderate depths (2.0m to 5.0m).
  * Safe Bearing Capacity (SBC): Ranges from $250$ to $400$ kN/m$^2$ at founding depth.
* **Foundation System**:
  * Cast-in-situ reinforced concrete isolated/combined footings with grade beams or continuous raft slab anchored on the hard stratum.
  * Plinth level transitions from cast-in-situ foundation to precast vertical shear walls and column pockets.

---

### 3. Structural Superstructure & Connection Detailing
* **Lateral Force Resisting System (LFRS)**: Precast concrete shear wall panels acting as vertical cantilevers tied through monolithic floor diaphragms.
* **Floor Diaphragm**: Precast prestressed hollow-core slabs or solid precast slabs with 65mm-75mm cast-in-situ structural topping reinforced with TMT mesh to guarantee rigid diaphragm action during seismic lateral load transfers.
* **Progressive Collapse Mitigation**: Robust internal, peripheral, and vertical ties provided in accordance with IS 15916 and international precast codes (ACI 318 / Eurocode 2) preventing disproportionate collapse.
