# Methodology Note: Precast High-Rise Construction & HEFA Financing
## Technical Benchmarking & Repository Integrity

---

### 1. Precast High-Rise Construction Benchmark
* **Significance**: Represents one of the largest institutional deployments of full precast RCC technology for G+12 high-rise residential towers in India.
* **Speed of Execution**: Factory-controlled precasting reduces structural construction cycle time by 40-50% compared to conventional in-situ cast RCC, achieving typical floor cycles of 4 to 6 days per floor.
* **Superior Quality Control**: Concrete casting in a controlled factory/yard environment eliminates honeycombing, improves concrete compaction, ensures precise rebar cover, and provides superior surface finishes requiring minimal plastering.
