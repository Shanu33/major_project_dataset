# CPWD e-Tender & Central Procurement Portal Navigation Guide

**Official Portals:**
- CPWD e-Tender Portal: `https://www.etender.cpwd.gov.in`
- Central Public Procurement Portal: `https://eprocure.gov.in`

---

## 1. Step-by-Step Document Access Instructions

To search and retrieve the live bid documents, BOQs, corrigenda, and technical packs from the official portals:

1. **Accessing CPWD e-Tender Portal:**
   - Navigate to `https://www.etender.cpwd.gov.in/`.
   - On the homepage, select **"Tenders by Organisation"** -> select **"Central Public Works Department"**.
   - Alternatively, enter the Master Reference Number in the Tender Search bar:  
     `01/NIT/CE CUM ED/ EE & SM-I/2024-25` or search Tender ID: `96818` / `89870`.

2. **Accessing via Central Public Procurement Portal (CPPP):**
   - Navigate to `https://eprocure.gov.in/eprocure/app`.
   - Select **"Search by Tender ID"** -> Input: `2024_CPWD_759521_1`.
   - Select **"View Tender Details"** to access the complete tender schedule, BOQ Excel sheet, and technical bid PDF packs.
