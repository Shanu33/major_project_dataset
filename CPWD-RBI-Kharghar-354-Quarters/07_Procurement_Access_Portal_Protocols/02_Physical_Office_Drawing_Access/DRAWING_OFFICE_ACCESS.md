## 2. Drawing Sets Available for Physical Inspection

1. **Architectural Working Sets:**
   - Master Layout Plan (Drawing CPWD/RBI/2021/L0/3/901 to 915).
   - Typical Floor Plans for Grade D, Grade B/C, and Grade A Towers.
   - ZTC Hostel Detailed Architectural Drawings & Sections.
   - Academic Block Tiered Auditorium & Classroom Plans.
2. **Structural & Foundation Drawings:**
   - Pile foundation layout, pile load testing reports, and pile cap reinforcement schedules.
   - High-rise column schedules, shear wall ductile detailing, and beam-slab schedules.
3. **MEP & Specialized Engineering Sets:**
   - 11 kV Substation layout, single-line diagrams (SLD), and cable tray routes.
   - Plumbing riser diagrams, STP layout, and fire-fighting hydraulic calculations.
