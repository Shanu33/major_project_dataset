- **11 kV High-Tension (HT) Switchgear:**
  - 11 kV indoor metal-clad Vacuum Circuit Breaker (VCB) panel board, 630A, 25 kA for 3 seconds fault rating.
  - Microprocessor-based combined numerical overcurrent, earth fault, and undervoltage protection relays.
- **Transformers:**
  - 2 Nos. $11	ext{ kV} / 433	ext{ V}$, Oil-Immersed / Cast Resin Dry Type Transformers (1,000 kVA or 1,250 kVA rating) with ONAN cooling and Off-Circuit Tap Changers (OCTC).
- **Main Low-Tension (LT) Switchgear:**
  - Form 4b fully compartmentalized Air Circuit Breaker (ACB) panels with micro-processor trip units.
  - Automatic Power Factor Correction (APFC) capacitor banks maintaining power factor $\ge 0.98$ lagging.

  - 2 Nos. CPCB-IV+ compliant acoustic enclosed Diesel Generator (DG) sets (500 kVA / 750 kVA) with AMF panels providing 100% emergency backup for lifts, fire pumps, street lights, and essential domestic circuits.
