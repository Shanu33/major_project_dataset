# Solar PV Generation, EV Charging & Extra Low Voltage (ELV) Systems

**Project Standards:** CEA Regulations, MNRE Directives & GRIHA Green Building Compliance  

---

## 1. Grid-Interactive Rooftop Solar Photovoltaic (SPV) Plant

- **Capacity:** ~150 kWp to 200 kWp distributed across the terraces of the 9 residential towers, ZTC Hostel, and Academic Block.
- **Solar Modules:** High-efficiency Mono-PERC / Bifacial PV modules ($\ge 540	ext{ Wp}$) with anti-reflective coating conforming to IEC 61215 and IEC 61730.
- **Grid-Tie Inverters:** String inverters with MPPT tracking, IP65 weather enclosure, and remote IoT monitoring dashboards.
- **Green Offset:** Solar energy directly offsets common area lighting, water pumping stations, and sewage treatment plant operational consumption.

---

## 2. Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure

- **Charger Density:** Dedicated EV bays on Podium Level P1 and surface parking conforming to Maharashtra EV Policy 2021.
- **Fast DC Chargers:** 2 Nos. 60 kW Dual-Gun CCS-2 DC Fast Chargers for institutional and fleet vehicles.
- **AC Slow Chargers:** 15 Nos. 7.4 kW / 22 kW Type-2 AC Smart Chargers for staff personal electric cars and scooters with RFID authentication and billing integration.

---

## 3. Extra Low Voltage (ELV) & Smart Security Systems

- **IP-CCTV Surveillance:** 4K ultra-high-definition IP cameras covering perimeter boundary walls, gates, podium walkways, building foyers, and lift lobbies.
- **Access Control:** Biometric fingerprint/RFID access control for academic labs, clubhouse, and server rooms.
- **Boom Barriers & ANPR:** Automatic Number Plate Recognition (ANPR) integrated motorized boom barriers at entry/exit gates.
- **Public Address & Fire Voice Evacuation:** Central PA system with ceiling/column speakers linked to addressable fire alarm panels.
