## 1. Scope Breakdown — Package-2

Package-2 represents a premier institutional interior and fit-out contract covering all 354 residential apartments, the ZTC Hostel, and the Academic Block:

1. **Factory-Made Modular Kitchen Systems:**
   - Marine grade boiling waterproof (BWP) plywood conforming to IS 710 with 1.0 mm high-pressure decorative laminate (HPL) on both sides.
   - Soft-close stainless steel (SS 304) tandem drawer systems, telescopic runners, corner carousel units, and cutlery inserts (Hettich / Hafele / Blum).
   - 20 mm thick mirror-polished jet black granite platform with molded bullnose edging and under-mount double-bowl SS 304 sink.
   - Overhead wall cabinets with frosted glass hydraulic lift-up flaps and integrated LED under-cabinet strip illumination.

2. **Custom Modular Wardrobes & Storage:**
   - Floor-to-ceiling built-in wardrobes in master bedrooms, guest bedrooms, and children's rooms across all 354 flats.
   - 18 mm BWP grade core ply with anti-fungal internal fabric-textured laminate and exterior textured decorative finishes.
   - Integrated full-length dressing mirrors, tie racks, safety lockers, and automated internal sensor LED lights.

3. **Loose & Ready-Made Furniture Supply:**
   - Living Room: 3+1+1 upholstered solid teakwood/oak sofa suites, tempered glass center tables, TV credenza consoles, and shoe storage cabinets.
   - Dining Area: Solid hardwood 6-seater / 4-seater dining tables with ergonomic upholstered chairs.
   - Bedrooms: King-size and Queen-size storage beds with hydraulic lifting mechanisms, 8-inch high-density orthopaedic pocket-spring mattresses, and bedside tables.

4. **Fitness, Recreational & Children's Play Equipment:**
   - Professional indoor clubhouse gym: Commercial grade motorized treadmills, cross trainers, multi-gym stations, Olympic barbells, dumbbell racks, and rubberized shock-absorbing flooring.
   - Outdoor podium playground: Heavy-duty FRP multi-activity play stations, spiral slides, double swings, see-saws, and EPDM safety impact surfacing.
