# Community Clubhouse, Swimming Pool & Commercial Support

**Purpose:** Staff Welfare, Wellness & Neighborhood Civic Self-Sufficiency  

---

## 1. Multi-Facility Community Clubhouse

- **Swimming Complex:**
  - Semi-Olympic size outdoor swimming pool ($25	ext{ m} 	imes 12.5	ext{ m}$) with balancing tank and ozonated sand filtration plant.
  - Dedicated shallow toddlers' splash pool ($0.6	ext{ m}$ depth) with anti-slip perimeter grating.
  - Male and female changing rooms with sauna and shower stalls.
- **Indoor Sports & Recreation:**
  - Wooden sprung floor badminton court meeting BWF dimensional standards.
  - Billiards and snooker room with tournament-grade slate tables.
  - Two table tennis stations and indoor carrom/chess recreation rooms.

---

## 2. Commercial & Administrative Facilities

- **Convenience Commercial Block:**
  - Dedicated spaces for daily grocery/supermarket, milk booth, pharmacy, and ATM kiosk.
- **Caretaker & Security Infrastructure:**
  - Central estate maintenance depot, plumber/electrician station, and caretaker residential flat.
  - Multi-operator telecom tower provision and optical fiber backbone network.
