# Zonal Training Centre (ZTC) Hostel & Academic Block Architecture

**Client:** Reserve Bank of India  
**Design Consultants:** M/s Nivedita & Uday Pande Consultants, New Delhi  

---

## 1. ZTC Hostel Building (Ground + 8 Floors)

- **Accommodation Profile:**
  - 82 Air-conditioned twin-sharing guest suites designed for mid-level and senior RBI trainee delegates.
  - Attached en-suite bathrooms equipped with hot water solar/geyser systems and water-saving CP sanitary fittings.
- **Guest Facilities:**
  - Central dining hall seating 120 guests simultaneously with modular hot bain-marie buffet counters.
  - Fully commercial hygienic kitchen equipped with stainless steel cooking ranges, cold storage rooms, and dishwashing plants.
  - Officer business lounge, reading rooms, laundry drop-off counters, and high-speed Wi-Fi throughout.

---

## 2. Academic Block (Ground + 2 Floors)

- **Training Infrastructure:**
  - 4 Nos. High-tech lecture halls with tiered amphitheater seating and interactive smart displays.
  - 2 Nos. 50-seater specialized computer simulation labs for financial analytics and banking systems training.
  - Modern central library and digital archives with dedicated quiet study carrels.
  - State-of-the-art 250-seat auditorium with professional digital acoustics, line-array speakers, and motorized projection screens.
