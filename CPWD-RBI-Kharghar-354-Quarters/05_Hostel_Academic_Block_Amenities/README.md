# 05 - Institutional ZTC Hostel, Academic Block & Clubhouse

Architectural planning and specifications for the institutional and staff welfare complex:
- ZTC Hostel Building: Ground + 8 Floors, 82 twin-sharing suites, dining hall, and commercial kitchen.
- Academic Block: Ground + 2 Floors, 4 smart lecture halls, 2 computer simulation labs, and 250-seat auditorium.
- Community Clubhouse: Semi-Olympic swimming pool, sprung-floor badminton court, and gym.
- Commercial Block: Pharmacy, grocery store, milk booth, and estate maintenance depot.
