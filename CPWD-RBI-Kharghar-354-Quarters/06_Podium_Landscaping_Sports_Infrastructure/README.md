# 06 - Podium Landscaping, Sports Courts & 24 Materials Palette

Architectural hardscape and recreational infrastructure per Drawing `CPWD/RBI/2021/L0/3/903` by M/s Nivedita & Uday Pande Consultants:
- 24-Material Landscape Palette (Bitumen, Pavers, Basalt Cobble, Flamed/Polished Granite, Kadappa, Kota).
- Active sports: Basketball court, 2 badminton courts, and roller skating rink.
- Health & wellness: Peripheral cushioned PU jogging track, open-air gym, and yoga court.
- Children & family: EPDM multi-play area, sand pit, and central festive event plaza.
