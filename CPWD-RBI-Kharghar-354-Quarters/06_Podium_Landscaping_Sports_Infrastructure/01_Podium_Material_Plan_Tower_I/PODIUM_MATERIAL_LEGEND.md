## 2. Integrated Recreational Activities & Sports Schedule

The podium is divided into designated active, passive, and communal activity nodes:

1. **Active Sports Enclaves:**
   - Standard Outdoor Basketball Court with acrylic cushioning and perimeter chain-link fencing.
   - 2 Nos. Outdoor Badminton Courts with anti-glare floodlighting.
   - Dedicated Roller Skating Rink with smooth polished concrete surface and safety guard rails.
2. **Health & Fitness Zones:**
   - Continuous cushioned PU Jogging Track looping around the podium perimeter.
   - Outdoor Fitness Gym equipped with weather-proof bodyweight workout apparatus.
   - Elevated Yoga & Meditation Court nestled amid quiet landscape greenery.
3. **Children & Family Community Nodes:**
   - Children's Multi-Play Area with EPDM impact flooring and modern play structures.
   - Sand Pit with sensory activity boards.
   - Central Festive Plaza & Event Amphitheater for community celebrations and national festivals.
   - Senior Citizen Pavilions with shaded pergola benches.
