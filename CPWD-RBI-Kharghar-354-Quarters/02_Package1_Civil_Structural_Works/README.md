# 02 - Package-1: Civil & Structural Engineering Framework

Structural design parameters and tower configurations for 9 high-rise residential towers:
- 1 Tower Grade D (19 Quarters, G+10)
- 4 Towers Grade B/C (159 Quarters, G+10)
- 4 Towers Grade A (176 Quarters, G+11)
- Bored cast-in-situ pile foundations, M-30/M-25 concrete grades, Fe-500D TMT reinforcement, and coastal waterproofing.
