# Civil Engineering Specifications & Structural Parameters

**Standard Authority:** CPWD Specifications 2019 (Volumes I & II) & Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)  
**Location:** CBD Belapur (Kharghar), Navi Mumbai (Coastal Marine Environment)  

---

## 1. Structural Engineering System

- **Framed System:** Earthquake-resistant Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC) space framed structure compliant with **IS 13920:2016** (Ductile Detailing).
- **Foundation System:** Deep RCC Bored Cast-in-Situ Piles resting on competent basalt rock strata / heavy-duty combined raft foundation under core shear walls.
- **Seismic Design Parameters:**
  - Seismic Zone: **Zone III** (per IS 1893:2016).
  - Zone Factor ($Z$): 0.16.
  - Importance Factor ($I$): 1.20 (Institutional / Central Bank residential assets).
  - Response Reduction Factor ($R$): 5.0 (Special Moment Resisting Frame - SMRF).

---

## 2. Material Specifications

- **Concrete Grades:**
  - Minimum **M-30** grade concrete for all columns, shear walls, and foundation elements.
  - Minimum **M-25** grade concrete for beams, slabs, lintels, and chajjas.
  - Water-cement ratio strictly limited to $\le 0.45$ with polycarboxylate ether (PCE) based superplasticizers conforming to IS 9103.
- **Steel Reinforcement:**
  - High-Yield Strength Deformed (HYSD) **Fe 500D / Fe 550D** TMT bars complying with IS 1786.
  - Elongation $\ge 14.5\%$ for enhanced energy dissipation during seismic excitation.
- **Masonry Infill:**
  - Exterior walls: 200 mm thick Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) blocks conforming to IS 2185 (Part 3) with polymer-modified adhesive mortar.
  - Interior partition walls: 100 mm / 150 mm thick AAC blocks.

---

## 3. Coastal Marine Waterproofing System

Due to Navi Mumbai's proximity to coastal creeks and high rainfall:
- **Substructure Waterproofing:** Integral crystalline waterproofing compound added to foundation concrete + exterior bentonite geotextile / SBS modified bitumen membrane.
- **Podium Slab Deck:** Two-coat elastomeric polyurethane (PU) waterproofing system with 100 mm protective cement screed graded to drainage gullies.
- **Toilet Sunken Slabs & Balconies:** Fiber-reinforced elastomeric acrylic waterproof coating carried 300 mm up the peripheral brick walls.
