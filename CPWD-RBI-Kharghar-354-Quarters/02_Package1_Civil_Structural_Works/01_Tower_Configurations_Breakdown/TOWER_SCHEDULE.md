## 2. Institutional & Auxiliary Building Schedule

In addition to the 354 residential staff quarters, the complex accommodates comprehensive institutional, hospitality, and civic support buildings:

1. **Zonal Training Centre (ZTC) Hostel Building:**
   - Configuration: **Ground + 8 Upper Floors (G+8)**.
   - Capacity: **82 Guest Rooms** (twin-sharing configuration for visiting trainee officers).
   - Amenities: Executive dining hall, commercial kitchen, recreation lounge, laundry suites, and baggage rooms.

2. **Academic & Training Block:**
   - Configuration: **Ground + 2 Floors (G+2)**.
   - Facilities: High-tech seminar halls, computer training laboratories, library, syndicate rooms, and a 250-seat state-of-the-art auditorium.

3. **Club House & Wellness Centre:**
   - Configuration: **Ground + 1 Floor (G+1)**.
   - Facilities: Full-size swimming pool with baby pool, gymnasium, indoor badminton court, table tennis, billiards room, and community event hall.

4. **Civic & Support Ancillary Structures:**
   - Estate Maintenance Office, Caretaker Residence, Security Control Command Centre, Commercial Convenience Stores (Milk/Grocery/Pharmacy), Substation (ESS), and Pump Houses.
