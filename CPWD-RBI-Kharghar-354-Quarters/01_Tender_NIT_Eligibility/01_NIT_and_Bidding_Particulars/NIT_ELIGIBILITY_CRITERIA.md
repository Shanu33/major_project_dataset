## 2. Minimum Bidder Eligibility Criteria (CPWD Standard)

1. **Past Track Record in Similar Works:**
   - The bidder must have successfully completed similar composite residential / institutional building works during the last 7 years ending previous day of last date of submission of tenders:
     - **Three similar works** each costing not less than **₹15.00 Crore**, OR
     - **Two similar works** each costing not less than **₹23.00 Crore**, OR
     - **One similar work** costing not less than **₹37.00 Crore**.
   - *Definition of Similar Work:* Construction of multi-storeyed residential complexes / institutional training academies including internal electrification, HVAC, firefighting, and modular interior furnishings for Central/State Govt, PSUs, or Autonomous bodies.

2. **Financial Qualifications:**
   - **Average Annual Financial Turnover:** Minimum **₹30.00 Crore** on construction works during the immediate last three consecutive financial years (ending 31st March).
   - **Solvency Certificate:** Bankers Solvency Certificate from a Nationalized / Scheduled Commercial Bank for an amount of at least **₹18.50 Crore** (40% of package cost) issued within 6 months.
   - **Net Worth:** Certified net worth must be positive and minimum **₹10.00 Crore** as of the last audited balance sheet.

3. **Technical Manpower & Equipment Mandate:**
   - Project Manager: B.E. (Civil) with minimum 15 years experience in high-rise building projects.
   - Resident Engineers (Civil & Electrical): Minimum 2 Degree holders (8+ years exp) or 4 Diploma holders (10+ years exp).
   - Dedicated QA/QC In-charge and Safety Officers (certified by RLI/CLI).
   - Fully computerized on-site testing laboratory accredited for compressive strength, slump, silt content, and sieve analysis.
