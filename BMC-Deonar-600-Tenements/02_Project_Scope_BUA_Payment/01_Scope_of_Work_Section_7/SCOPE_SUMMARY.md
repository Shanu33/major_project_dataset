## 2. Detailed Scope of Work

1. **Architectural & Civil Works:**
   - Turnkey execution of 6 towers (Buildings 01 to 06) from foundation up to terrace level.
   - 3 levels of continuous podium parking (Levels P1, P2, and P3) covering 60,881.88 m² total area.
   - Stilt level incorporating entrance lobbies, society offices, service meters, and security cabins.
   - 22 upper residential floors per tower, each accommodating typical 1BHK / self-contained 300 sq.ft units.
   - Refuge floors and terrace amenities conforming to Mumbai Fire Brigade (CFO) and NBC 2016 norms.

2. **Social & Commercial Infrastructure:**
   - 10 Commercial Shops on designated ground/podium frontage.
   - Bank branch space, Balwadis, Crèches, and Gymnasium.
   - Multi-purpose Community Halls and Welfare Centre for residents.
   - Society offices, security kiosks, and automated boom barrier access control.

3. **Public Health & Environmental Engineering (MEP):**
   - Underground Water Storage Reservoirs (UGT) with dedicated domestic and fire-fighting compartments.
   - Hydro-pneumatic pumping stations and overhead water tanks (OHT) on all towers.
   - Dedicated Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) with tertiary filtration for flushing and landscape recycling.
   - Rainwater harvesting system with recharge borewells and desilting chambers.
   - Solid waste management facility with organic waste composters (OWC).

4. **Electrical & Fire-Life Safety Systems:**
   - Dual-source electrical supply network, substation, transformers, and 100% DG backup for essential services.
   - High-speed passenger and stretcher elevators with automatic rescue devices (ARD).
   - Wet riser, automatic sprinkler network, addressable fire alarm, smoke evacuation, and fire dampers.
   - Solar PV rooftop generation and solar-powered campus street lighting.

5. **Site Infrastructure & External Development:**
   - Boundary wall, storm water drainage network connected to municipal outfalls, and site nallah canalization.
   - Heavy-duty asphalt/concrete internal access roads designed for fire tender movement (minimum 6.0m to 9.0m clear width).
   - Landscaping, children's play equipment, senior citizen pavilions, and tree transplantation.
