## 1. Prefabricated / Composite Construction Technology Mandate

Under the special conditions of contract, BMC mandates modern rapid-construction engineering to ensure structural durability, fire resistance, and execution speed within the 24-month timeline:

- **Technology Paradigm:**
  - Mandatory Composite Construction or Precast Concrete Wall/Slab Panel System.
  - Monolithic concrete structural systems (aluminium formwork / MIVAN or precast modular construction) are permitted provided they meet seismic and structural standards.
  - Standard conventional brick masonry infill is restricted; lightweight aerated autoclaved concrete (AAC) blocks or precast hollow/solid concrete panels must be utilized.

- **Quality & Dimensional Tolerances:**
  - Precast elements manufactured in certified off-site casting yards or dedicated on-site automated batching & precast plants.
  - Joint connections, shear keys, and non-shrink grouting shall comply with IS 15916 (Code of Practice for Building Design and Erection Using Prefabricated Concrete Systems).
  - High-precision seismic connection details compliant with IS 13920:2016 (Ductile Design and Detailing of Reinforced Concrete Structures).

---

## 2. High-Performance Concrete Specifications

- **Minimum Concrete Grade:**
  - **M-40** is the mandatory minimum grade for all columns, shear walls, beams, slabs, transfer girders, and foundation elements.
  - Controlled batching through automated RMC plants with micro-silica / fly ash mineral admixtures conforming to IS 456:2000 and IS 10262:2019.
  - Maximum water-cement ratio: $\le 0.40$.

- **Steel Reinforcement:**
  - Thermo-Mechanically Treated (TMT) bars of grade **Fe 500D** or **Fe 550D** conforming to IS 1786.
  - Mandatory carbon equivalent $\le 0.42\%$ to ensure ductility and weldability under dynamic seismic loads.
  - Splicing via mechanical couplers for bar diameters $\ge 20$ mm.

---

## 3. Durability & Coastal Marine Environment Protection

Given Mumbai's saline coastal atmosphere and proximity of Deonar to creek waters:
- Concrete cover: Minimum 40 mm for beams/columns and 50 mm for foundations in contact with soil/subsoil water.
- Corrosion inhibitors and crystalline waterproofing admixtures in all basement/podium retaining walls and water-retaining structures.
- Multi-layer elastomeric waterproofing system for podium slab top decks, landscape planter boxes, and terrace slabs with a minimum 10-year written performance guarantee.

---

## 4. Internal & External Finishing Standards

- **Flooring:** Vitrified tile flooring ($600 \times 600$ mm) in living rooms and bedrooms; anti-skid ceramic tiles in bathrooms and balconies.
- **Kitchen Platform:** Granite platform with stainless steel sink, glazed ceramic tile dado up to 2 ft height above counter.
- **Joinery:** Flush doors with laminate finish / FRP doors for toilets; powder-coated aluminium sliding windows with clear float glass and safety grills.
- **External Paint:** High-performance exterior grade elastomeric acrylic emulsion paint with anti-fungal and anti-algal properties resistant to heavy monsoon rain and UV exposure.
