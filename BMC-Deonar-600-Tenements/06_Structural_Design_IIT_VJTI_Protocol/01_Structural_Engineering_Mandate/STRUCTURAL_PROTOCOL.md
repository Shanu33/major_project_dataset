## 1. Contractor's Turnkey Structural Engineering Responsibility

In accordance with the Turnkey EPC model:
- The contractor is exclusively responsible for the complete structural design, engineering calculations, and detailed execution drawings for all 6 towers (P+S+22 floors), podium decks, ramps, retaining walls, UGTs, and auxiliary structures.
- All structural designs must strictly adhere to the latest Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) codes:
  - **IS 456:2000** — Plain and Reinforced Concrete
  - **IS 1893 (Part 1): 2016** — Criteria for Earthquake Resistant Design of Structures (Seismic Zone III, Mumbai)
  - **IS 13920:2016** — Ductile Detailing of Reinforced Concrete Structures Subjected to Seismic Forces
  - **IS 875 (Part 3): 2015** — Wind Loads on Buildings and Structures (Basic wind speed $V_b = 44\text{ m/s}$)
  - **IS 16700:2017** — Criteria for Structural Safety of Tall Concrete Buildings

---

## 2. Advanced 3D Computational Analysis

- Structural analysis must be performed using validated finite element analysis (FEA) software platforms such as **STAAD Pro**, **ETABS**, or **SAFE**.
- Dynamic response spectrum analysis, P-Delta secondary effect considerations, torsion sensitivity checks, and wind tunnel testing (or high-frequency base balance data) where building slenderness warrants.
- Base shear and drift limit: Story drift ratio must not exceed 0.004 under design seismic forces.

---

## 3. Mandatory Third-Party Vetting Protocol (IIT Bombay / VJTI Mumbai)

To ensure uncompromised structural integrity and safety for 2,068 families:
1. **Accredited Proof Check Institutions:**
   - **Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IIT Bombay)**, Powai, Mumbai, OR
   - **Veermata Jijabai Technological Institute (VJTI)**, Matunga, Mumbai.
2. **Scope of Vetting:**
   - Geotechnical investigation analysis, safe bearing capacity (SBC) validation, and pile/raft foundation design.
   - 3D computational structural model independent re-analysis.
   - High-rise RCC core wall and frame ductile detailing verification.
   - Prefabricated joint design, connection integrity, and shear transfer capacity.
3. **Execution Milestone:**
   - No civil execution or concreting work can commence on site without obtaining the official **Proof Checking Certificate and Structural Stability Endorsement** issued and sealed by the structural engineering department of IIT Bombay or VJTI Mumbai.
   - All proof checking and vetting fees payable to IIT Bombay / VJTI are borne fully by the turnkey contractor.
