# Scope of Work: Architectural Design & Master Planning

**Project:** KMRL Multi-Storey Residential Staff Quarters at Muttom  
**Location:** Muttom Metro Operations Yard, Aluva, Kochi  

---

## 1. Architectural Concept & Spatial Planning

1. **Site Analysis & Zoning:**
   - Topographic survey validation, solar orientation, prevailing wind considerations, and noise abatement measures due to proximity to the Muttom Metro Train Depot.
   - Comprehensive zoning separating vehicular service access, resident parking, pedestrian green corridors, and children's recreational zones.

2. **Apartment Typologies & Unit Planning:**
   - **Type-II Quarters:** Compact self-contained residential units for operating and technical field staff.
   - **Type-III Quarters:** 2-BHK executive residential units with living/dining, kitchen, utility balcony, and two bathrooms.
   - **Type-IV Quarters:** 3-BHK spacious quarters designed for supervisory officers and department heads.
   - Barrier-free accessible ground floor flats compliant with universal accessibility guidelines.

3. **Statutory Approvals & Compliance:**
   - Preparation and coordination of statutory sanction dossiers for local Municipality (Kalamassery / Aluva), Town Planning Department, Fire and Rescue Services (CFO NOC), and Kerala State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB).
