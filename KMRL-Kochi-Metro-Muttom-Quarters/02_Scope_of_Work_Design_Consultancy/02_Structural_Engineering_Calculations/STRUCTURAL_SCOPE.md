# Scope of Work: Structural Engineering Calculations & Modeling

**Mandate:** Complete 3D Structural Analysis & Independent Verification  
**Applicable Codes:** Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)  

---

## 1. 3D Computational Analysis

- **Finite Element Modeling:**
  - Structural modeling using validated commercial FEA software (STAAD Pro, ETABS, or SAFE).
  - Spatial consideration of multi-storey frames, core shear walls around lift cores, and staircase shafts.
- **Loading Combinations:**
  - Dead Loads (DL) per IS 875 (Part 1).
  - Imposed / Live Loads (LL) per IS 875 (Part 2).
  - Wind Loads (WL) per IS 875 (Part 3) considering Kochi basic wind speed $V_b = 39	ext{ m/s}$ and terrain category 2.
  - Seismic Analysis (EL) per IS 1893 (Part 1): 2016 for **Seismic Zone III** ($Z = 0.16$, $I = 1.2$, $R = 5.0$).

---

## 2. Structural Design Calculations Submission

As explicitly required by the tender conditions:
> *"Commented and approved concept drawings are to be taken up for starting the Structural work, a copy of the same to be submitted with the structural design calculations."*

- **Deliverables:**
  - Full structural calculation book detailing column axial-flexural interaction curves.
  - Beam flexural and shear reinforcement envelopes.
  - Slab two-way bending and deflection verifications.
  - Substructure pile/raft load distribution, lateral capacity, and settlement predictions under maximum serviceability load combinations.
