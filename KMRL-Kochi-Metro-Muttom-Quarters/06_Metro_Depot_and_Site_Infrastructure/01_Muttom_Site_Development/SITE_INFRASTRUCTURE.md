# Muttom Site Infrastructure & Metro Depot Environmental Interface

**Site Context:** Muttom, Kochi (Proximity to Kochi Metro Maintenance Depot & Train Yard)  

---

## 1. Environmental & Acoustic Engineering

- **Proximity to Metro Yard:** The staff quarters are located immediately adjacent to the Muttom Metro Train Stabling Depot and Maintenance Workshop.
- **Acoustic Mitigation:** Peripheral green belt buffer with dense evergreen foliage and acoustic barrier boundary walls to isolate train shunting noise.
- **Vibration Isolation:** Foundation designs reviewed to verify absence of resonant ground vibrations originating from rail depot trackwork.

---

## 2. External Services Infrastructure

- **Water Supply Network:** Connection to Kerala Water Authority (KWA) main supply + underground storage reservoirs (UGT) with dual domestic/fire reserves.
- **Sewage Treatment Plant (STP):** Decentralized modular STP utilizing Moving Bed Biofilm Reactor (MBBR) technology with tertiary sand and carbon filtration.
- **Internal Roads & Drainage:** Heavy-duty interlocking concrete paver driveways designed for emergency fire-tender axle loads with covered stormwater drainage channels.
