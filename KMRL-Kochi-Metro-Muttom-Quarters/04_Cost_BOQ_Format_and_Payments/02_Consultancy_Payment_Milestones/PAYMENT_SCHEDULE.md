# Consultancy Fee Staged Payment Schedule

**Payment Terms:** Progressive milestone-based disbursement upon technical deliverables approval  

---

## 1. Fee Disbursement Stages

| Milestone # | Project Stage & Deliverable | Percentage of Fee Payable | Cumulative Fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stage 1** | Inception Report, Site Layout Options & Concept Architectural Plans | **15%** | 15% |
| **Stage 2** | Detailed Architectural Plans & Statutory Clearance Drawings Submission | **25%** | 40% |
| **Stage 3** | Structural Design Calculations Book, Foundation & MEP Schemes Approval | **25%** | 65% |
| **Stage 4** | Final Bill of Quantities (BOQ), Detailed Specifications & Tender Docs | **15%** | 80% |
| **Stage 5** | Issuance of Stamped 'Good For Construction' (GFC) Complete Drawing Sets | **15%** | 95% |
| **Stage 6** | Construction Oversight & As-Built Verification / Final Completion | **05%** | 100% |

---

## 2. Performance Guarantee & Retentions

- **Performance Security:** 5% of total consultancy contract value submitted via Bank Guarantee.
- **Validity:** Maintained until completion of construction and issuance of final occupancy certificate.
