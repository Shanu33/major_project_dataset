# Bill of Quantities (BOQ) Format & Measurement Structure

**Tender Reference:** `KMRL/PRJ/STAFF QTRS @ MUTTOM-162/2014/TEN 03-15`  
**Contract Model:** Design Consultancy Lump-Sum Fee + Turnkey Construction Rate Schedule  

---

## 1. BOQ Pricing Architecture

The tender document provides the standard itemized format to be developed and priced:

| Schedule Section | Disciplinary Focus | Typical Work Items Included |
|---|---|---|
| **Schedule A** | **Civil Substructure & Piling** | Earthwork, dewatering, bored cast-in-situ piles, pile caps, grade beams |
| **Schedule B** | **Superstructure Concrete Frame** | M-25/M-30 RCC columns, beams, suspended floor slabs, lift core walls |
| **Schedule C** | **Masonry & Plastering** | Blockwork infill, lintels, chajjas, internal plastering, external sand-face plaster |
| **Schedule D** | **Finishes & Joinery** | Vitrified flooring, ceramic dado, flush doors, aluminium sliding windows |
| **Schedule E** | **Public Health Engineering (PHE)** | CPVC water lines, SWR drainage, sanitary fixtures, pumps, OHT & UGT |
| **Schedule F** | **Electrical Installations** | Point wiring, conduits, distribution boards, LED fixtures, lightning arrestor |
| **Schedule G** | **External Infrastructure** | Roads, paved paths, compound wall, gates, stormwater drains, STP |
