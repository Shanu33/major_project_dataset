# Technical Specifications & Civil Engineering Standards

**Authority Standards:** KMRL Technical Guidelines & CPWD/Kerala PWD Standard Specifications  

---

## 1. Concrete & Reinforcement Metallurgy

- **Concrete Grades:**
  - Minimum **M-25** for general reinforced concrete elements (slabs, beams, staircases).
  - Minimum **M-30** for primary columns, shear core walls, pile caps, and subterranean retaining walls.
  - Strictly automated ready-mix concrete (RMC) from certified batching plants with mineral admixtures (fly ash / GGBS) to mitigate coastal chloride permeability.
- **Reinforcement Steel:**
  - High-Yield Strength Deformed (HYSD) **Fe 500 / Fe 550D** Thermo-Mechanically Treated (TMT) bars complying with IS 1786.
  - Mandatory ductile detailing complying with **IS 13920:2016**.

---

## 2. Building Finishes & Coastal Durability

- **Masonry Infill:** Lightweight solid concrete blocks or cellular autoclaved aerated concrete (AAC) blocks bedded in polymer mortar.
- **Plastering & Painting:**
  - Internal: 12 mm thick smooth cement plaster finished with wall putty and premium acrylic emulsion.
  - External: Dual-coat 20 mm sand-faced cement plaster with waterproofing admixtures, coated with exterior-grade anti-fungal silicone acrylic elastomeric paint resistant to heavy coastal monsoons.
- **Joinery & Windows:** Heavy-gauge powder-coated or anodized aluminium sliding windows with clear float glass and safety stainless steel insect screens.
