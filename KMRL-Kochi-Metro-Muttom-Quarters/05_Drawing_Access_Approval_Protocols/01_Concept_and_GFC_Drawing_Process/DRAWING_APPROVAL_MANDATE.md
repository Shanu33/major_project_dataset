# Drawing Approval Protocol & GFC Release Mechanism

**Tender Reference:** Section 8 of Master Bid Document (`NIT-TENDER-DOCUMENT-STAFF-QUARTERS-AT-MUTTOM.pdf`)  

---

## 1. The Core Drawing Approval Workflow

The tender document mandates a strict peer-review and approval hierarchy before any on-site civil activity commences:

```
[Consultant Concept Submission] 
       │
       ▼
[KMRL Technical Committee Review & Comments]
       │
       ▼
[Approval of Concept Drawings] 
       │
       ▼
[Structural Design Calculations & Peer Validation] 
       │
       ▼
[Release of Stamped 'Good For Construction' (GFC) Drawings]
       │
       ▼
[Site Execution by Civil Contractor]
```

---

## 2. Drawing Deliverables Package

1. **Architectural GFC Set:** Master site plan, foundation setting-out plan, floor plans, sections A-A' & B-B', 4 elevations, door/window schedules, and toilet/kitchen detailing.
2. **Structural GFC Set:** Pile layout, reinforcement schedules for pile caps, plinth beams, column schedule, ductile shear wall detailing, and beam-slab reinforcement.
3. **Services GFC Set:** Water supply piping routes, sanitary drainage schematics, electrical conduits/wiring routes, single-line diagrams, and lightning protection layouts.
