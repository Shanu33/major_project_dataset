# KMRL Eligibility Criteria & Consultant Selection Norms

**Authority:** Kochi Metro Rail Limited (KMRL)  
**Contract No.:** `KMRL/PRJ/STAFF QTRS @ MUTTOM-162/2014/TEN 03-15`  
**Tender Title:** Design Consultancy Services for Construction of Multi-Storey Residential Staff Quarters at Muttom  

---

## 1. Professional Registration & Statutory Qualifications

1. **Architectural Firm / Principal Architect:**
   - The architectural lead or consulting firm must have a Principal Architect registered with the **Council of Architecture (COA)** under the Architects Act, 1972.
   - Minimum professional standing of 7 years in architectural master planning and multi-storeyed building design.

2. **Structural Engineering Consultant:**
   - The principal structural engineer must possess a Master's degree in Structural Engineering with licensure recognized by local municipal bodies and Kerala State PWD.
   - Demonstrated experience in 3D finite element analysis, dynamic response spectrum analysis, and ductile detailing per **IS 13920**.

3. **MEP & Specialized Services Consultants:**
   - Qualified mechanical, electrical, and public health engineers with demonstrated competency in residential substation design, firefighting networks, and sewage treatment plants (STP).

---

## 2. Technical Experience Benchmarks

- **Similar Work Track Record:**
  - Successfully provided comprehensive architectural and structural design consultancy for at least **three multi-storey residential projects** (each $\ge G+4$ floors) for government, public sector, metro rail, or reputed corporate clients during the last 7 years.
  - Comprehensive scope must have included concept formulation, structural calculation submission, statutory approvals, tender preparation, and GFC drawing issuance.

- **Financial Solvency:**
  - Demonstrated positive net worth and required minimum average annual turnover on consultancy services over the last 3 audited financial years.
