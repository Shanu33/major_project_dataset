# FLN Station: Service Building Vertical Extension Scope

**Location:** Phulera (FLN) DFCCIL Operational Station  
**Component:** Providing Additional Floor Over Existing Operational Service Building  

---

## 1. Structural Engineering & Construction Protocol

The vertical extension at Phulera constitutes a sensitive brownfield expansion over an active railway operational facility:

1. **Non-Destructive Testing & Substructure Audit:**
   - Visual inspection and non-destructive rebound hammer / ultrasonic pulse velocity (UPV) testing of existing ground floor RCC columns and beams.
   - Validation that existing foundation footings and soil bearing capacity are adequate for vertical gravity and wind increments.

2. **Column Rebar Anchoring & Structural Continuity:**
   - Chipping of existing roof slab column heads and drilling holes for vertical rebar continuation using high-strength chemical anchor injection mortar (Hilti HIT-RE 500 V3 or equivalent).
   - Micro-concrete encasement / column enlargement jacket where supplementary axial stiffness is mandated.

3. **Superstructure Floor Addition:**
   - Erection of lightweight block masonry and reinforced concrete roof slab.
   - Incorporation of duty officer rooms, crew rest areas, signaling equipment rooms, and conference amenities.

4. **Weatherproofing & Operational Safety:**
   - Multi-layer elastomeric waterproofing over the newly cast top terrace slab.
   - Strict safety netting, debris barriers, and electrical insulation to ensure **zero interference** with continuous 24x7 heavy-haul freight train operations on adjacent DFC tracks.
