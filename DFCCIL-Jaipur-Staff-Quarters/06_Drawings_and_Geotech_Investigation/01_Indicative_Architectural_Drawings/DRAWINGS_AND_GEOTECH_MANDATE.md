# Part VIII: Drawings & Physical Office Inspection Protocol

**Tender Reference:** Part VIII (Page 198) of Master Bid Document  
**Governing Clauses:** Clause 1.5 (Drawings) & Clause 2.17 (Architectural and Structural Design)  

---

## 1. Nature of Drawings Included in Part VIII

- **Indicative Architectural Drawings:**
  - Standard Type-3 Staff Quarter floor plans, elevations, and sections attached on Page 198 for bidding guidance.
  - Indicative floor extension plan for the existing service building at Phulera (FLN) station.
- **Contractual Status:**
  - Per Clause 2.17: *"These drawings are indicative only and are not to be used for execution purpose."*
  - The turnkey contractor must prepare detailed Good For Construction (GFC) working drawings based on the geotechnical investigation and obtain approval from the Engineer.

---

## 2. Drawing Inspection at Chief General Manager Office, Jaipur

Per Clause 1.5 (Page 13):
> *"The Drawing for the work can be seen in the office of the Chief General Manager/ DFCCIL/Jaipur at any time in working hours during working days. The drawings are only for the guidance of Tenderer(s). Detailed working drawings (if required) based generally on the drawing mentioned above, will be given by the Engineer or his representative from time to time."*
