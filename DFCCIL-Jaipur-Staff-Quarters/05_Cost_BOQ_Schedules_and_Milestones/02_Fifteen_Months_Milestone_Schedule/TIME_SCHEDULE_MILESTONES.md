# Part VI: 15-Month Milestone & Time Execution Schedule

**Contract Period:** 15 (Fifteen) Calendar Months  
**Governing Section:** Part VI (Page 135) of Master Tender Document  

---

## 1. Staged Execution Milestones

| Milestone No. | Target Month | Critical Path Deliverables & Physical Target | Financial Progress Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Milestone 1** | **Month 02** | Mobilization, Geotechnical Investigation, Structural Design & Vetting Approval | **10%** |
| **Milestone 2** | **Month 05** | Substructure completion, foundation footings & plinth beams across all 7 stations | **25%** |
| **Milestone 3** | **Month 08** | Superstructure RCC frame completion and FLN vertical floor slab casting | **55%** |
| **Milestone 4** | **Month 11** | Brickwork masonry infill, internal/external plastering, door frames fixing | **75%** |
| **Milestone 5** | **Month 14** | Flooring, electrical wiring, plumbing, sanitary fixtures, windows & painting | **95%** |
| **Milestone 6** | **Month 15** | Testing, commissioning of services, final inspection, site cleanup & handover | **100%** |

---

## 2. Liquidated Damages & Progress Enforcement

- If the contractor fails to achieve progress corresponding to the scheduled milestones, liquidated damages / penalties will be levied as prescribed in Part IV (GCC Clause 17) and Part V (SCC).
