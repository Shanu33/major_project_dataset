# Technical Specifications & Mandatory Engineering Vetting

**Governing Standards:** Indian Railways Standard Specifications (IRUSS), CPWD Specifications & BIS  
**Key Contract Clauses:** Page 13 (Clause 1.5) & Page 15 (Clause 2.17)  

---

## 1. Mandatory Geotechnical & Structural Proof-Checking Protocol

As stipulated under Clause 2.17 (Page 15) of the tender document:
> *"The indicative Architectural drawings of Type-3 quarters and extension of FLN service building are attached. These drawings are indicative only and are not to be used for execution purpose. The successful bidder to whom the work is awarded shall do the Geotechnical Investigation and submit the Structural Design & Drawings duly vetted from Government Approved Structural Engineer. Cost of all Architectural and structural drawings, design and proof checking is deemed to be included in the quoted rates and nothing extra shall be paid for this item."*

- **Geotechnical Investigation:** Rotary core drilling of boreholes up to competent rock/hard strata at each of the 7 station locations, standard penetration tests (SPT), and determination of Safe Bearing Capacity (SBC).
- **Independent Proof-Checking:** The complete structural design calculations, STAAD Pro models, and GFC working drawings must be vetted and sealed by a Government Approved Structural Engineer / premier engineering institution (e.g., MNIT Jaipur / IIT Roorkee / approved state engineering college).

## 2. Civil Materials & Construction Specifications

- **Concrete Grades:**
  - Plain Cement Concrete (PCC): M-15 / M-20 for sub-base and levelling courses.
  - Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC): Minimum **M-25 / M-30** for all foundations, plinth beams, columns, floor slabs, and lintels.
- **Steel Reinforcement:**
  - Thermo-Mechanically Treated (TMT) **Fe 500 / Fe 550D** steel bars conforming to IS 1786 with valid ISI certification.
- **Masonry & Finishes:**
  - Common burnt clay bricks / fly-ash cement blocks conforming to IS 1077 with compressive strength $\ge 7.5	ext{ N/mm}^2$.
  - Internal plaster: 12 mm thick in 1:6 cement sand mortar finished with acrylic washable distemper.
  - External plaster: 15–20 mm thick two-coat waterproof sand-faced cement plaster with weather-resistant exterior acrylic paint.
  - Flooring: Premium vitrified ceramic tiles in living/bedrooms, Kota stone in staircases/corridors, and non-skid ceramic tiles in toilets.
