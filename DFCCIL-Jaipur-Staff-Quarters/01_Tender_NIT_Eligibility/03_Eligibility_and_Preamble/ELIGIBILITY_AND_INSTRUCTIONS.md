## 1. Technical & Financial Eligibility Criteria (Part III, Para 10)

1. **Technical Capability (Similar Work Definition):**
   - The bidder must have successfully completed or substantially completed any of the following during the last 7 years ending the last day of the month previous to the one in which tenders are invited:
     - **Three similar works** each costing not less than the amount equal to **30% of advertised value** of the tender (**₹2.64 Crore** each), OR
     - **Two similar works** each costing not less than the amount equal to **40% of advertised value** of the tender (**₹3.52 Crore** each), OR
     - **One similar work** costing not less than the amount equal to **60% of advertised value** of the tender (**₹5.28 Crore**).
   - *Definition of Similar Nature of Work:* "Construction of residential building / staff quarters / service buildings or any multi-storeyed building with RCC framed structure and finishing works."

2. **Financial Standing:**
   - **Contractual Turnover:** The tenderer must have received a minimum contractual turnover of at least **150% of the advertised value** of the tender (**₹13.20 Crore**) during the last three financial years plus the current financial year up to the date of tender opening.
   - Audited balance sheets and Form 26AS certified by a Chartered Accountant must be uploaded as proof.

3. **Statutory Credentials:**
   - Mandatory registration on the Indian Railway E-Procurement System (IREPS) portal (`www.ireps.gov.in`) with a valid Class-III Digital Signature Certificate (DSC).
   - Valid EPF, ESI, and GST registration certificates.
