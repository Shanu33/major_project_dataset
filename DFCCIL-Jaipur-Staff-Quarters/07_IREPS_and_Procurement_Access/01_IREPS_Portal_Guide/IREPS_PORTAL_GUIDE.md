# IREPS Portal Submission & E-Tendering Navigation Guide

**Official Portals:**
- Indian Railway E-Procurement System (IREPS): `https://www.ireps.gov.in`
- DFCCIL Official Portal: `https://www.dfccil.com`

---

## 1. Step-by-Step Bidding Navigation

1. **Accessing IREPS Portal:**
   - Navigate to `https://www.ireps.gov.in/`.
   - Log in using an authorized Class-III Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) registered with IREPS.
   - Navigate to **"Goods & Services / Works Tenders"** -> Select Organization: **"DFCCIL"** -> Zone: **"Jaipur"**.
   - Input Tender Number: `JP-EN-Quarter-2024-11` (or earlier ref `JP-EN-Quarter-2023-18`).

2. **Online Fee & EMD Remittance:**
   - Tender Fee: **₹11,800/-** (including 18% GST) paid online via IREPS payment gateway.
   - Earnest Money Deposit (EMD): **₹5,89,800/-** remitted online prior to bid submission cutoff.

3. **Tender Package Downloads:**
   - Download the full tender document PDF (`Tender_document_12_07_M359.pdf`), Form No. 1 BOQ Excel template, and any uploaded corrigenda.
