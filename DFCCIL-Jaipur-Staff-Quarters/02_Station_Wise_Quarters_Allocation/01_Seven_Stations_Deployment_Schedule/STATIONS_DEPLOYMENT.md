# Station-Wise Deployment: Type-3 Staff Quarters Across 7 DFC Stations

**Jurisdiction:** Western Dedicated Freight Corridor (WDFC) — REJN to KSGN Section  
**Target Unit Typology:** Indian Railways / DFCCIL Standard Type-3 Staff Quarters  

---

## 1. Geographic Station Distribution

The contract covers construction of Type-3 residential accommodation across seven vital operational DFC crossing/junction stations located in Rajasthan:

| Station Code | Station Name | Operational Classification | Civil Works Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| **REJN** | **Renwal** | DFC Crossing Station | Construction of Type-3 Staff Quarters, approach pathways, water supply connection |
| **AELN** | **Asalpur Jobner** | DFC Station | Construction of Type-3 Quarters, septic tank, soak pit, boundary wall & gate |
| **DBLN** | **Dabla** | DFC Junction / Yard | Construction of Type-3 Quarters Block, OHT water supply & electrical services |
| **BAGN** | **Bagru** | DFC Industrial Station | Type-3 Quarters for Station Masters, Loco Pilots, and Track Maintainers |
| **SMPN** | **Srimadhopur** | DFC Crossing Station | Type-3 Staff Housing, external drainage, and peripheral development |
| **PMPN** | **Pachar Malikpur** | DFC Station | Type-3 Staff Quarters, dedicated water storage, and site development |
| **FLN** | **Phulera Junction** | Major DFC Operations Hub | Type-3 Staff Quarters Complex + Additional Floor over Service Building |

---

## 2. Standard Type-3 Staff Quarter Configuration

- **Plinth Area:** ~60 to 65 sq.m per unit conforming to Railway Board residential guidelines.
- **Accommodation:** Living room, two bedrooms, kitchen with cooking platform and sink, independent bathroom, toilet (water closet), front verandah, and rear service court/balcony.
- **Structural Integrity:** Conventional RCC framed structure with brick masonry infill designed for an operating life of 50–100 years.
