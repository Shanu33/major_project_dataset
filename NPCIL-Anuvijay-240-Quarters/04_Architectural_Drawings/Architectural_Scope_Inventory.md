# Architectural Drawing Inventory & Floor Scope (6 Blocks G+10)

## Block Configuration
- **Building Typology:** Multi-Storey High-Rise Residential Quarters (D-Type)
- **Number of Blocks:** 6 Blocks
- **Height / Levels:** Ground Floor + 10 Upper Floors (G+10)
- **Units per Block:** 40 Quarters (4 Units per floor × 10 upper residential floors; Ground floor accommodates stilt parking, services, and entrance lobby)
- **Total Quarters:** 240 D-Type residential units

## Drawing Sheets (Embedded in Master Tender PDF)
1. **Master Site Plan:** Overall layout of 6 blocks in Anuvijay Township, showing access roads, peripheral drainage, parking bays, and substation connections.
2. **Ground Floor Plan:** Entrance lobby, stilt parking layout, electrical room, pump room, security cabin, and staircase/lift core arrangement.
3. **Typical Floor Plan (1st to 10th Floor):** 4 units of D-Type quarters centered around a central service lobby, twin lifts, and dual fire-escape staircases.
4. **D-Type Unit Layout:** Living/dining hall, master bedroom with attached toilet, secondary bedroom, common toilet, kitchen with utility balcony, and front sit-out balcony.
5. **Elevations:** Front (North), Rear (South), Side (East & West) architectural elevations showing facade treatment, louvers, and chajjas.
6. **Cross Sections:** Longitudinal and transverse structural sections through lift core, staircases, and residential units illustrating floor-to-floor heights (3.0 m).
