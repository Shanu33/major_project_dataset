# Structural Design Basis & Conceptual System (6 Blocks G+10)

- **Foundation System:** Deep RCC isolated / combined footings resting on hard strata or raft foundation engineered for coastal soil conditions.
- **Superstructure:** Moment-resisting RCC frame system (SMRF) with reinforced concrete shear walls strategically placed at lift shafts and staircase cores to absorb lateral seismic and coastal wind forces.
- **Floor-to-Floor Height:** 3.00 meters; total building height approx. 34.50 meters above ground level.
- **Slab Design:** Two-way RCC slabs (125 mm to 150 mm thick) designed for live load of 2.0 kN/m2 (rooms) and 3.0 kN/m2 (corridors/balconies).
- **Seismic Design:** IS 1893 (Part 1): 2016 conforming to Zone II/III with Importance Factor 1.2 (essential government housing infrastructure).
