# NPCIL Anuvijay Township — Technical Specifications Summary

- **Tender Reference:** NPCIL/KK-3&4/CONST/CIVIL-INFRA/PT/2018/150 (Tender ID: 2025_NPCIL_222105_1)
- **Scope:** Construction of 240 Nos. D-Type Residential Quarters (6 Blocks of G+10 Floors) including External Services
- **Client:** Nuclear Power Corporation of India Limited (NPCIL) – Kudankulam Nuclear Power Project

## 1. Concrete & Structural Specifications
- **Design Standard:** IS 456:2000 (Plain and Reinforced Concrete - Code of Practice), IS 1893:2016 (Criteria for Earthquake Resistant Design of Structures - Seismic Zone II / III).
- **Concrete Grade:**
  - Foundations / Raft / Plinth beams: RCC Grade M30
  - Superstructure columns, shear walls, beams, and slabs: RCC Grade M25 / M30
  - Plain Cement Concrete (PCC) leveling course: M10 / M15 (1:3:6 / 1:2:4)
- **Reinforcement:** High-yield strength deformed thermo-mechanically treated bars confirming to IS 1786 (Grade Fe 500D).
- **Formwork:** Engineered modular steel / film-faced plywood shuttering designed to withstand dynamic pouring pressure; minimum stripping times strictly as per CPWD / IS 456 specifications.

## 2. Masonry & Plastering
- **External & Internal Walls:** Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) blocks conforming to IS 2185 (Part 3) with specialized thin-bed polymer adhesive mortar, or class designation 7.5 burnt clay bricks in cement mortar 1:4.
- **Plastering:**
  - Internal Plaster: 12 mm single-coat cement plaster 1:4 (1 cement : 4 fine sand), finished smooth with lime rendering.
  - External Plaster: 18 mm two-coat water-resistant cement plaster (12 mm base coat 1:5 + 6 mm top coat 1:3 with integral waterproofing compound conforming to IS 2645).

## 3. Finishes & Joinery
- **Flooring:** 600×600 mm vitrified tiles (double charged / glazed vitrified) in living, dining, and bedrooms; anti-skid ceramic tiles (300×300 mm) in kitchens, balconies, and toilets.
- **Joinery:** Factory-made pressed steel door frames with flush door shutters (35 mm thick, BWP grade conforming to IS 2202); powder-coated / anodized 3-track aluminum or UPVC sliding windows with 5 mm toughened float glass.
- **Painting:** Premium acrylic emulsion on internal walls over two coats of wall putty; 100% acrylic exterior paint with anti-fungal properties on external surfaces.

## 4. MEP Services & Nuclear Township Protocols
- **Plumbing:** CPVC pipes (SDR 11) for internal hot and cold water distribution; UPVC pipes (Type B as per IS 13592) for soil and waste drainage; RCC Hume pipes (NP3 class) for external sewer networks.
- **Electrical:** FRLS (Flame Retardant Low Smoke) multi-strand copper conductors in recessed heavy-gauge PVC conduits; modular switches; dedicated circuit breakers (MCBs/RCCBs) conforming to IS 12640.
- **Quality Assurance:** Level-1 QA manual adherence with mandatory third-party test certificates for cement, reinforcement steel, aggregates, and cube compressive strength records.
