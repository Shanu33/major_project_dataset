# Architectural Scope & Unit Plan Inventory (Type-I & Type-II)

## Typology & Block Layout
- **Building Height:** Low-rise G+1 / G+2 RCC framed structure
- **Location:** Burdwan Division Campus, Purta Bhavan, Hakimpara, Purba Burdwan
- **Total Quarters:** 16 residential units (8 Units Type-I + 8 Units Type-II)

## Unit Configurations
1. **Type-I Quarters (1BHK):**
   - Plinth Area: ~38.5 sq.m (approx. 415 sq.ft)
   - Layout: Living Room (3.2m × 3.0m), Bedroom (3.2m × 3.6m), Kitchen (2.0m × 2.4m), Bath (1.5m × 1.2m), WC (1.2m × 1.0m), Front Verandah/Balcony.
   - Target User: Junior grade staff and ministerial employees of WB PWD.
2. **Type-II Quarters (2BHK):**
   - Plinth Area: ~53.5 sq.m (approx. 575 sq.ft)
   - Layout: Living/Dining Room (3.6m × 4.2m), Master Bedroom (3.3m × 3.6m), Secondary Bedroom (3.0m × 3.3m), Kitchen (2.4m × 2.4m), Attached/Common Toilets, Front & Service Balconies.
   - Target User: Supervisory, technical, and engineering staff.
