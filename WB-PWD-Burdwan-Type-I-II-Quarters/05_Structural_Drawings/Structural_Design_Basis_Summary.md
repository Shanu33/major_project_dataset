# Structural Design Basis & Framing Scheme (G+2 Blocks)

- **Framing System:** Regular Special Moment Resisting RCC Frame (SMRF) conforming to IS 13920 ductile detailing.
- **Design Standard:** IS 456:2000 (Plain and Reinforced Concrete), IS 875 Parts 1-3 (Design Loads), IS 1893:2016 (Seismic Zone III for Purba Burdwan).
- **Substructure:** Isolated trapezoidal RCC footings with interconnecting tie/plinth beams at ground level to arrest differential settlement.
- **Column Schedule:** 250×350 mm and 250×400 mm RCC columns with 8 Nos. 16mm / 20mm Fe-500D bars.
- **Beam Schedule:** 250×350 mm floor and plinth beams with 125 mm thick two-way floor slabs.
