# MEP & Utilities Engineering Scope

- **Water Supply:** Dual water distribution connected to campus municipal feeder with underground sump and overhead RCC/PVC storage tanks (5,000 liters capacity per block).
- **Sanitary & Sewage:** Individual toilet stacks connected to external masonry inspection chambers discharging into an on-site dual-compartment RCC septic tank and dispersion soak well.
- **Power & Electrification:** 3-phase 415V power intake from WBSEDCL local feeder; individual energy sub-meters per residential quarter with miniature circuit breaker protection.
