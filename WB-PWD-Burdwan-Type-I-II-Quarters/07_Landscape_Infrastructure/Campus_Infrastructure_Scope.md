# Campus Infrastructure & External Site Development

- **Site Boundary:** 1.8 m high brick masonry boundary wall with MS grill fencing on top and two heavy MS vehicular access gates.
- **Circulation:** 3.5 m wide interlocking concrete paver block internal pathways connecting quarters to the main Purta Bhavan arterial road.
- **Surface Drainage:** Brick-lined peripheral storm water drains (300 mm width) discharging to the municipal roadside culvert.
