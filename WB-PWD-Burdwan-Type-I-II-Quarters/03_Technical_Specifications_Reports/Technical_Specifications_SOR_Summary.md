# West Bengal PWD Technical Specifications & SOR Standards

- **Authority:** Public Works Department, Government of West Bengal – Burdwan Division (Social Sector)
- **Standard Baseline:** West Bengal PWD Schedule of Rates (SOR) for Building Works & Sanitary-Plumbing Works, aligned with CPWD Specifications 2019 / IS Codes.

## 1. Substructure & Earthwork
- **Excavation:** Foundation trenches in all kinds of soil dressed and rammed. Bottom treated with anti-termite chemical emulsion where specified.
- **PCC Leveling Bed:** 1:3:6 nominal mix (1 cement : 3 sand : 6 graded stone ballast 20 mm nominal size) 75 mm to 100 mm thick.
- **Footings:** Isolated spread footings in RCC M25 grade designed for safe bearing capacity of Burdwan alluvial soil strata (~150 kN/m2 at 1.8 m depth).

## 2. Reinforced Concrete & Steel
- **Concrete Grade:** Superstructure columns, beams, lintels, chajjas, and slabs cast in design mix RCC M25. Cube compressive testing at 7 and 28 days strictly enforced.
- **Steel Reinforcement:** High-yield deformed TMT bars Fe-500D confirming to IS 1786.
- **Waterproofing:** Roof slabs waterproofed with integral liquid water-proofing compound and brick-coba / polymer-modified cementitious coating.

## 3. Masonry & Finishes
- **Brick Masonry:** Traditional 1st class burnt clay bricks of crushing strength not less than 7.5 N/mm2 laid in cement mortar 1:4.
- **Plaster:** Internal 15 mm cement plaster (1:4) finished with wall putty and two coats of acrylic emulsion. External 20 mm two-coat sand-face plaster (1:3) with weather-coat acrylic emulsion.
- **Flooring:** 600×600 mm vitrified tiles with matching skirting in living and bedroom areas. Anti-skid ceramic tiles in toilets and kitchen areas.

## 4. MEP Services
- **Piping:** Concealed CPVC pipes for water supply; UPVC pipes with rubber ring joints for soil, waste, and rainwater drainage.
- **Electrification:** FRLS multi-strand copper conductors in heavy-duty PVC conduits with modular switchboards and dedicated miniature circuit breakers (MCBs).
