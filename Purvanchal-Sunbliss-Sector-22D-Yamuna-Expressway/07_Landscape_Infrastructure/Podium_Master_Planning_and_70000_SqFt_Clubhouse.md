# Podium Master Planning & 70,000 Sq.Ft. Clubhouse
## Purvanchal Sunbliss, Sector 22D, Yamuna Expressway

---

### 1. Landscape Architecture on Elevated Podium
* **Podium Landscape Concept**:
  * An expansive zero-traffic ecological deck designed with soil-depth profiles between 600mm and 1200mm supporting lush tropical plantations, manicured lawn areas, and shade-providing flowering trees.
  * Drainage and waterproofing systems: High-density polyethylene (HDPE) dimpled drainage boards, geotextile filter fabrics, and multi-layer elastomeric waterproofing membranes protecting the subterranean parking structures.
* **Key Amenity Zones**:
  * Central water bodies with reflective cascades and aerating fountains.
  * Dedicated children's adventure play park with shock-absorbing EPDM rubber safety surfacing.
  * Reflexology walking tracks, senior citizen gazebos, and meditation groves.
  * Continuous perimeter jogging and cycling trail.

---

### 2. Standalone 70,000 Sq.Ft. Luxury Clubhouse (Block 8)
* **Aquatic Facilities**:
  * All-weather temperature-controlled indoor heated lap pool.
  * Resort-style outdoor swimming pool with dedicated kids' wading pool and sunbathing sundeck.
* **Sports & Athletics**:
  * Full-size indoor badminton courts and squash court.
  * Table tennis, billiards room, and indoor recreation games salon.
  * Multi-station cardiovascular and strength fitness center / gymnasium.
* **Social & Community Infrastructure**:
  * Multi-purpose banquet hall with pre-function foyer and catering pantry for community gatherings.
  * AV / Mini-theatre projection room.
  * Co-working business lounge and reading library.
  * Spa, steam, sauna, and wellness therapy suites.
