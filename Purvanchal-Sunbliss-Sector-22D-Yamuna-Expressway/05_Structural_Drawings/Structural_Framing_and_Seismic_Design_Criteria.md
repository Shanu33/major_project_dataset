# Structural Framing & Seismic Design Criteria
## Purvanchal Sunbliss, Sector 22D, Yamuna Expressway

---

### 1. Applicable Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) Codes
* **IS 456:2000**: Code of Practice for Plain and Reinforced Concrete.
* **IS 1893 (Part 1): 2016**: Criteria for Earthquake Resistant Design of Structures.
* **IS 13920:2016**: Ductile Design and Detailing of Reinforced Concrete Structures Subjected to Seismic Forces.
* **IS 875 (Parts 1, 2, 3 & 5): 2015**: Code of Practice for Design Loads (Dead, Imposed, Wind, and Special Combinations).
* **IS 2911 (Part 1/Sec 2): 2010**: Code of Practice for Design and Construction of Pile Foundations.

---

### 2. Geotechnical & Seismic Baseline
* **Seismic Zone**: **Zone IV** (High Seismic Hazard Zone, Peak Ground Acceleration Zone Factor Z = 0.24).
* **Importance Factor (I)**: 1.2 (Residential apartment complex with high occupant density).
* **Response Reduction Factor (R)**: 5.0 (Special RC Shear Wall System - ductile detailing).
* **Soil Foundation Stratum**: Deep alluvial deposits of the Yamuna river floodplains. Foundation adopted consists of high-capacity bored cast-in-situ concrete piles with deep pile caps / raft slab foundation designed to resist uplift and hydrostatic water pressure.

---

### 3. Structural Superstructure Engineering
* **Lateral Force Resisting System (LFRS)**: Monolithic reinforced concrete shear walls cast seamlessly with floor slabs using aluminum formwork.
* **Concrete Grades**:
  * Foundations / Piles / Raft: M35 / M40 grade with sulfate-resisting cement / low-heat slag mix.
  * Shear walls & Columns: M40 / M35 transitioning to M30 at upper levels.
  * Slabs & Beams: M30 / M25.
* **Reinforcement**: High-yield strength deformed Fe 500D / Fe 550D TMT bars conforming to IS 1786 with enhanced elongation properties for cyclic seismic dissipation.
