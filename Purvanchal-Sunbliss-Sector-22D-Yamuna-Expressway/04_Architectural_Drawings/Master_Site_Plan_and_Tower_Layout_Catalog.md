# Master Site Plan & Tower Layout Catalog
## Purvanchal Sunbliss, Sector 22D, Yamuna Expressway

---

### 1. Site Spatial Planning & Master Deck
* **Total Land Parcel**: 42,406.00 sq.m (~10.478 acres) rectangular corner plot on Sector 22D arterial road.
* **Podium Concept Master Plan**:
  * Complete vertical grade separation between vehicular movement and pedestrian living zones.
  * Ring vehicular roadway at site periphery leading directly into subterranean double-basement parking garages.
  * 100% traffic-free central landscaped upper deck (podium) providing safe, uninterrupted pedestrian access to all tower lobbies and the community center.

### 3. Circulation & Accessibility
* **Primary Entry / Exit**: 45m wide sector road gateway with multi-lane automated boom barriers, RFID vehicle scanning, and security checkposts.
* **Emergency Circulation**: 6.0m clear paved peripheral fire tender driveway accommodating 45-tonne fire hydrants with turning radii conforming to NBC 2016 Part 4.
