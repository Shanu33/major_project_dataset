# Technical Specifications & Architectural Finishes Schedule
## Purvanchal Sunbliss, Sector 22D, Yamuna Expressway (Schedule D ATS Extract)

---

### 1. Structural Framing & System Formwork
* **Structural System**: Cast-in-situ reinforced concrete shear wall framework engineered for Seismic Zone IV forces per IS 1893:2016 and IS 13920:2016.
* **Formwork System**: Precision modular aluminum formwork (MIVAN technology) enabling monolithic casting of vertical shear walls, columns, and horizontal slabs in uniform 7-to-10 day pour cycles.

### 3. Sanitary, Plumbing & Brand Schedule

* **CP Fittings**: **Jaquar** / **Roca** or equivalent approved brand.
* **Sanitaryware**: **Hindware** / **Parryware** or equivalent vitreous chinaware (white / off-white).
* **Drinking Water**: Individual under-counter Reverse Osmosis (RO) plant in each apartment unit.
* **General Water Supply**: Central automated softening plant delivering 24-hour softened domestic water.
* **Balcony Amenity**: Factory-fabricated ceiling-mounted retractable clothes drying hanger.
