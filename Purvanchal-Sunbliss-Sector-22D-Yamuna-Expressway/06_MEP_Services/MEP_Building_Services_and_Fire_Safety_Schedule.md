# MEP Building Services & Life Safety Schedule
## Purvanchal Sunbliss, Sector 22D, Yamuna Expressway

---

### 1. Electrical & Power Infrastructure
* **Grid Power Distribution**: High-tension (HT) dual underground power feed from UP Power Corporation Limited (UPPCL) with dedicated step-down dry-type transformers.
* **Emergency Power Backup**: Central Diesel Generator (DG) synchronization system providing 100% emergency service backup + dedicated 3 KVA backup per flat.
* **Internal Electrification**: Concealed FRLS (Flame Retardant Low Smoke) multi-strand copper wiring in rigid PVC conduits, modular switches (Havells / Schneider / Legrand).
* **Telecommunications**: Optical Fiber-to-the-Home (FTTH) network infrastructure throughout the complex for gigabit internet, IPTV, and intercom telephony.

---

### 2. Public Health Engineering (PHE) & Water Supply
* **Dual Water Distribution**:
  * Potable & Domestic supply: Filtered and softened water from centralized water treatment / softening plant.
  * Flushing supply: Recycled tertiary-treated water from the Sewage Treatment Plant (STP).
* **Drinking Water**: Individual under-counter Reverse Osmosis (RO) filtration systems in each apartment kitchen.
* **Storage Infrastructure**: Massive segmented Underground Reservoir (UGR) with separate compartments for domestic water, flushing water, and dedicated fire reserve.
* **Water Boosting System**: Hydro-pneumatic variable-speed pumping system maintaining balanced head across high-rise zones.

---

### 3. Environmental & Waste Treatment
* **Sewage Treatment Plant (STP)**: Moving Bed Biofilm Reactor (MBBR) or SBR biological treatment technology treating 100% domestic effluent to statutory CPCB/UPPCB discharge parameters.
* **Rainwater Harvesting**: Recharging wells strategically distributed around the perimeter with silt-trapping filtration chambers.

---

### 4. Fire Safety & Emergency Evacuation
* **Compliance**: National Building Code of India (NBC) 2016 Part 4 & UP Fire Service Directorate bylaws.
* **Systems Provided**:
  * Automatic fire sprinkler network in all basement garages, lift lobbies, and commercial areas.
  * External yard hydrants and internal wet risers equipped with 63mm first-aid hose reels.
  * Microprocessor-based addressable Fire Alarm and smoke detection system.
  * High-pressure fire pumps (Electrical main pump, Diesel backup pump, and Jockey pressurization pump).
  * Dual fire escape staircases with fire door assemblies offering 2-hour fire rating, pressurized shafts, and refuge terraces at regular statutory intervals.
