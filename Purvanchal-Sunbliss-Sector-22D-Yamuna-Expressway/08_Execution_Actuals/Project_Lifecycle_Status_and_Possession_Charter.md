### 2. Statutory Quality Assurance & Defect Liability
* **RERA Section 14(3)**: Promoter is statutorily bound to remedy any structural defect or any other defect in workmanship, quality or provision of services within 30 days of notice, without further charge, for a period of **5 years** from the date of handing over possession.
* **Supervision & Testing**: Standard quality assurance program adhering to NABL-accredited cube testing, steel tensile testing, ultrasonic pulse velocity (UPV) concrete testing, and waterproofing ponding tests.
