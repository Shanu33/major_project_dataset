# Architectural Master Planning, FAR, Ground Coverage & Spatial Density Norms (HBC 2017)

## 1. Regulatory Zoning Framework

Multi-tower residential group housing projects in Haryana are designed under the **Haryana Building Code, 2017 (HBC-2017)**, administered by DTCP Haryana:

- **Floor Area Ratio (FAR):**
  - Standard Base FAR: **1.75 to 2.25** depending on road width and sector development plan.
  - Purchasable FAR (TOD / Transit-Oriented Development): Up to **2.50 to 3.00+** along designated mass transit corridors (such as Dwarka Expressway and SPR).
- **Ground Coverage:**
  - Maximum permissible ground coverage is restricted to **30% to 35%**, ensuring that **65% to 70% of the site area remains open** for surface landscaping, vehicular circulation, and recreational amenities.
- **Setbacks & Fire Separation:**
  - Front Setback: Minimum 15 m to 18 m from the sector/master road.
  - Side and Rear Setbacks: Minimum /3$ or 12 m (whichever is greater, where $ is building height) to ensure adequate fire tender access.
  - Peripheral Fire Driveway: Unobstructed minimum 6.0 m to 9.0 m wide motorable road with minimum 45-tonne load bearing capacity.

## 2. Spatial Density & Unit Planning

- **Dwelling Unit Density:** Maximum density typically ranges from **250 to 300 persons per acre** (approximately 50 to 60 units per acre).
- **Efficiency Ratios:**
  - High-rise towers with 2-3 basements achieve **68% to 75% carpet-to-super built-up area efficiency**.
  - Common areas (lift lobbies, fire escape stairs, refuge corridors, vertical utility shafts) consume 25% to 32% of floor plate.
- **Parking Allocation (ECS):**
  - Minimum 1.5 to 2.0 Equivalent Car Spaces (ECS) per residential unit.
  - Provision of mandatory visitor parking (10% of total parking capacity) at surface level.
