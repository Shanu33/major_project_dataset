# Site Master Infrastructure, Podium Landscaping & Recreational Amenities

## 1. Site Civil Infrastructure

- **Internal Road Network:** Heavy-duty reinforced asphalt / concrete paver block roads (minimum 9 m to 12 m carriageway) designed to carry heavy construction traffic and fire tenders.
- **Stormwater Management:** Reinforced concrete box culverts along perimeter roads connected to rainwater harvesting recharge wells (minimum 1 recharge pit per 1,000 m² of site area).
- **Underground Utility Corridors:** Precast concrete trenches for electrical cables, telecom optical fibers, piped natural gas (PNG), and municipal water pipes, preventing surface disruption during maintenance.

---

## 2. Landscape Architecture & Green Buffers

- **Surface Landscaping:** Peripheral tree belts, sound buffers with native evergreen trees (Neem, Gulmohar, Amaltas, Jamun), pedestrian walking trails, and open amphitheaters.
- **Podium Greenery:** Extensive landscaped podium deck above parking basements featuring waterproofing membranes, drainage cells, root barriers, and lightweight planting soil mixes.

---

## 3. Clubhouse & Recreation Complex

- **Built-up Area:** Typically 15,000 sq.ft. to 40,000 sq.ft. depending on overall project scale.
- **Core Amenities:** Temperature-controlled indoor/outdoor swimming pools, modern fitness center, squash courts, badminton hall, banquet/party halls, business lounge, and kids crèche.
