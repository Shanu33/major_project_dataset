# Technical Specifications & Finishes Architecture for Haryana NCR Multi-Tower Residences

## 1. Civil & Structural Core Specifications

- **Structural Superstructure:** Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC) shear wall / framed structure designed in compliance with **IS 1893:2016** (Seismic Zone IV criteria) and **IS 13920:2016** (Ductile detailing of reinforced concrete structures).
- **Formwork Technology:** Monolithic aluminum formwork system (Mivan or equivalent) for high-rise towers to achieve high dimensional accuracy, seismic resistance, and rapid cycle times (7-9 days per slab).
- **Masonry & Partitions:** Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) blocks / fly ash bricks (IS 2185 Part 3) with polymer-modified adhesive mortar for non-structural interior partitions to reduce dead loads.

---

## 2. Flooring & Surface Finishes

- **Living, Dining & Foyer:** Premium double-charged vitrified tiles (size 800×800 mm or 1200×600 mm) or imported Italian marble (in luxury towers).
- **Master Bedroom:** Laminated wooden flooring (AC4 grade) with skirting or high-grade glazed vitrified tiles.
- **Kitchen:** Anti-skid vitrified/ceramic floor tiles; premium granite counter-slab with stainless steel single/double bowl sink (Nirali/Franke or equivalent); ceramic wall tiles up to 600 mm above working counter.
- **Bathrooms:** Anti-skid ceramic/vitrified floor tiles; glazed vitrified wall tiles up to false ceiling height (2.4 m); granite/composite stone counter under vanity basins.
- **Balconies & Terraces:** Matte-finish anti-skid ceramic tiles; weather-proof exterior texture paint on soffits.
- **Common Areas / Lift Lobbies:** Granite / Indian marble / designer vitrified tiles with border patterns; textured acrylic emulsion paint on walls.

---

## 3. Joinery, Glazing & Railings

- **Main Entrance Door:** 2.4 m high polished teakwood frame with veneered flush door shutter, multi-point digital mortise lock, and brass/SS architectural hardware.
- **Internal Doors:** Hardwood frame with factory-pressed laminated/painted flush door shutters.
- **Windows & External Glazing:** Heavy-duty powder-coated or anodized aluminum sections (minimum 2.0 mm thickness) or multi-chambered UPVC sliding/casement windows with toughened/double-glazed glass (IGU: 6mm Toughened + 12mm Air Gap + 6mm Toughened) for thermal and acoustic insulation.
- **Balcony Railings:** Laminated safety glass railings with SS 304 handrails or MS railings (minimum height 1.2 m from finished floor level per NBC 2016).
