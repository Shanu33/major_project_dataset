# RERA Regulatory Dataset vs Public EPC Tender Methodology Note

## 1. Platform Positioning & Classification Rationale

This project is classified as **Reference only** in the Construction Intelligence platform.

### Key Distinction:
- **Public Sector Tenders (Gold / Silver Tier):** CPWD, DFCCIL, SBI, NPCIL, and OIL tenders provide itemized civil Bills of Quantities (BOQs), item-by-item rate analysis, unredacted structural/architectural CAD drawings, and technical specifications.
- **Private Sector RERA Schemes (Reference Only Tier):** Real estate projects under HARERA disclose statutory macro-level project financials (Form A-H), certified land and construction costs, quarterly execution progress, and DTCP approved layouts. However, itemized contractor BOQ schedules and working CAD drawings remain confidential developer assets.

---

## 2. Quantitative Benchmark Utility

Despite the absence of line-item BOQs, the Haryana RERA dataset provides essential macro-benchmarks:
1. **Macro Cost Calibration:** Ratio of structural construction cost to total project capital outlay.
2. **Regional Price Calibration:** Empirical construction costs per square meter of carpet area across Gurugram corridors.
3. **Execution Milestone Tracking:** Real-world quarterly delivery timelines for high-rise residential towers in North India.
