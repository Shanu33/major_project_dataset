# Regulatory Execution Tracking, Quarterly Progress Reports (QPR) & Occupancy Protocols

## 1. Statutory Monitoring Framework (HARERA Section 11)

Under Section 11(1)(b) of the RERA Act, promoters must submit **Quarterly Progress Reports (QPR)** on the HARERA portal within 15 days of the end of each quarter:

1. **Building Completion Percentages:** Floor-by-floor tracking of foundation, excavation, RCC superstructure slabs, internal masonry, plaster, flooring, and external painting.
2. **Infrastructure Completion Percentages:** Status of internal roads, water supply piping, sewage network, storm water drains, electrification, and landscaping.
3. **Financial Disclosures:** Amounts collected from allottees, amounts deposited in the 70% Escrow Account, and amounts withdrawn.

---

## 2. Mandatory Professional Certifications

Every quarterly withdrawal and milestone update requires three statutory certificates:
- **Architect Certificate (Form RERA-1):** Verifying the physical percentage of work executed against sanctioned plans.
- **Structural Engineer Certificate (Form RERA-2):** Certifying structural safety, compliance with building codes, and foundation adequacy.
- **Chartered Accountant Certificate (Form RERA-3):** Auditing the 70% Escrow Account, validating that withdrawals strictly match construction progress and do not exceed certified expenditure.

---

## 3. Occupancy Certificate (OC) / Completion Certificate (CC) Issuance

Upon completion of construction:
1. **Application to DTCP Haryana:** Accompanied by as-built architectural plans, completion drawings, and structural stability certificates.
2. **NOC Clearance:** Final Fire NOC from Haryana Fire Services, Environmental Clearance compliance report, Lift Inspection Certificate from Haryana Electrical Inspectorate, and Public Health NOC (water supply & sewerage connection).
3. **Physical Site Inspection:** Joint inspection team verifies building height, setbacks, parking provision, and green areas against sanctioned plans.
4. **Grant of Occupancy Certificate:** Formal statutory permission for physical possession and conveyance deed execution.
