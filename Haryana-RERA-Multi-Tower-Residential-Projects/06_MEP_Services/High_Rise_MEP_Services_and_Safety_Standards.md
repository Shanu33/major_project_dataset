# MEP Building Services, Fire Life Safety & Electromechanical Infrastructure

## 1. Electrical & Power Infrastructure

- **Grid Power Connection:** Dual HT power supply fed from DHBVN (Dakshin Haryana Bijli Vitran Nigam) 33 kV or 11 kV sub-station.
- **Sub-Station Equipment:** Step-down oil-cooled or dry-type transformers (11 kV / 433 V), Vacuum Circuit Breakers (VCB), and Air Circuit Breakers (ACB).
- **Emergency Power Backup:** 100% automated standby Diesel Generator (DG) backup with Auto Mains Failure (AMF) synchronizing panels and acoustic enclosures meeting CPCB-IV norms.

---

## 2. Plumbing, Water Supply & Sanitary Services

- **Water Demand & Sizing:** Designed per NBC 2016 norms at **135 liters per capita per day (LPCD)**.
- **Dual Plumbing System:**
  - Potable Domestic Water Line: Sourced from GMDA municipal connection or filtered borewell supply.
  - Flushing Water Line: Supplied from the tertiary treated water stream of the on-site Sewage Treatment Plant (STP).
- **Decentralized Sewage Treatment Plant (STP):**
  - Moving Bed Biofilm Reactor (MBBR) or Sequential Batch Reactor (SBR) technology with tertiary filtration (sand filter + activated carbon filter + UV/chlorination).
  - Treated effluent parameters conforming to CPCB norms: $	ext{BOD} \le 10	ext{ mg/l}$, $	ext{TSS} \le 10	ext{ mg/l}$, $	ext{COD} \le 50	ext{ mg/l}$.

---

## 3. Fire Protection & Life Safety (NBC 2016 Part 4)

- **Fire Detection:** Addressable fire alarm system with optical smoke detectors in apartments, lobbies, and electrical shafts.
- **Fire Suppression:** Automatic wet sprinkler system across all residential floors, common lobbies, and multi-level basements.
- **Hydrant System:** Wet risers (internal hydrants with hose reel drums) in every staircase lobby + external yard hydrants spaced at 30 m intervals.
- **Pressurization:** Dedicated mechanical pressurization fans for fire escape staircases and lift lobbies ($\Delta P = 25	ext{ to }50	ext{ Pa}$) to prevent smoke infiltration.
