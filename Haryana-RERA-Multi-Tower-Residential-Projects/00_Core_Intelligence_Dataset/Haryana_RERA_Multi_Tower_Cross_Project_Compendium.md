# Haryana RERA Multi-Tower Residential Projects — Cross-Project Compendium & Intelligence Synthesis

## 1. Executive Summary & Regulatory Scope

The **Haryana Real Estate Regulatory Authority (HARERA)** operates two primary jurisdictions:
1. **Gurugram Authority (HRERA Gurugram):** Regulating the high-density NCR growth corridors including Golf Course Extension, Dwarka Expressway (Northern Peripheral Road - NPR), Southern Peripheral Road (SPR), New Gurgaon (Sectors 76-95), and Sohna.
2. **Panchkula Authority (HRERA Panchkula):** Regulating Panchkula, Faridabad, Karnal, Panipat, Sonipat, and other Haryana districts.

Under the **Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016** and **Haryana Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Rules, 2017**, all residential group housing schemes with land area exceeding 500 m² or containing more than 8 apartments must be registered before advertising or selling.

This compendium analyzes multi-tower high-rise residential projects (ranging from G+10 to G+25 floors) registered with HARERA, extracting certified empirical project outlays, tower configurations, spatial metrics, and regulatory milestones.

## 3. Macro Financial Ratios & Benchmark Observations

Analysis of certified expenditures across these multi-tower developments reveals consistent macro-structural cost ratios:

1. **Direct Apartment Construction Cost:**
   - Accounts for **33% to 76%** of total project outlay depending on land acquisition vintage.
   - For high-rise RCC framed / shear wall towers (G+15 to G+25), direct civil, structural, finishing, and internal MEP construction cost ranges between **₹3,200 and ₹5,500 per sq.ft. of carpet area**.
2. **Site Infrastructure & Internal Development Works (IDW):**
   - Accounts for **3% to 9%** of total expenditure (₹2.2 Cr to ₹72.0 Cr depending on site acreage and amenities).
   - Encompasses internal asphalt/paved roads, storm water drainage culverts, sewage collection networks, decentralized STPs, underground electrical distribution lines, and clubhouse structures.
3. **Land & Regulatory Carrying Costs:**
   - Land cost ranges from **15% to 45%** depending on sector premium (e.g. SPR / Golf Course Road vs New Gurgaon).
   - External Development Charges (EDC), Infrastructure Development Charges (IDC), scrutiny fees, conversion charges, and financing costs comprise **20% to 35%** of capital outlay.
