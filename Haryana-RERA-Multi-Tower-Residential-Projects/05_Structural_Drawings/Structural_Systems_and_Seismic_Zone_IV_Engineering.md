# Structural Design Paradigms & Seismic Zone IV Engineering Standards for High-Rise Towers

## 1. Seismic Zone IV Parameters (Delhi-NCR Region)

Gurugram and the surrounding Delhi-NCR region are located in **Seismic Zone IV**, characterized by high seismic vulnerability:

- **Seismic Zone Factor ($):** .24$ (as per IS 1893 Part 1: 2016).
- **Importance Factor ($):** .20$ for residential colonies with occupancy $> 200$ persons.
- **Response Reduction Factor ($):** .0$ for Special RC Moment Resisting Frames (SMRF) or ductile shear wall systems.
- **Soil Classification:** Type II (Medium Soil) / Type III (Soft Soil) typical of Indo-Gangetic alluvial deposits (sandy silt with clay pockets).
- **Wind Parameters:** Basic wind speed  = 47	ext{ m/s}$ (169.2 km/h) per IS 875 (Part 3): 2015.

---

## 2. Structural Superstructure Architecture

- **Lateral Force Resisting System (LFRS):** Reinforced concrete shear wall dual system. Core shear walls surround elevator shafts and fire escape staircases, resisting up to 80% of total base shear, while perimeter moment frames provide torsional stiffness.
- **Floor Diaphragm:** Solid RC slabs (thickness 150 mm to 200 mm) cast monolithically with beams and shear walls, functioning as rigid horizontal diaphragms to transfer seismic inertial forces.
- **Ductile Detailing:** High-yield strength deformed (TMT) rebars of Fe-500D or Fe-550D grade complying with **IS 13920:2016** (minimum elongation $> 14.5\%$, seismic detailing at plastic hinge regions).

---

## 3. Substructure & Foundation Engineering

- **Foundation Typology:** Raft foundation or Piled-Raft foundation.
  - For towers up to G+15: Solid RC raft (depth 1.5 m to 2.2 m) resting on engineered compacted soil or bored cast-in-situ piles.
  - For towers G+20 to G+30: Piled raft with bored cast-in-situ RC piles (diameter 600 mm to 1000 mm, length 18 m to 30 m) socketed into dense sand/silt layers.
- **Settlement Criteria:** Total settlement limited to $\le 25	ext{ mm}$ for raft on sand and $\le 40	ext{ mm}$ on clay; differential settlement limited to 1:500.
