# Haryana RERA Regulatory Framework, Form REP-I & Form A-H Architecture

## 1. Statutory Foundations

Under the **Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (Central Act 16 of 2016)** and the **Haryana Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Rules, 2017**, private residential group housing projects in Haryana are governed by strict statutory registration and financial discipline:

- **Section 3:** Prior registration of real estate projects with HARERA before marketing, booking, or sales.
- **Section 4:** Application for registration with comprehensive disclosure of legal title, licensed land schedule, sanctioned building plans, layout plans, development works, bank account details, and promoter credentials.
- **Section 4(2)(l)(D) (The 70% Rule):** Mandatory deposit of **70% of amounts realized from allottees** into a dedicated separate bank account (Escrow Account) in a scheduled bank to cover land cost and construction cost.
- **Withdrawal Protocols:** Funds can only be withdrawn from the separate account in proportion to the percentage of completion of the project, certified by an **Architect**, an **Engineer**, and a **Chartered Accountant in practice**.

## 2. Structure of Form REP-I (Application for Registration)

The statutory application is organized into 8 comprehensive parts (Parts A through H):

| Form Part | Section Focus | Data Extracted & Disclosed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Part A** | Promoter Profile | Company name, registration address, CIN, Directors/Partners, past 5-year track record. |
| **Part B** | Land & License Details | DTCP License number, total licensed area (acres/m²), title deed, collaboration agreements, revenue estate records. |
| **Part C** | Financial Outlay | Estimated project cost: (i) Land cost, (ii) Apartment construction cost, (iii) Site infrastructure cost, (iv) Other costs. |
| **Part D** | Execution Schedule | Quarter-by-quarter expenditure schedule across civil, structural, and infrastructure components. |
| **Part E** | Sanctioned Approvals | DTCP License, Zoning Plan, Sanctioned Building Plans, Environmental Clearance (EC), Fire NOC, Airport NOC. |
| **Part F** | Banking & Escrow | Dedicated RERA bank account number, branch, IFSC code, and authorized signatories. |
| **Part G** | Certifications | CA certificates for estimated costs, Architect certificates for layout, Structural Engineer undertakings. |
| **Part H** | Affidavits & Undertakings | Non-encumbrance declarations, timeline adherence undertakings, and legal indemnities. |

## 3. Department of Town & Country Planning (DTCP) Haryana Licensing

Before applying for HARERA registration, developers must obtain approvals from DTCP Haryana under the **Haryana Development and Regulation of Urban Areas Act, 1975 (Act No. 8 of 1975)**:
1. **Grant of License (e.g. License 54 of 2011, License 41 of 2010):** Authorizes conversion of agricultural/urban estate land into a Group Housing colony.
2. **Bilateral Agreement (LC-IV):** Developer agrees to construct internal development works and pay External Development Charges (EDC) and Infrastructure Development Charges (IDC).
3. **Bank Guarantees (BG):** Developer furnishes BGs amounting to 25% of the estimated cost of internal development works (IDW) to ensure infrastructure delivery.
