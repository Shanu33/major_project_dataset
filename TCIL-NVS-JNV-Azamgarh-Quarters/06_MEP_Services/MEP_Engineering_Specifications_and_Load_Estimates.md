# MEP Engineering Specifications & Electrical Abstract

## Electrical Installation Scope
Governed by Pages 44 to 48 of Volume 2 Financial BOQ:
- **Sub-Head I (Internal Electrification):** Point wiring in concealed PVC conduits using FRLS copper wires (1.5 sq.mm for light/fan points, 4.0 sq.mm for power points).
- **Sub-Head II (Distribution Boards & Switchgear):** MCB distribution boards with double door, IP42 protection, 30mA RCCBs for earth leakage protection.
- **Sub-Head III (Luminaires & Fixtures):** Energy-efficient LED ceiling fixtures, heavy-duty 1200 mm sweep ceiling fans, 300 mm exhaust fans.
- **Sub-Head IV (Earthing & Protection):** Chemical earthing with copper-bonded electrodes and GI strips.

## Plumbing & Sanitation Scope
- **Water Supply:** Dual pipe distribution (potable municipal/borewell water + flush recycled water). CPVC pipes SDR 11 for internal lines; UPVC class 6 for external ring main.
- **Sanitation & Drainage:**
  - SWR (Soil, Waste, Rainwater) PVC type B pipes with elastomeric rubber ring joints conforming to IS 13592.
  - RCC Septic Tank sized for 100 permanent users (Volume 2, Page 59) including brick masonry soak pit.
