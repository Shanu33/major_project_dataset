# Technical Specifications & Quality Assurance Standards

## Governing Code Hierarchy
1. **National Building Code of India (NBC 2016)**
2. **CPWD Specifications 2019 (Volumes I, II, & III)** with all up-to-date correction slips
3. **CPWD General Specifications for Electrical Works:**
   - Part I (Internal) — 2013
   - Part II (External) — 1994
   - Part IV (Substations) — 2013
4. **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) Codes:**
   - IS 456:2000 — Plain and Reinforced Concrete
   - IS 1893:2016 — Criteria for Earthquake Resistant Design of Structures (Zone III for Azamgarh)
   - IS 13920:2016 — Ductile Design and Detailing of Reinforced Concrete Structures
   - IS 875 (Parts 1 to 5) — Design Loads for Buildings

---

## Concrete Technology & Structural Execution Standards
- **Footings & Rafts:** M25 design mix concrete with sulphate resisting cement or OPC 43.
- **Columns, Beams & Slabs:** M25 grade reinforced concrete with controlled water-cement ratio (<0.45).
- **Sub-Base / Levelling Course:** Plain Cement Concrete 1:4:8 (1 cement : 4 coarse sand : 8 graded stone aggregate 40 mm nominal size).
- **Plinth Protection:** 75 mm thick PCC 1:2:4 over 75 mm bed of dry brick ballast, with 1:3 cement plaster neat punning finish.
- **TMT Reinforcement:** High-strength deformed bars Fe-500 conforming to IS 1786 from primary producers (SAIL/Tata/RINL). Lapping strictly staggered with minimum 50d lap length.

## Mandatory Site Field Laboratory Requirements
Under Clause 4.54 of the contract, the contractor must establish a functional testing laboratory within 15 days of award, containing:
1. Compression Testing Machine (2000 kN capacity, digital/dial gauge calibrated)
2. Standard set of sieves for coarse and fine aggregate analysis
3. Slump test apparatus with tamping rod
4. 70.6 mm and 150 mm cube moulds (minimum 18 sets)
5. Physical balance (accuracy 0.1 g and 1.0 g)
6. Vicat apparatus for cement setting time
7. Electric oven for moisture determination
