# Structural Design Basis & Engineering Notes

## Structural Scheme
- **Structural Typology:** Cast-in-situ Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC) Moment Resisting Space Frame.
- **Floors:**
  - Type-II Quarters: Ground + 1 Upper Floor (G+1), planned for future vertical extension capability.
  - Guest House: Single Storey (G+0) framed structure.
- **Foundation System:** Isolated RCC spread footings interconnected with continuous tie beams at plinth level.
- **Slab System:** Two-way and one-way solid RCC slabs (125 mm to 150 mm thickness).

---

## Design Loading Parameters
- **Dead Loads (IS 875 Part 1):**
  - Self-weight of RCC: 25.0 kN/m³
  - Brick Masonry: 20.0 kN/m³
  - Floor Finishes: 1.0 to 1.5 kN/m²
- **Imposed / Live Loads (IS 875 Part 2):**
  - Living / Bedrooms: 2.0 kN/m²
  - Balconies & Corridors: 3.0 kN/m²
  - Staircases: 3.0 kN/m²
  - Accessible Roof: 1.5 kN/m²
- **Seismic Design Criteria (IS 1893:2016):**
  - Seismic Zone: Zone III (Moderate seismic risk)
  - Zone Factor (Z): 0.16
  - Importance Factor (I): 1.2 (Institutional residential housing)
  - Response Reduction Factor (R): 5.0 (Special RC Moment-Resisting Frame — SMRF)
- **Wind Loading (IS 875 Part 3):**
  - Basic Wind Speed (Vb): 47 m/s (Azamgarh region)
  - Terrain Category: Category 2

---

## Concrete & Reinforcement Detailing Guidelines
- **Concrete Mix:** Minimum M25 grade for all structural elements.
- **Reinforcement:** Thermo-Mechanically Treated (TMT) Fe-500 Grade conforming to IS 1786.
- **Clear Concrete Cover:**
  - Footings: 50 mm
  - Columns: 40 mm
  - Beams: 25 mm
  - Slabs: 15 mm to 20 mm
- **Ductile Detailing:** Strict compliance with IS 13920:2016 for beam-column joint confining hoops, 135° hooks, and cross-ties.
