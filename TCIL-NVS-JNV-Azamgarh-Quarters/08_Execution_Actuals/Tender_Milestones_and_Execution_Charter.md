## Quality Assurance & Compliance Gateways
1. **Material Testing:** Every 50 tonnes of cement and steel reinforcement sampled and tested at approved NABL laboratory.
2. **Cube Test Sampling:** Minimum 6 cubes per 15 m³ of concrete poured, tested at 7 days and 28 days for characteristic strength (M25 ≥ 25 N/mm²).
3. **Liquidated Damages:** Compensation for delay levied @ 1.0% per month of delay subject to a maximum cap of 10% of total contract value.
