## 3. Overall Visual QA Summary

1. **Text Overflows**: Zero instances of text overflowing bounding boxes, slide borders, or table cells.
2. **Typography Consistency**: Unified sans-serif font family across all 12 slides with strictly calibrated scales:
   - Slide Main Titles: 21pt Bold (Navy `#0F2C59`)
   - Category Banners: 10pt Bold Uppercase (Steel `#2B6CB0`)
   - Container Titles: 13.5pt Bold (Navy `#0F2C59`)
   - Primary Bullets: 11.5pt Bold (Navy `#0F2C59`)
   - Secondary Descriptions: 10.5pt Regular (Charcoal `#334155`)
   - Table Headers: 10pt Bold (White on Navy/Red)
   - Table Cells: 9.5pt Regular (Charcoal on White/Light Slate)
3. **Color Harmony**: Restricted to an academic civil engineering palette (Navy, Steel Blue, Slate, White, and Crimson Red for blocked/firewalled scopes). Flashy neon AI gradients and ungrounded graphics were completely avoided.
4. **Speaker Notes**: 100% of slides contain comprehensive, professional speaker delivery notes explaining the slide content in civil engineering terminology.
