## Technical Summary of Visuals Built into `.pptx`

1. **Native Shape Count**: Over 35 custom geometric shapes (rounded cards, process flow banners, boundary boxes) formatted with professional civil engineering color theory.
2. **Native Tables**:
   - **Slide 10 Table**: 7 rows $\times$ 6 columns with exact Priority F sample openings and rooms (`D1 = 1.680 m²`, `W1 = 1.440 m²`, `DW1 = 4.200 m²`, `Living/Dining = 21.914 m²`, `Master Bedroom = 12.381 m²`, `Kitchen = 6.295 m²`).
   - **Slide 11 Table**: 6 rows $\times$ 4 columns detailing blocked packages (Piling, Pile Caps, Rebar BBS, Brickwork, Commercials) with specific missing inputs and unblocking actions.
3. **Typography & Hierarchy**: High-contrast, clean sans-serif typography with calibrated point sizes (28pt titles, 14pt section labels, 11-12pt bullets, 9.5-10pt table cells).
4. **Zero External Dependency**: The entire deck renders cleanly without requiring broken external image links, missing raster files, or proprietary plugins.
