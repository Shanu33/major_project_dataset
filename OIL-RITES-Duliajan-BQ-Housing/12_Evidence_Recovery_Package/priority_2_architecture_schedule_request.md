## 1. Context & Objective of Request

Following the establishment of the governed v2 engineering baseline for the typical Stilt+6 BQ residential tower, deterministic quantities for superstructure framing, masonry, architectural finishes, and execution durations remain blocked due to the absence of detailed working drawings and the contractor's approved execution programme.

This formal request solicits Good-for-Construction (GFC) architectural drawings, framing schedules, and the approved master baseline schedule to eliminate reliance on empirical parametric ratios.

---

## 2. Specific Documents & Technical Information Requested

### 2.1 Dimensioned Wall-Centerline Plans & Lintel Detailing (`REQ-V2-005`)
To replace synthetic opening deduction ratios (ASM-007: 23% external wall deduction; ASM-008: 10% internal partition deduction) with exact CAD geometry:
1. **Dimensioned Architectural Working Drawings:** 1:50 scale floor plans for Stilt, Typical Residential Floor (Floors 1 to 6), and Mumty level, showing:
   - True wall-centerline lengths for $230\text{ mm}$ external brick masonry (CM 1:6) and $115\text{ mm}$ internal brick partition walls (CM 1:4).
   - Structural column ($16\text{ columns}$) and shear wall ($33\text{ legs}$) block-out locations along wall runs to prevent double-counting of masonry concrete interfaces.
2. **Beam/Soffit & Lintel Information:**
   - Clear ceiling heights under beam soffits per room type.
   - Continuous versus cut lintel beam schedules and sunshade (chajja) projections over external openings.

### 2.2 Room-Wise Architectural Finish Schedule (`REQ-V2-005`)
To establish deterministic surface takeoffs for plaster, flooring, and paint:
1. **Tabulated Room Finish Schedule:** Flat-by-flat and room-by-room schedule detailing:
   - Living/Dining, Bedrooms, Kitchen, Toilets, Balconies, Common Corridors, and Staircases.
   - Flooring material specifications (Vitrified tiles $600 \times 600\text{ mm}$, anti-skid ceramic tiles, Kota stone treads/risers, and Vacuum Dewatered Flooring in stilt).
   - Internal wall plaster ($12\text{ mm}$ cement plaster), ceiling plaster ($15\text{ mm}$), and external plaster ($18\text{ mm}$ waterproof plaster).
2. **Elevation Envelope Schedules:** Gross exterior facade areas across North, South, East, and West elevations to verify external finish and weather-coat paint takeoffs.

### 2.3 Opening Schedule Final Confirmation (`REQ-V2-005`)
To confirm the v2 opening reconciliation:
- Formal verification of door assembly dimensions: D1 ($800 \times 2100\text{ mm}$), D2 ($1000 \times 2100\text{ mm}$), D3 ($1050 \times 2100\text{ mm}$) per Sheet AR/TD/005.
- Shop details for combination openings: DW1 ($2000 \times 2100\text{ mm}$) and DW2 ($2295 \times 2100\text{ mm}$).
- Shaft door dimensions: SD1 ($900 \times 2100\text{ mm}$), SD2 ($600 \times 2100\text{ mm}$ / $2000\text{ mm}$), SD3 ($900 \times 2100\text{ mm}$), and SD4 ($1200 \times 2100\text{ mm}$).
- Windows W1–W4 and Ventilators V1–V2 fabrication sizes and lintel/sill confirmations.

### 2.4 Member-Wise Beam & Slab Framing Schedules (`REQ-V2-004`)
Gross beam centreline and averaged slab assumptions may materially distort framing quantities and floor-cycle modelling. The magnitude of this effect has not been calculated or independently validated. The $403.5\text{ m}$ gross centreline basis is retained only as an estimated legacy calculation basis. To eliminate structural concrete joint double-counting:
1. **Beam Framing Schedule:** Bay-by-bay schedule for Plinth, Typical Floor, and Terrace beams detailing clear span lengths ($L_{\text{clear}}$), member cross-sections ($b \times D$), and beam-column joint deduplication rules.
2. **Slab Detailing:** Panel-by-panel slab schedule indicating one-way versus two-way spanning configurations, clear panel boundaries, exact slab depths ($125\text{ mm}$ vs. $150\text{ mm}$), and sunken slab depressions in toilet areas.

### 2.5 Rooftop Overhead Water Tank (OHT) Detailing (`REQ-V2-007`)
To replace code-based assumptions (ASM-010):
1. **Structural Detail Sheet:** Complete GFC detail sheet for the rooftop Overhead Water Tank showing:
   - Wall thicknesses, base slab thickness, and roof slab sections.
   - Reinforcement detailing, bar marks, and water-stop construction joint layout conforming to IS 3370 (Code of Practice for Concrete Structures for the Storage of Liquids).
2. **Plumbing Integration:** Tank capacity verification, compartment division, and pipe sleeve penetration coordinates.

### 2.6 Approved Baseline Construction Master Programme (`REQ-V2-006`)
To unblock single-tower duration modeling:
1. **Approved Critical Path Method (CPM) Programme:** Native Primavera P6 (`.xer`) or MS Project (`.mpp`) approved baseline construction programme submitted under the EPC contract and approved by the RITES Engineer-in-Charge.
2. **Single-Tower WBS & Logic:**
   - Detailed Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) specifically isolating Tower Block A (typical).
   - Predecessor and successor activity logic network from piling handover through RCC superstructure framing to architectural finishing.
   - Approved cycle times (target days per typical floor pour).
   - Resource loading curves and equipment deployment histograms (e.g., concrete pump and crane utilization).
