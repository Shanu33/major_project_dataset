## 2. Specific Commercial Documents Requested (`REQ-V2-008`)

### 2.1 Approved Itemized EPC Contract Price Breakdown
1. **Contract Price Schedule:** The contractor's approved detailed cost break-up submitted and accepted under EPC Mode-II contract conditions, specifically detailing the lump-sum allocation for:
   - **BOQ Item 1.01 (Residential Towers):** Total allocated sum for the 8 residential towers.
   - **Single-Tower Allocation:** Contractual billing base for one typical Stilt+6 residential tower (Block A).
2. **Trade-Wise Cost Distribution:** Detailed breakdown allocating capital expenditure across primary construction disciplines for the single tower:
   - Substructure Works (Piling, pile caps, plinth beams, and earthworks)
   - Superstructure RCC Works (Columns, shear walls, beams, slabs, and stairs)
   - Architectural Works (Brick masonry, plastering, flooring, doors, windows, and painting)
   - Plumbing & Sanitary Installations (Internal water supply, drainage, and rooftop OHT connections)
   - Electrical & Fire Fighting Installations (Conduiting, wiring, shafts, and wet riser systems)

### 2.2 Schedule of Milestone Payments & Billing Break-Up
1. **Approved Schedule of Payments:** The formal milestone payment schedule agreed between OIL/RITES and M/s Badri Rai & Company governing running account (RA) bill disbursements per tower (e.g., percentage payable upon completion of piling, stilt framing, typical floors 1 through 6, terrace, finishing, and testing/handover).
2. **Work-Package Unit Rates / Rate Analysis:** Approved unit rate build-ups or benchmark rates per square meter of plinth area used for evaluating scope variations or statutory price adjustments.

### 2.3 Tower-Specific Billing Records (If Available)
1. **First-Tower RA Bill Measurements:** Initial Running Account bill measurement sheets or abstract of costs reflecting actual billing certified by RITES for Tower Block A (typical).
