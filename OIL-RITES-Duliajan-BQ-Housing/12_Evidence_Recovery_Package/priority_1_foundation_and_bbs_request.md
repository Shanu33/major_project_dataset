## 1. Context & Objective of Request

The Engineering Estimation & Data Governance Review Team is preparing a governed, evidence-controlled estimation baseline for **exactly ONE typical Stilt+6 BQ residential tower** (24 dwelling units, 3,419.38 sq.m plinth area) within the OIL/RITES Workman Housing Complex at Duliajan.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Purpose of Request:**
> These records are requested solely for **engineering reconciliation and baseline data verification**. This request is **not** an audit challenge to the approved EPC contract, the tender award, or contractor billings. Its sole objective is to replace preliminary parametric assumptions with approved Good-for-Construction (GFC) engineering records.
> 
> **Explicit Standard:**
> Please provide official, approved engineering documents (drawings, tabulated schedules, fabrication lists, and inspection test records). **No unsupported material quantities, synthetic summaries, or rough cost figures should be supplied as a substitute.**

---

## 2. Verified Foundation Baseline & Governing Sources

A rigorous cross-verification of project drawings has established the following baseline parameters:
1. **Governing Pile Count & Diameter:** **207 bored cast-in-situ RCC piles** of **600 mm diameter** are directly confirmed and enumerated on **Sheet STR/TD/100** (Foundation Layout Plan & General Notes).
2. **Pile Cap Schedule Discrepancy:** On **Sheet STR/TD/101** (Foundation General Arrangement), physical delineation shows **54 pile cap entities**. Conversely, preliminary design summaries list **84 pile caps**, leaving **91 piles** under continuous strip caps unallocated and causing an irreconcilable variance in substructure concrete volume ($185.00\text{ m}^3$ vs. $208.38\text{ m}^3$).

To eliminate these substructure blockers and establish deterministic quantities, the specific technical documents detailed below are required.

## 3. Specific Documents & Technical Data Requested

### 3.1 Bored Piling Schedule & Founding Records (`REQ-V2-001`)
For the 207 bored cast-in-situ RCC piles ($600\text{ mm}$ dia) supporting the typical tower:
1. **Approved Pile Schedule / Borehole Logs:** The tabulated schedule specifying individual pile marks (P-1 to P-207), grid intersections, cut-off levels (COL), founding strata reduced levels (RL), and approved socketing lengths.
2. **Termination Depths:** As-designed / as-drilled termination depth records for all 207 piles (to replace the midpoint assumption of $18.0\text{ m}$ derived from the Design Basis Report range of $15\text{ m}$ to $20\text{ m}$).
3. **Reinforcement Detailing:** Pile cage fabrication drawing and bar schedule detailing longitudinal reinforcement (number and diameter of main bars, lap locations) and helical spiral/stirrup spacing per IS 2911.

### 3.2 Foundation Pile-Cap General Arrangement & Allocation Map (`REQ-V2-002`)
To resolve the contradiction on Sheet STR/TD/101:
1. **Approved Pile-Cap GA Drawing:** The signed Good-for-Construction (GFC) foundation layout indicating unambiguous boundaries for every isolated, combined, and continuous strip cap.
2. **Tabulated Pile-Cap Schedule:** Complete schedule specifying:
   - Cap identification marks (PC-1 to PC-n, and strip caps PC-W)
   - Plan dimensions: Length ($L$) and Width ($W$)
   - Structural depth / thickness ($D$) for each cap type (reconciling the $750\text{ mm}$ marked depth with the preliminary $1000\text{ mm}$ assumption)
   - Concrete grade (confirming M30)
3. **Complete 207-Pile Allocation Map:** Explicit mapping detailing exactly which piles are grouped under which pile caps, with specific allocation for the 91 piles supporting shear wall and perimeter strip caps.

### 3.3 Structural Bar Bending Schedules (BBS) & Shop Drawings (`REQ-V2-003`)
In accordance with IS 2502 and IS 13920:2016 (Ductile Design and Detailing of Reinforced Concrete Structures subject to Seismic Forces - Zone V):
1. **Vertical Elements BBS:** Contractor shop BBS and cutting lists for:
   - 16 Columns (4 C1: $1200 \times 350\text{ mm}$, 8 C2: $1200 \times 300\text{ mm}$, 4 C3: $1200 \times 300\text{ mm}$)
   - 33 Ductile Shear Wall legs (SW1 through SW10)
   - Showing bar marks, bar diameters (Fe 500D / Fe 550D), cut lengths, lap splice locations, and seismic confinement hoop spacing.
2. **Horizontal Framing BBS:** Approved BBS and shop drawings for:
   - Plinth Beams (PB-1 to PB-34)
   - Typical Floor Beams (B-1 to B-34 across Floors 1 to 6)
   - Terrace Beams (TB-1 to TB-34)
   - Floor and Roof Slabs (S1: $125\text{ mm}$ and S2: $150\text{ mm}$ panels)
3. **Ancillary Structural BBS:**
   - Doglegged Staircase flights (waist slab $150\text{ mm}$, landing beams, rebar schedules)
   - Rooftop Overhead Water Tank (OHT) and Mumty structural framing rebar details.
