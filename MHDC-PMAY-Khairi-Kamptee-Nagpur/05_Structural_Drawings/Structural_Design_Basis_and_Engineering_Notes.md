# Structural Design Basis & Engineering Notes

## 1. Structural Scheme
- **Building Height:** G+14 Floors (~45.0 m above ground level).
- **Structural System:** Reinforced Concrete Shear Wall - Frame Interaction System (Dual System) or Special Moment Resisting Frame (SMRF) with ductile detailing per IS 13920:2016.
- **Foundation System:** Isolated / Combined RCC spread footings or Raft Foundation founded on competent hard strata (basalt rock / dense murrum).
- **Expansion Joints:** Provisioned as per IS 3414 to limit continuous structural blocks within 45 meters length.

---

## 2. Design Loading Parameters
- **Dead Loads (IS 875 Part 1):**
  - Reinforced Concrete: 25.0 kN/m3
  - AAC Block Masonry: 8.0 kN/m3
  - Floor Finishes & Screed: 1.5 kN/m2
- **Imposed / Live Loads (IS 875 Part 2):**
  - Residential Units (Living/Bedrooms): 2.0 kN/m2
  - Corridors, Lobbies & Staircases: 3.0 kN/m2
  - Refuge Areas: 4.0 kN/m2
  - Roof Terrace (Accessible): 1.5 kN/m2
- **Seismic Design Parameters (IS 1893:2016):**
  - Seismic Zone: Zone II (Nagpur Region)
  - Zone Factor (Z): 0.10
  - Importance Factor (I): 1.2 (Large-scale mass housing)
  - Response Reduction Factor (R): 5.0 (Ductile SMRF / Shear Wall system)
- **Wind Design Parameters (IS 875 Part 3):**
  - Basic Wind Speed (Vb): 44 m/s
  - Terrain Category: Category 2

---

## 3. Contractor's Engineering Responsibilities
Under Chapter 4:
- The contractor must carry out confirmatory soil investigation bores (minimum 1 bore per building footprint down to hard rock).
- Structural designs and detailed reinforcement drawings must be vetted and certified by a structural proof consultant from an approved Government Engineering College (VNIT Nagpur, COEP Pune, or VJTI Mumbai).
