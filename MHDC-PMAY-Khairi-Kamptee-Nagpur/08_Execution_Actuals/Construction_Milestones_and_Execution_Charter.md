# Construction Milestones & Execution Charter

## 1. Contractual Phasing Matrix (24 Months Completion)
Governed by Clause 1.1 (IV) and Clause 12.12 of Volume I:

```text
[Month 1]      Site possession, mobilization, confirmatory geotechnical bores & layout demarcation
[Month 2-4]    Excavation, foundation footings, raft casting, columns up to stilt level
[Month 5-12]   Superstructure RCC frame casting (Floors 1 to 14, targeting 2 slabs/month per tower)
[Month 9-16]   AAC block masonry, external and internal cement plastering
[Month 13-19]  Flooring, ceramic wall tiles, door/window frames, plumbing and electrical rough-ins
[Month 17-21]  Elevator installation, fire fighting wet risers, electrical sub-station, STP & roads
[Month 21-23]  Internal and external painting, architectural finishes, landscaping, fixture fit-outs
[Month 24]     Integrated testing, CFO NOC, MSEDCL permanent charging, OC & handing over
```

---

## 2. Quality Audit & Compliance Gateways
1. **Third-Party Quality Assurance (TPQA):** Mandatory monitoring by an independent government agency / engineering college appointed by MHDC.
2. **Liquidated Damages for Delay (Clause 12.12 A):** Compensation for delay levied @ 1.0% per month of delay on the unexecuted contract value, capped at a maximum of 10% of total contract value.
3. **Incentive Clause (Clause 12.12 B):** Early completion bonus @ 0.5% per completed month ahead of schedule (max 2%).
4. **Defect Liability Period (DLP):** Mandatory 60 Months (5 Years) warranty period backed by security deposit retention.
