# MEP Engineering Specifications & Services Schedule

## 1. Electrical Engineering Scope (Chapter 5 & Chapter 6 Part III-IV)
- **Power Incomer:** 11 kV HT power sub-station with dual step-down dry-type / oil-cooled transformers (MSEDCL standards).
- **Backup Generation:** Automatic Mains Failure (AMF) Diesel Generator sets for emergency backup of lifts, firefighting pumps, common lighting, and water supply pumps.
- **Internal Wiring:** Concealed heavy-duty PVC conduits with FRLS multi-strand copper conductors.
- **Metering:** Dedicated digital energy meters located in ground-floor central metering rooms with modular distribution boards in each flat.

---

## 2. Plumbing, Sanitation & Water Supply (Chapter 6 Part II)
- **Water Supply Network:** Dual plumbing system (treated recycled water for toilet flushing + potable municipal/borewell water for domestic use).
- **Pumping System:** Hydro-pneumatic pumping system with variable frequency drive (VFD) pumpsets for constant pressure distribution across all 14 upper floors.
- **Drainage & Venting:** SWR PVC Class B pipes with acoustic insulation in vertical shafts and elastomeric ring joints.
- **Solar Water Heating:** Central rooftop solar hot water heating system integrated with individual bathroom inlets per PMAY green building norms.

---

## 3. Fire Fighting & Life Safety (Chapter 6 Part V)
Compliant with NBC 2016 Part 4 and Maharashtra Fire Prevention and Life Safety Measures Act:
- Dedicated underground static water storage tank (200,000 liters) + Overhead terrace fire tank (20,000 liters per tower).
- Dual electric fire booster pumps + standby diesel engine-driven fire pump + jockey pump.
- 150 mm dia MS wet riser system with landing valves, hose reels, and fire brigade inlet connections on each floor.
- Automatic fire sprinkler network in stilt parking and basement areas.
- Automatic addressable fire alarm system with smoke and heat detectors in electrical shafts and common lobbies.

---

## 4. Vertical Transportation (Elevators)
- Each G+14 building equipped with minimum 2 high-speed passenger elevators (13-passenger capacity, 1.5 m/s speed) including 1 stretcher-compatible elevator with Automatic Rescue Device (ARD).
