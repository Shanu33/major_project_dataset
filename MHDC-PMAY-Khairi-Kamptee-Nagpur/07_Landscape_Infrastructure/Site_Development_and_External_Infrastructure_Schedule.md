# Site Development & External Infrastructure Schedule

## 1. Campus Road & Pavement Network
- **Main Spine Road:** 12.00 m to 15.00 m wide asphalt / rigid concrete carriageway designed for heavy fire tender axle loads.
- **Peripheral Internal Roads:** 7.50 m to 9.00 m wide concrete access lanes with mountable kerbs.
- **Pedestrian Walkways:** Interlocking concrete paver blocks (60 mm / 80 mm thick) over compacted sand bedding.

---

## 2. Stormwater Drainage & Rainwater Harvesting
- **Peripheral Storm Drains:** RCC box drains with heavy-duty cast-iron perforated grating along road margins.
- **Rainwater Harvesting:** Recharge shafts and percolation pits with gravel-sand filtration media distributed across the 46,800 m2 campus to recharge the regional groundwater table.

---

## 3. Sewage Treatment & Environmental Systems
- **Sewage Treatment Plant (STP):** Decentralized Moving Bed Biofilm Reactor (MBBR) or Sequential Batch Reactor (SBR) technology STP designed for full peak sewage discharge.
- **Recycled Water Utilization:** Tertiary treated effluent reused for arboriculture landscaping and toilet flushing.
- **Solid Waste Management:** Segregated waste collection chambers (organic and recyclable) and organic waste composters (OWC).

---

## 4. Compound Wall & Security
- **Perimeter Security:** 1.80 m high brick masonry boundary wall with 0.60 m Y-angle barbed wire / concertina coil fencing along the complete site boundary.
- **Entrance Gates:** Heavy-duty decorative structural steel entrance gates with security cabins and boom barriers.
