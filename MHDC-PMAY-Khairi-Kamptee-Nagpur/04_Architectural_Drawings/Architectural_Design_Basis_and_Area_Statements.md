# Architectural Design Basis & Area Statements

## 1. Master Planning & Campus Typology
- **Site Layout:** Khasra No. 51 & 52, Khairi, Taluka Kamptee, Nagpur. Total Plot Area: 46,800 m2.
- **Master Plan Configuration:** 10 Residential High-Rise Towers (G+14 Floors) + 1 Commercial Building (G+2 Floors).
- **Package-1 Scope:** 6 High-Rise Towers dedicated to **1,444 LIG Tenements** with full RERA compliance.
