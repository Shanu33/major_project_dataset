## 2. Minimum Eligibility Criteria (Clause 1.2)
1. **Empanelment Status:** Open to MHDC empanelled contractors in the corresponding financial category and open eligible national contractors meeting prequalification requirements.
2. **Technical Experience:** Successfully completed during the last 5 financial years:
   - Three similar housing projects each costing not less than 40% of estimated cost (Rs. 81.87 Cr each), OR
   - Two similar housing projects each costing not less than 50% of estimated cost (Rs. 102.34 Cr each), OR
   - One similar housing project costing not less than 80% of estimated cost (Rs. 163.74 Cr).
   *Definition of Similar Work:* Construction of multi-storied residential / commercial building projects of minimum G+7 floors.
3. **Financial Turnover:** Average annual financial turnover during the immediate last 3 financial years must be at least 75% of the estimated cost of work (Rs. 153.51 Crores).
4. **Solvency Certificate:** Minimum solvency of 20% of estimated cost from a Nationalized or Scheduled Commercial Bank.
