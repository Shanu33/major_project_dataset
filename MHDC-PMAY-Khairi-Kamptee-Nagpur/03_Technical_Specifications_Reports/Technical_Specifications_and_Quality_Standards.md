# Technical Specifications & Quality Assurance Standards

## 1. Governing Code Hierarchy
- **National Building Code of India (NBC 2016)**
- **Maharashtra Public Works Department (PWD) Standard Specifications**
- **Indian Standard Codes (BIS):**
  - IS 456:2000 — Plain and Reinforced Concrete
  - IS 1893:2016 — Earthquake Resistant Design of Structures (Nagpur Zone II)
  - IS 13920:2016 — Ductile Design and Detailing of Reinforced Concrete Structures
  - IS 875 (Parts 1 to 5) — Design Loads for Buildings
  - IS 16700:2017 — Criteria for Design of High-Rise Concrete Buildings

---

## 2. Concrete Technology & Construction Methodology
- **Concrete Grade:**
  - Foundations & Footings: M30 Design Mix Concrete.
  - Columns & Shear Walls: M35 / M30 up to 7th floor; M25 for upper floors.
  - Slabs & Beams: M25 Design Mix Concrete.
- **Alternative Building Technology (Aluminium Formwork / Monolithic Construction):**
  - Under Chapter 6 Part B, the contractor is permitted and encouraged to use aluminium formwork for casting RCC walls and slabs monolithically in a single pour.
  - Formwork cycle time: 7 to 10 days per floor slab cycle.
- **Waterproofing Specifications (Clause 3.4 & 12.62):**
  - Basement & Stilt: Integral waterproofing admixture with crystalline slurry treatment.
  - Wet Areas (Toilets/Baths): Two coats of elastomeric polymer modified cementitious coating with protective screed.
  - Terrace Roof: Brick bat coba waterproofing with APP modified bituminous membrane or polyurethane liquid coating (10-year durability guarantee).

---

## 3. Mandatory Site Laboratory & Testing Rigor
Contractor must establish a fully equipped NABL-calibrated on-site quality control lab:
1. Digital Compression Testing Machine (2000 kN capacity)
2. Cube moulds (minimum 36 sets of 150 mm size)
3. Sieve shaker & standard sieves for aggregate gradation
4. Rebound hammer & Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV) non-destructive test equipment
5. Core cutting machine for hardened concrete sampling
