## 4. Engineering & Infrastructure Scope
1. **Civil & Structural:** Cast-in-situ RCC moment-resisting framed structures (M25/M30 concrete, Fe-500D TMT bars) or approved Alternative Building Technologies (Monolithic concrete construction using aluminium formwork system).
2. **Internal Plumbing & Sanitation:** Concealed CPVC water distribution lines, SWR drainage stacks, vitrified tiled wet areas with water-efficient CP fittings.
3. **Electrification:** Concealed FRLS copper wiring, modular switches, independent digital metering, transformer substation, and full DG backup for common services.
4. **Fire Fighting Systems:** Wet riser systems, automatic sprinkler networks in basement/stilt, external yard hydrants, fire alarm panels, and high-capacity fire pumps.
5. **Campus Infrastructure:** Internal asphalt/concrete roads, surface stormwater drainage, Sewage Treatment Plant (STP), Rainwater Harvesting pits, perimeter compound wall with main entrance gates, and solar hot water heating.
